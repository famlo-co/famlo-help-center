Not every family can commit to a whole term. Some can come on Tuesdays but not
Thursdays. Some want to try a class twice before they sign up. Some would rather
spread $380 over three months than pay it in September.

A class on Famlo can now offer all of that at once. You set the ways families
can join, and each family picks the one that fits.

## More than one way into the same class

In the class editor, **Price & spots** starts with the whole-term price. Under
it, **+ Add another way to join** offers up to six more, depending on how the
class meets:

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/announcements/ways-to-join-menu.webp" alt="The Add a way to join list in the class editor: Single dates, Pack of visits, Payment plan, Monthly, Members price and Free (RSVP)" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/watercolor-studio-cape-coral-fl-cf0wuo2d4q/edit" width={606} height={573} />

- **Single dates**: families pick the meetings they come to. It is also how a
  family who finds you mid-term signs up for what is left. If you would rather
  keep room for whole-term families, choose **Protect the term** and hold some
  spots on every date.
- **Pack of visits**: a family prepays a set number of visits and uses them any
  week until the class ends. One pack covers any of the family's children, and a
  visit canceled in time goes back into the pack.
- **Payment plan**: the term price split into 2 to 6 monthly payments, by card.
  The first is taken when the family signs up, and every payment finishes before
  the last class.
- **Monthly**: the class billed each month until it ends. A family who leaves
  stops paying.
- **Members price**: a lower price only your members see. Families who aren't
  members never see it.
- **Free (RSVP)**: no payment, but the spot still counts toward your limit and
  your attendance.

Every way to join shares the same spots, so whole-term children and drop-ins
are added together. Famlo stops taking bookings at the number you set.

Two more pricing options sit alongside these. **Family rates** take something
off for a second or third child in the same order, applied automatically, so
families never have to ask. And a price can be set **Per hour**, so families
choose how many hours they want at checkout.

## Sections: one class, several groups

A class that runs for two age groups, on two days, or in two places doesn't
have to be listed twice. Open **When** in the editor and choose
**Add a section**. Each section can have its own ages, teacher, days, place,
spots and price. Extras, refund terms and questions stay with the class, so
you set them once.

Families see one listing. At checkout each child is matched to the section that
fits their age, and siblings can sit in different sections in the same order.

## Programs: families choose their days

Some programs are not a term at all. Families come two days a week, or three,
and stay until they give notice. A **Program** is built for that.

You set a month's tuition for each number of days, the days you offer, an
optional registration fee, and how much notice a family gives to leave. Each
day has its own spots and roster.

Families tick the days each child will come and enroll with a saved card.
They are billed on the 1st of each month. From **Account settings** ›
**Memberships** they can **Change days** or **Give notice**, and the page shows
exactly when the last day and the last payment fall.

## Plan the term in one place

The class editor got easier to start, too. **New class** asks how you want to
begin: **Copy a class you've run**, which moves the dates to next term and
brings the price, photo, description and rules with it, or one of five shapes:
**Weekly class**, **Camp**, **One day**, **Drop-in program** or **Program**.

Each class can carry a week-by-week plan of what you will do and what to bring.
Turn on **Show the outline to families** if you want them to see it, and use
**Copy from last term** when the class has run before. If you move a date, its
plan moves with it.

When a class already has bookings, saving opens **Review changes** first. It
lists what is changing, such as a date, the time, the place or the teacher, and
what families will hear about it.
For a moved date, families who can't come choose a credit or a refund for that
day. Add a note if you like, then press **Save and tell N families**, and one
message goes to everyone booked. A new price only applies to new families:
anyone already booked keeps what they paid.

## For families: one checkout for every child

You can register several children in one checkout. Add each child as an attendee with **+ Add another attendee**.
Each child gets their own section, their own way to join and their own
questions. Then, under **Who pays for each spot?**, choose who pays for each
child: you, by card today, or your scholarship program if the organization
accepts it.

If the class offers it, you can pay the term in monthly payments, buy a single
date, or use a visit from a pack you already hold. Packs you have bought appear
under **Account settings › Packs**, with the visits left and what happened to
the rest.
