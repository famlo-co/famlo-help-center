Running a co-op is mostly small jobs at the wrong moment. A parent at the door
asks who is teaching Room 2. A dues payment fails on a Tuesday. A new teacher
starts next week and has no Famlo account yet.

The console is built for that. It opens on what is happening today, keeps
everything else in a short list of places, and lets you share the work without
handing everyone the keys.

## A short list of places

The left side of your console is a list of places, each named for a part of
your week: **Today**, **Schedule**, **Classes**, **Services**, **People**,
**Messages**, **Store**, **Money**, **Website** and **Testimonials**, with
**Local insights** and **School settings** at the foot.

Each place shows only if you can use it. A teacher sees **Today** and the
messages about their own classes, not the money. A bookkeeper sees **Money**,
not the class list.
Nobody has to click into a page only to be told they are not allowed in.

A search field sits at the top of the console. It finds children,
families, classes and people in your organization, and **⌘K** on a Mac
(**Ctrl+K** elsewhere) jumps straight to it. On a phone, a tab bar along the
bottom holds **Today**, **Schedule**, **Classes** and **People**, with
everything else under **More**.

## Today: now, next, and what needs you

**Today** is where the console opens. For the people running the school, it
shows the class happening **Now**, the one coming up **Next**, and a **Needs
you** list: failed dues payments, classes with no teacher, invitations nobody
has accepted. Each row has one button that does the job, such as **Review**,
**Assign** or **Resend**. Below that, the whole day is laid out on one
timeline, by place.

A new organization also gets a **Get ready** checklist on Today, with steps
such as adding your first class, setting this semester's dates and bringing
your families in. Only owners and co-leaders see it, and **Skip setup** hides
it if you would rather find your own way.

## People, in one place

**People** holds everyone connected to your organization, in six tabs:
**Families**, **Children**, **Staff & teachers**, **Join requests**,
**Invitations** and **Mailing list**.

Adding a teacher does not have to wait for an email address. **Add a teacher or
staff member** needs only a name, so you can put Maya on the schedule today.
When she is ready, connect her account: send an invitation to her email, or
find her if she already uses Famlo. She confirms, and the profile becomes hers.

## Decide what each person can do

Access is set area by area: classes, attendance, families and children,
messages, money, the website, staff, and school settings. Start from a preset
and adjust any line you like:

| Preset | In short |
|---|---|
| **Co-leader** | Runs the school with you: classes, families, messages, the website and settings. Not money. |
| **Class organizer** | Every class and every register, and the families in them. |
| **Teacher** | Their own classes: the register, the children, and messages with those families. |
| **Front desk** | Checks every child in and out, and can look families up. |
| **Bookkeeper** | Money: dues, payments, refunds and recording cash. |
| **Profile only** | On your Staff page, with no access to anything. |

You can also make someone an **Owner**, and there can be more than one, so the
school does not depend on a single login. Payouts and closing the listing stay
with the person who created or claimed it.

## One inbox for the whole team

**Messages** is a shared inbox for every conversation families start with your
organization. Filter it by **All**, **Waiting** (families who are waiting on an
answer) or **By class**, and whoever on your team can help picks it up. A
teacher set to see only their classes sees only those conversations.

When something is for everyone, **Announce** opens **Announce to everyone**.
Send it to **Members of this organization**, **Everyone registered for any
activity**, or **Both**, and each person gets it once.

## Your data, as a spreadsheet

When you need a list on paper or in another tool, export it as a CSV: your
families from **People**, the money ledger from **Money**, registrations from
**Classes**, and a class's roster and attendance from the class itself. Your
mailing list exports too.

## Getting started

Nothing to switch on. Open your organization from the avatar menu with
**Manage organizations** and you land on **Today**.

A good first step is your team. Add the people who teach or help, choose what
each of them can do, and decide who answers messages.
