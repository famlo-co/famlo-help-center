# Homeschool Records Tool

The Homeschool Records Tool is a free page for each state at
famlo.co/tools/homeschool-records. It shows what your state asks you to keep
and gives you a log of courses and hours you can fill in and print. You don't
need an account to use it. When you want to keep the log, save it to a free
Famlo account and it moves into your child's school record.

## Open your state's page

1. Go to famlo.co/tools/homeschool-records. From the famlo.co header, open **For families** and select **Homeschool records**.
2. Under **Your state**, choose your state. You can also pick it on the map or from the list of every state below it.
3. Select **Open [state]'s records**, for example **Open Georgia's records**.

The same page is the **Records** tab of the State Homeschool Guides. To switch
to another state from a state's page, select **Change state**, or use
**Nearby states** in the side column.

## What your state asks you to keep

The top of each state's page is a card headed "[State] requires". It covers:

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Days or hours of instruction** | The days or hours the state sets, such as "180 days/year, 4.5 hours/day". If Famlo hasn't confirmed this for your state yet, it says "Not established here yet" and links to your state's guide, which has the full rules. |
| **Compulsory school age** | The ages when school is required. |
| **Notice of intent** | Whether you need to notify the state or district, and when. |
| **Testing or evaluation** | Whether your child needs testing or an evaluation, and how often. |
| **Teacher qualifications** | Shown only for states that set one. |

Under the card are the law the summary comes from, the date it was last
reviewed, and a link to the full requirements. It's a summary, not legal
advice. Rules change, so check with your school district before you file
anything.

The side column links to the other free tools for your state:
**Homeschooling in [state]**, **Funding and ESAs in [state]** and the
**Notice of intent letter** where your state has one.

## Keep a log in your browser

**Your log** sits under the requirements card. It's **Saved in this browser
only**: nothing goes to Famlo until you choose to save it.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/tools/homeschool-records-log.webp" alt="Georgia's records page: the requirements card, Your log with Student name, the entry row and Add highlighted, three entries, Preview this log, the watermarked log document, and the Save this to a free Famlo account box with Your email and Email me a code highlighted" url="famlo.co/tools/homeschool-records/georgia" width={1280} height={1436} />

1. Under **Student name** (1), type your child's name. It goes at the top of the printed log.
2. Type a **Course or activity**, for example a class, a curriculum you use at home, books or sport.
3. Optionally, type a **Provider, or at home** and the **Hours**. Half hours are fine.
4. Select **Add** (2).

Repeat for each course or activity. Each entry appears in the list with its
hours, and **Total hours** and **Entries** update as you go. To take an entry
out, select **Remove** next to it.

Keep these in mind:

- Each state's page keeps its own log. Your log is on the state's page where you made it.
- The log stays in the browser and on the device you used. It isn't on your other devices.
- Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. In a private window it lasts only for that visit.

To keep the log safe for longer, save it to a free account (next section).

## Print or preview the log

Select **Preview this log** (3) to open your browser's print window. From
there you can print the log or save it as a PDF.

The printed log is headed with your child's name and "Log of educational
activities" with your state's name. It lists each activity, its provider or
materials and its hours, with a total and a line for the supervising parent
to sign and date.

Without an account, the log carries a **PREVIEW** watermark, on screen and on
paper. Save it to a free Famlo account to take the watermark off: the
documents built from your child's school record print without it.

## Save your log to a free Famlo account

The **Save this to a free Famlo account** box in the side column moves every
entry into your child's school record. On a phone it's further down the page;
**Save my log**, next to the watermark notice, takes you to it. The account
is free.

1. Under **Your email** (4), type your email address. If you already have a Famlo account, use its email and the log is added to that account.
2. Select **Email me a code** (5).
3. On the **Confirm it's you** screen, type the code from the email and select **Continue**. If the email doesn't arrive, select **Send a new code**.
4. If the email is new to Famlo, finish creating your account: add your name, choose a password and agree to continue. See [Signing in and creating your account](/docs/account/signing-in) for more about codes and passwords.

If you're already signed in to Famlo, selecting **Email me a code** skips the
code and takes you straight to the next step.

Famlo holds your log for 2 days while you finish. Your browser keeps its own
copy the whole time, so if the 2 days run out, open your state's page and
save it again.

## Add your log to a school record

Next you see **Add your log to a school record**, with the number of entries,
the total hours and the school year they go into.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/tools/homeschool-records-import.webp" alt="The Add your log to a school record page for 3 entries and 38.5 hours, with Max selected under Whose record is it?, the Mathematics subject for Math Circle, and Save to the school record highlighted" url="famlo.co/family/import" width={1280} height={1000} />

1. Under **Whose record is it?**, choose your child (1). If the name you typed in the tool matches one of your children, that child is already selected.
2. For a child who isn't in your family yet, choose **Someone new** (or **Your child** if you haven't added any children). Check the **First name** and pick a **Grade**, or leave it on **Choose later**. This adds the child to your family.
3. Under **What was each one?**, check the subject for each entry (2). Famlo makes a first guess. An entry with no guess shows **Pick a subject** with an amber border.
4. Select **Save to the school record** (3).

Famlo opens your child's school record with a message such as "Your log is
saved: 3 entries moved into Max's record." The entries go into the current
school year as your own rows, under **Added by you**, and you can edit them
there like any other row. From now on, classes you book on Famlo add their
hours on their own. See [Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record).

The subjects are English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Foreign
Language, Fine Arts, Physical Education, Health, Technology and Electives. A
subject is what a transcript groups hours under. If you leave an entry on
**Pick a subject**, you can set it later in the record.

## Limits

- A log can save up to 500 entries at a time.
- Each course or activity, and each provider, can be up to 200 characters. Anything longer is cut short.
- One log goes to one child. Each state's page holds one log, so after you save one child's log, remove its entries before you start the next child's.

## Troubleshooting

**"Your log wasn't saved yet"**
This page explains what went wrong. Select **Back to your log**: your log is
still in your browser.

- "That page was open a while. Go back, reload it, and save again": reload your state's page, then save again.
- "There's nothing in your log to save yet. Add an entry first.": add at least one entry, then save.
- "Enter your email address and we'll send you a code.": type your email, then select **Email me a code** again.
- "Enter a valid email address.", or a request to check a Gmail address: correct the address and try again.

**"There's no saved log waiting to be added."**
Famlo shows this on your **Family** page when it has no log waiting for you.
The log was already saved, the 2 days ran out, or you signed in on a different
browser from the one where you saved. Open your state's page in the browser
where you made the log and save it again.

**My log is gone from the page**
Make sure you're on the same state's page, in the same browser and on the same
device where you made it. If your browser's site data was cleared, or you used
a private window, the log can't be brought back.

**Some entries show up twice in the school record**
Saving doesn't clear the log in your browser. If you save the same log again,
its entries are added again. Remove the extra rows in the school record, and
use **Remove** in the tool for entries you've already saved.

**Saving shows a blank page after several tries**
Saving pauses for a while after several attempts in a few minutes. Go back,
wait about ten minutes, then try again. Your log is still in your browser.

**The watermark is still on my printout**
The tool's own printout always carries the **PREVIEW** watermark. Once your
log is saved, print from your child's school record instead. See
[Transcripts, diplomas and other documents](/docs/families/school-records/transcripts-and-documents).
