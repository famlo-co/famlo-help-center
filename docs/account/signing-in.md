# Signing in and creating your account

Famlo uses one screen for both: enter your email at famlo.co/login and Famlo
sends you a six-digit code. If you already have an account, the code signs you
in. If you don't, it starts a new, free account. You need to be 18 or older to
have an account; children don't get their own logins.

## Log in or sign up with an emailed code

1. On famlo.co, open the menu and select **Log in or sign up** (on a phone,
   tap **Log in** in the bar at the bottom), or go straight to famlo.co/login.
2. Type your address in **Email** (1).
3. Select **Continue** (2). Famlo emails you a code.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/signing-in-log-in-or-sign-up.webp" alt="The Log in or sign up screen with the Email field and the Continue button highlighted, above a passkey button and the Use a password instead link" url="famlo.co/login" width={1280} height={800} />

4. Open the email. Its subject is "Your Famlo confirmation code is" followed
   by the six digits.
5. On **Confirm it's you**, type the six digits into the boxes (1).
6. Select **Continue** (2).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/signing-in-confirm-code.webp" alt="The Confirm it's you screen with the six code boxes, the Continue button and Try another way highlighted" url="famlo.co/login/verify" width={1280} height={800} />

If you already have an account, you're now signed in. If you're new, Famlo
asks for a few details next (see the next section).

About the code:

- It works for **10 minutes**, and you get 5 tries at typing it.
- If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then select **Send a new
  code** under "Didn't get it?". Sending a new code cancels the old one.
- A wrong or expired code shows "That code isn't right, or it has expired.
  Request a new one."
- If you ask for too many codes in a short time, you'll see "Too many attempts.
  Please wait a few minutes and try again."
- **Try another way** (3) lets you use a passkey or your password instead (see
  [Other ways in](#other-ways-in)).

The first screen looks the same whether or not an address has an account, so
nobody can use it to find out who is on Famlo.

## Create your account (first time only)

After you confirm the code for a new address, you'll see **Let's create your
account**, with the line "You're signing up as" and your email.

1. Fill in **First name** and **Last name**.
2. Choose a **Password**. It needs at least 8 characters. Very common
   passwords, and passwords that have shown up in a data breach on another
   website, are refused. You can always sign in with an emailed code instead.
3. If you'd like news from Famlo, tick "Email me occasional updates from Famlo
   — new activities near me, and product news. Unsubscribe anytime." It is
   unticked unless you choose it.
4. Select **Agree and continue**. This confirms you are 18 or older and agree
   to Famlo's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Finish this screen within about 30 minutes of entering your code. After that,
start again from famlo.co/login.

### Everyone belongs here

Next comes the Community Commitment: "I will treat everyone in this community
— every family, every leader, and every child — with respect, and without
judgment or bias."

- Select **Agree and continue** to finish. You'll see "Welcome to Famlo! Your
  account is ready." and Famlo sends you a welcome email.
- Select **Decline** if you don't want to agree. Famlo asks "Declining removes
  the account you just created. Continue?" If you continue, the account is
  deleted.

If you were in the middle of a checkout when you signed up, Famlo takes you
straight back to it and skips this screen.

## The Welcome back screen

When a browser has been used to sign in to Famlo, it remembers you for 45 days,
even after you log out. Instead of the email field you'll see **Welcome back**
with your first name and a partly hidden email address.

- Select **Log in** to have a code sent to that address. Then continue from
  step 4 above.
- If you've used a passkey on this browser, the screen leads with the passkey
  button, with **Email me a code instead** below it.
- Select **Not you?** to make the browser forget you and bring back the empty
  email field. Do this on a shared or borrowed computer.

The Welcome back screen can't sign anyone in by itself. A code still goes only
to your inbox.

## Other ways in

### Passkey, Touch ID or Windows Hello

If you've added a passkey (see [Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security)),
you can sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. The button sits
under **Continue**, after "or". Its name depends on your device:

| Device | Button |
|---|---|
| Mac | **Continue with Touch ID** |
| Windows | **Continue with Windows Hello** |
| iPhone, iPad and others | **Continue with a passkey** |

The button only appears on a device that can use a passkey. Your browser may
also offer a saved passkey when you tap the **Email** field.

### Google, Facebook or Apple

Below the passkey button are **Continue with Google**, **Continue with
Facebook** and **Continue with Apple** (they aren't in the picture at the top
of this page). On a wide screen they show as three icons.

- **No Famlo account yet:** the first time you use one of these, Famlo creates
  an account with the email that Google, Facebook or Apple shares, and shows
  the **Everyone belongs here** screen.
- **You already have an account with that email:** Google and Apple connect to
  it automatically. Facebook doesn't. You'll see "An account already exists
  for" your email; sign in with your code or password first, then connect
  Facebook in [Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security).
- **The provider shares no email:** Famlo can't sign you in that way. Use your
  email instead.
- **Apple's Hide My Email:** your Famlo account uses Apple's private relay
  address, not your real one. It won't connect to an account you made with
  your real email.
- An account made this way has no password. You can still sign in with an
  emailed code, or set a password later.

### Use a password instead

1. On **Log in or sign up**, select **Use a password instead**.
2. On **Enter your password**, fill in **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Continue**.

From there, **Forgot your password?** sends a reset link, and **Email me a
sign-in code instead** takes you back to the code. A wrong password shows "Try
another email address or password."

### Try another way

On **Confirm it's you**, select **Try another way** for two options: **Use a
passkey** ("If you set one up on this device") and **Enter your password**.
The passkey row shows only on a device that can use one.

## Signing up from an activity page

On some free activities you can skip this page entirely. Type your email in
the card, select **Remind me the day before**, and confirm with the six-digit
code. That signs you up for the activity and makes a free Famlo account in one
go, with no password.
See [RSVP to a free activity](/docs/families/booking/free-rsvp).

## Signing in on an organization's own website

Some organizations have their own website that takes bookings through Famlo.
Selecting **Parent portal** or **Sign in** there opens a Famlo page titled
**Sign in with Famlo**, dressed in that organization's look. Every way in on
this page works there too, and the same Famlo account works on every
organization's website. See
[Organizations with their own website](/docs/families/organization-websites/overview).

## Where you land

- **Signing in:** the page you were trying to reach, or your **Dashboard**
  (**Home** on a phone).
- **A brand-new account:** where you were headed (an activity, a checkout, an
  invitation). If you were just browsing, you land on Home with a **Getting
  started** checklist. See [Your Home page](/docs/families/home).
- **Signed up to accept a staff invitation:** that organization's console. See
  [Your console and Today](/docs/organizations/getting-started/your-console).

Invitations from organizations sent to your address are accepted when you
create your account with it.

## How long you stay signed in

You stay signed in on a device until you go 30 days without using Famlo there,
or for a year at most. Then you sign in again.

When you sign in from a device or place your account hasn't used before, Famlo
emails you "Did you just sign in to Famlo?" with a **Review my account**
button. If it wasn't you, change your password right away.

## Log out

- **On a computer:** open the avatar menu (your picture, top right) and select
  **Log out**.
- **On a phone:** open **Account settings**, scroll to the bottom and select
  **Log out**. Confirm "Log out of Famlo on this device?"

Logging out signs out only the device you're on. To sign out other devices,
use **Device history** in [Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security).
On a shared computer, also select **Not you?** on the next sign-in screen so it
stops greeting you by name.

## Troubleshooting

**The code never arrived.**
Check spam and any "Promotions" or "Updates" tab, make sure the address on
**Confirm it's you** is spelled right, then select **Send a new code**. Only
the newest code works.

**"That code isn't right, or it has expired."**
Codes last 10 minutes and allow 5 tries. Ask for a new one and use it straight
away.

**"Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again."**
You asked for or tried codes too many times in a short time. Wait a few
minutes, then try once more.

**"Check that address. Gmail ignores dots, so try typing it without them."**
Famlo spotted an unusual Gmail address. Type it without the extra dots.

**"This account is scheduled for deletion, so it can't be signed in to."**
The confirmation email you got when you asked to delete the account has a link
to cancel it. See [Download your data or delete your account](/docs/account/closing-your-account).

**I can't get in any way.**
See [How do I recover my account?](/docs/how-to/recover-account)
