# Notifications

You choose what Famlo tells you about, and how, in **Account settings** › **Notifications**. Every kind of notification has its own **In-app**, **Email** and **Push** boxes, and one switch pauses all non-essential email at once. Essential account emails, such as booking confirmations and refunds, are always sent.

## Change your notification settings

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/notifications-grid.webp" alt="The Notifications page in Account settings, with In-app, Email and Push boxes for each kind of notification and the Save preferences button" url="famlo.co/account/notifications" width={1280} height={1440} />

1. Open the avatar menu and choose **Account settings**.
2. Select **Notifications** (1). On a phone, **Account settings** opens as a list; tap **Notifications**.
3. For each kind of notification, tick or clear **In-app**, **Email** and **Push**.
4. Select **Save preferences** (2). You'll see "Notification preferences saved."

Nothing changes until you select **Save preferences**. Everything starts switched on.

You can also get here from the gear menu in **Messages** (**Notification settings**), or from **Manage notification preferences** at the foot of any Famlo email.

## What each box does

| Box | Where it reaches you |
| --- | --- |
| **In-app** | Your **Messages** inbox on famlo.co. The bell in the header opens it, and on a phone it's the **Messages** tab. |
| **Email** | The email address on your account. |
| **Push** | Your phone, through the Famlo iPhone app. |

A Famlo iPhone app is coming soon. Until you're signed in on it, the page says "You're not signed in on a phone yet", and the **Push** boxes don't do anything. Famlo doesn't send browser notifications.

There's no separate notifications page. Everything in the app gathers in [Messages](/docs/families/messages), including replies to your support tickets. There's no quiet-hours setting.

## What you're notified about

Everyone sees these rows.

| Row | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Event reminders** | Reminders about activities you're booked on, the Friday weekly check-ins you turned on, and a reminder if a payment request from an organization is still unpaid. |
| **Activity updates** | New activities, freed-up spots, announcements and requests for a review from organizations you follow, saved or booked with, and new listings that match your location alerts. |
| **Messages & followers** | Direct messages from organizers and new followers. This also covers a once-a-day email when you have unread conversations. |
| **News from Famlo** | Occasional messages from the Famlo team about your account or the product. |
| **Marketing email** | Announcements, new activities near you, and occasional offers. Never about a child. |

Turning off a row's **Email** box doesn't turn off its **In-app** box, and the reverse is also true. For example, with **Activity updates** email off, an organization's announcement still shows in your Messages inbox.

## For organizations you run

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/notifications-organizers.webp" alt="The For organizations you run section, with Team updates, Team inbox, Bookings & sign-ups and Payments & invoices" url="famlo.co/account/notifications" width={736} height={565} />

If you own or help run an organization, the page has a second section, **For organizations you run**. These settings apply to every organization you own or help run. You can't set them differently for each one.

| Row | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Team updates** | Role changes, join requests, roster changes and reminders to take attendance. |
| **Team inbox** | Families' questions to your organization's inbox, and messages flagged for review. |
| **Bookings & sign-ups** | A family booked, signed up for, moved or canceled something at your organization. |
| **Payments & invoices** | Scholarship invoices families send to their program for your classes. |

The "New booking for [class]" email goes to the person who created or claimed the organization's listing. Every booking also shows in your console, whether or not the email is on. To stop the booking emails, clear **Email** on **Bookings & sign-ups** and save.

Some organization messages are always sent. These include refund requests from families, a family paying a payment request you sent, and a refund or payment that fails.

## Pause all notification email

The **Email** section has one switch, **Receive notification emails**. Clear it and select **Save preferences** to pause all non-essential email at once.

- Essential account emails still arrive (see [Emails you always get](#emails-you-always-get)).
- It pauses email only. In-app notifications, and the **Push** boxes, stay as you set them.
- It doesn't pause **Bookings & sign-ups** email for organizers. To stop those, clear that row's **Email** box.

## Weekly check-ins, location alerts and Famlo updates

The controls under **Save preferences** each save straight away, with their own buttons.

- **Weekly check-ins**: one row for each child, with **Turn on** or **Turn off**. When it's on, Famlo reminds you on Fridays to confirm that child's school hours for the week, filled in from their usual week. Check-ins follow your **Event reminders** settings. See [Log a week](/docs/families/school-records/log-a-week).
- **Location alerts**: the areas you're watching, each with **Remove**, and **Add an area** to set up another. Alerts follow your **Activity updates** settings. See [Location alerts](/docs/families/discover/location-alerts).
- **Updates from Famlo**: Famlo's own email list, about new activities near you and product news. Having an account doesn't put you on it. To join, tick the box and select **Add me**. Once you're on it, the section shows the date you joined and **Unsubscribe from Famlo updates**.
- **Series you're in**: appears under **Updates from Famlo** when you're getting a short run of emails on one topic. **Stop this series** ends that run and keeps you on the list for everything else.

## Organization mailing lists

If you asked an organization to email you when you registered, it appears under **Organization mailing lists**. These emails come from the organization itself, not from Famlo, so the boxes above don't apply to them.

To leave one, select **Unsubscribe** next to the organization, then **Unsubscribe** again. Leaving a mailing list doesn't change your Famlo account, your membership, or anything you've booked.

## Unsubscribe from an email

Emails you can turn off have an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom. You don't need to sign in to use it.

1. Select **Unsubscribe** in the email.
2. The page asks, for example, "Unsubscribe from activity update emails?". Select **Unsubscribe**.

This turns off email for that one kind of notification, the same as clearing that row's **Email** box. To turn it back on, tick the box again in **Account settings** › **Notifications** and save.

A marketing email that's part of a series offers **Stop this series** (leave just that series) or **Unsubscribe from everything** (stop all marketing email).

## Emails you always get

Some emails can't be turned off, because you need them to use something you paid for, joined or asked for. They include:

- booking confirmations, cancellations and refunds
- payment requests, failed payments and expiring cards
- membership payment reminders and expiry notices
- invitations, and answers to your requests to join an organization
- an organization's replies to your questions about an activity
- security emails: sign-in codes, a password or email change, passkeys added or removed, a sign-in from a new device, and account deletion

## Troubleshooting

**I'm not getting any email from Famlo.** Check that **Receive notification emails** is ticked and that **Email** is ticked on the rows you want, then select **Save preferences**. Look in your spam folder too, and make sure the address under **Personal information** is one you still read (see [Your profile and preferences](/docs/account/personal-information)).

**I turned email off but still get some.** It's probably an essential email from the list above, which always arrives. Or it's from an organization's mailing list, which you leave under **Organization mailing lists**.

**I paused email but still get "New booking" emails.** **Receive notification emails** doesn't pause **Bookings & sign-ups**. Clear that row's **Email** box and save.

**My changes didn't stick.** The boxes and the email switch only save when you select **Save preferences**. If you left the page first, set them again and save.

**I don't get any location alerts.** Check that **Activity updates** is on for the way you want to hear (in the app or by email), and that you have at least one area under **Location alerts**.
