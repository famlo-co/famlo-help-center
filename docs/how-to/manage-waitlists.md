# How Do I Handle Waitlists for Popular Events?

When a class on Famlo fills up, its button changes to **Join the waitlist**. The waitlist isn't a queue, though, and that changes how organizers and families should use it. This guide covers both sides: what organizers can do when a class keeps filling, and how families can get in.

## How the waitlist works

**Join the waitlist** saves the activity. That's the whole mechanism. When a spot opens, **everyone** who saved it is alerted at the same time, and whoever registers first gets it. Nobody has a position, nobody is moved up automatically, and no spot is held for anyone.

| | Famlo's waitlist | A queue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who hears that a spot opened | Everyone waiting, at the same time | The next person in line |
| Who gets it | Whoever registers first | Whoever is at the front |
| Your position | None. Joining earlier doesn't put you ahead | Set by when you joined |
| Getting in | You register yourself | You're moved into the spot |
| Is a spot held | No | Yes, for the person moved up |

A spot opens when:

- a family cancels,
- a booking is refunded,
- a checkout that was never finished lets its spot go, or
- the organizer raises the number of spots.

If several spots open within a few minutes, families get one alert, and its title says how many spots are open ("3 spots opened in …").

:::warning
Joining early doesn't put you ahead of anyone. You hear about an open spot at the same time as everyone else, and you still have to register for it.
:::

## For organizers

### See how many families are waiting

Open **Classes** and select the class. The **This class** panel shows **Spots** as booked out of total (1). Under **Views, last 30 days** (2), a line such as "3 saved, not registered" counts the families who saved the class but haven't booked it.

Families see a similar count on the activity page, under the button.

### Add spots to a full class

When a class is full, the class editor shows a suggestion in **Price & spots**, such as "Pottery Wheel Workshop is full. 3 families are waiting. Add spots, or open another section."

1. Open **Classes**, select the class, then open **•••** and select **Edit details**.
2. In **Price & spots**, select **Add spots** (1) on the suggestion. This takes you to the **Spots** box (2) and doesn't save anything yet.
3. Enter the new total in **Spots** (2).
4. Select **Save · review what families see**, check the summary, and save.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/manage-waitlists-add-spots.webp" alt="The Price and spots card for a full class, with the Add spots suggestion, the Spots box and a per-order limit highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/pottery-wheel-workshop-cape-coral-fl-h26s0r6bzh/edit" width={648} height={736} />

When the new spots are saved, every family who saved the class gets a spot-opened alert. You don't need to message anyone. Select **Not now** to hide the suggestion for this class. Hiding it doesn't stop you from changing **Spots** later.

### Limit how many spots one order can take

A price can have a **Most per order** limit, which caps how many spots one checkout can book. It's on the **Change** line below a saved price (3). That line appears once the price has one of its extra details set, such as its own name or a sale window. Families who try to book more see "You can book at most [number] for [price name]." The limit applies to each order, so a family could still book again in a second checkout.

### Single dates fill on their own

If a class sells single dates as well as the whole term, each date can fill up separately. Under **Spots**, **When a date fills** gives you two choices:

- **First come**: "Drop-ins can fill a date. Once it's full, the term is offered for the dates with room."
- **Protect the term**: **Hold** a number of spots "on every date for whole-term families. Blank holds half the class. Drop-ins can't take the last of them."

When a spot opens on one date, Famlo alerts the families who saved that date or the class. Families who saved other dates aren't alerted. For more on single dates, see [Prices, spots and ways to join](/docs/organizations/tickets-and-pricing).

### What you can't do

- **Register a family into a full class yourself.** There's no control for adding a family to a class. To let someone in, a spot has to exist first, and then whoever registers first gets it.
- **Reorder the waitlist or pick who gets the next spot.** There's no order to change.
- **Turn the waitlist off.** Every full class on famlo.co offers **Join the waitlist**.

### If demand keeps beating capacity

- **Add spots** if your space and ratios allow it.
- Run it again. Open **•••** on the class and select **Duplicate** to make a draft copy, then change the dates.
- Split the class into sections so families can choose another time. See [Split a class into sections](/docs/organizations/classes/sections).
- Tell families ahead of time when registration opens, so everyone starts at the same time, instead of the spots going to whoever happened to be looking.

## For families

### Join the waitlist

1. Open the full activity.
2. Select **Join the waitlist** (1). The heart on the activity page or on an activity card does the same thing.
3. The button changes to **You're on the waitlist**. Under it, a line says how many families are waiting.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/manage-waitlists-join-button.webp" alt="A full activity page with the Join the waitlist button highlighted and the line 3 families are already waiting" url="famlo.co/activities/pottery-wheel-workshop-cape-coral-fl-h26s0r6bzh" width={1280} height={800} />

The activity now appears on your **Saved** page (avatar menu › **Saved**), with the label **You're on the waitlist**. To leave the waitlist, select **You're on the waitlist** again, or the filled heart.

If one date of a class is full at checkout, select **Tell me if a spot opens** next to it. That opens the page for that date, where you can join its waitlist.

For more on the **Saved** page, see [Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

### The alerts you'll get

| Alert | When | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Almost full | Once per activity, when a class you saved is nearly full (5 or fewer spots left, or 10% or less on a class of more than 50) | "[Activity] is almost full", with **Secure your spot** in the email |
| Spot opened | Every time a spot opens on a full activity you saved | "A spot opened in [Activity]" (or "3 spots opened in …"), with **Register now** in the email |

Both alerts arrive in Famlo and by email, and both link to the activity page, not straight to checkout. Neither is sent if you already have a booking for that activity.

If the spot is taken before you look, the alert in Famlo changes to "[Activity] filled up again". You stay on the waitlist and get another alert the next time a spot opens.

### Make sure the alerts reach you

1. Open your avatar menu and select **Account settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Find the **Activity updates** row. It covers freed-up spots.
3. Tick **In-app**, **Email** or both.
4. Select **Save preferences**.

Leaving **Email** ticked is the best way to hear about a spot quickly. See [Notifications](/docs/account/notifications).

### Improve your chances

- **Save it as soon as you find it full.** You can't be alerted about an activity you haven't saved.
- **Decide in advance:** which child, which option, and whether the date still works.
- **Save the other dates or times too.** A less popular slot often has room.
- **Stay on after a near miss.** Every new opening sends a new alert.

## Troubleshooting

**I clicked the alert and the activity was full again.**
Another family registered first. You're still on the waitlist, and you'll get an alert the next time a spot opens.

**Where am I on the waitlist?**
You don't have a position. Everyone waiting is alerted at the same time, and the first family to register gets the spot.

**I'm an organizer and a family asked me to let them in.**
You can't add them to a full class. If you can take more children, raise **Spots**. Everyone waiting is then alerted, including that family.

**I didn't get an alert.**
Check that **Activity updates** is ticked in **Account settings** › **Notifications**, and that the activity is still on your **Saved** page. You won't get alerts for an activity you've already booked.
