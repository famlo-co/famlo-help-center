# How Do I Recover My Account?

Most people who can't get into Famlo are looking for a password they don't
need. Every account can sign in with a six-digit code sent to its email
address, so if you can read that mailbox, you can get back in. This page
covers the code, the other ways in, a forgotten password, and what to do if
none of them work.

## Quick answer

1. Go to famlo.co/login.
2. Type your address in **Email** and select **Continue**.
3. Open the email titled "Your Famlo confirmation code is" followed by six
   digits.
4. On **Confirm it's you**, type the six digits and select **Continue**.

You're signed in. The code is something you type, not a link you click, and
it works for **10 minutes**.

:::tip
If you see **Let's create your account** after typing the code, Famlo has no
account for that address. Don't fill it in. Go back to famlo.co/login and try
the other addresses you use.
:::

The full sign-in guide is in
[Signing in and creating your account](/docs/account/signing-in).

## If Famlo says "Welcome back"

A browser you've signed in on before remembers you for 45 days, even after you
log out. Instead of the email field, the sign-in screen shows "Welcome back"
with your first name and part of your email address.

- Select **Log in** (1) to have a code sent to that address. Then type it on
  **Confirm it's you**, as above.
- Select **Not you?** (2) if that isn't you, or if you want to sign in with a
  different address. The browser forgets the account and the usual email
  field comes back.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/recover-account-welcome-back.webp" alt="The Welcome back sign-in screen with a masked email address, the Log in button and the Not you? link highlighted" url="famlo.co/login" width={1280} height={800} />

If you've used a passkey on that browser, the screen puts **Continue with a passkey** first.
On a Mac it reads **Continue with Touch ID**, and on Windows **Continue with Windows Hello**.
Select **Email me a code instead** if the passkey doesn't work.

The partly hidden address is a useful clue when you can't remember which
email you signed up with.

## The other ways in

Each of these opens the same account. Adding one never turns another off, and
the emailed code always works as long as you can read the mailbox.

| Way in | Where it is | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emailed code | **Email**, then **Continue** | Access to your inbox |
| Passkey | **Continue with a passkey** (or **Touch ID**, **Windows Hello**), under the email field | The device or password manager it's saved in |
| Google, Facebook or Apple | The Google, Facebook and Apple buttons, below the passkey button (on a phone they read **Continue with Google** and so on) | That account to be connected to Famlo |
| Password | **Use a password instead** | A password you set earlier |

On the code screen, **Try another way** also offers your passkey or
**Enter your password**.

A passkey is the one way in that doesn't depend on your inbox. Once you're
back in, add one from **Account settings** › **Login & security**. See
[Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security).

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple and that account isn't
connected to Famlo, and its email has no Famlo account, Famlo starts a new
account instead. If you see **Everyone belongs here**, you're creating a new
account; select **Decline** to remove it and try another way in.

## If you set a password and forgot it

A password on Famlo is optional. The quickest fix is to skip it and sign in
with an emailed code. If you'd rather reset it:

1. At famlo.co/login, select **Use a password instead**.
2. Select **Forgot your password?**.
3. Type your address in **Email** (1).
4. Select **Email reset instructions** (2).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/account/recover-account-forgot-password.webp" alt="The Forgot your password? screen with the Email field and the Email reset instructions button highlighted" url="famlo.co/forgot-password" width={1280} height={800} />

5. Open the email titled "Reset your password" and select its button. The
   link works for **15 minutes**.
6. On **Update your password**, fill in **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
   It needs at least 8 characters.
7. Select **Save new password**.

You'll see "Password has been reset." The reset signs you out on every
device, so sign in again with your new password. Your passkeys keep working.

The screen confirms the request the same way whether or not the address has
an account, so if no email arrives, the address may not be the one on your
account.

## If your account is scheduled for deletion

An account that's waiting to be deleted can't be signed in to. Famlo shows:
"This account is scheduled for deletion, so it can't be signed in to. The
confirmation email we sent has a link to cancel it."

1. Find the email titled "Your Famlo account is scheduled for deletion".
2. Select **Cancel deletion**.
3. Sign in with an emailed code.

You can cancel for 30 days after asking to delete. Canceling brings your
account back, but not your password, passkeys, connected Google, Facebook or
Apple sign-ins, canceled memberships or saved cards. Set those up again once
you're in. See [Closing your account](/docs/account/closing-your-account).

If the link says "That link has expired or the account has already been
deleted," the 30 days have passed and the account is gone. You're welcome to
create a new one.

## If you think someone else signed in

When your account is signed in to from a device or place it hasn't used
before, Famlo emails you "Did you just sign in to Famlo?" with when, where and
the device. If it was you, ignore it.

If it wasn't you:

1. Sign in with an emailed code.
2. Open **Account settings** › **Login & security**.
3. Change your password. That signs out every other device and removes every
   passkey on the account.
4. Check **Device history** and sign out anything you don't recognize.

A "Reset your password" email you didn't ask for is safe to ignore. Your
password doesn't change unless someone uses the link.

## Why you were signed out

- **You were away for 30 days.** Famlo signs you out after 30 days without
  activity, and after a year in any case. Sign in again with a code.
- **The password changed.** Changing it in **Login & security** signs out
  every other device. A reset signs out every device.
- **The device was signed out from Device history**, by you on another device.

## Troubleshooting

**The code hasn't arrived.** Wait a minute, then check your spam folder and
search your mail for "Famlo". Select **Send a new code** on **Confirm it's you**.
A new code cancels the old one.

**It says "That code isn't right, or it has expired."** You have 10 minutes
and 5 tries per code. Request a new one and use the latest email.

**It says "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again."** You
asked for several codes quickly. Wait a few minutes, then try once.

**I don't know which email I used.** Search each mailbox for "Famlo" and look
for a code, a booking or a welcome email. A browser you've used before shows
part of the address on the "Welcome back" screen. If you connected Google,
Facebook or Apple, try that button.

**I can't read that mailbox anymore.** If you can still sign in another way
(a passkey, Google, Facebook, Apple or a password), change your address in
**Account settings** › **Personal information**. Famlo sends a code to the new
address, and the old one keeps working until you confirm. See
[Your profile and preferences](/docs/account/personal-information). If you
can't sign in at all, contact support (below).

**The reset link says "Password reset link is invalid or has expired."** Links
last 15 minutes and work until the password changes. Request a new one, or
sign in with a code instead.

**Was my account deleted for not using it?** No. Famlo doesn't remove
accounts for inactivity. Check that you're using the right address.

## Getting help when you can't sign in

The support desk at famlo.co/support needs you to be signed in. If you can't
sign in, reply to an email from Famlo instead, such as a code email or a
"Reset your password" email. Your reply opens a support ticket. (Replies to
an email about a message go into that conversation, so don't use those.)

You can always get a fresh email to reply to: enter your address at
famlo.co/login and reply to the code email.

Tell us the address you think is on the account and what happens when you
try. We read every ticket and usually reply within one business day.
