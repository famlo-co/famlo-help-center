# How Do I Start a Homeschool Group?

This guide takes you from no listing to a first class families can book, in the order that works best. Anyone with a Famlo account can start a group and becomes its owner. Listing it and running free classes costs nothing.

## Quick Answer

Open the account menu and choose **List your organization**, answer the short set of questions, and select **Publish**. You land on **Today** in your console, where the **Get ready** card lists what's left. Add your teachers by name, invite your families by email, choose who can join, and publish your first class. If you'll take money, choose a Famlo plan and connect Stripe.

## Step 1: Create your listing, or claim the one that exists

Search Famlo for your group's name first. Families or Famlo sometimes add a group before its leaders arrive.

**If your group is already listed**, open its page. A bar under the header asks **Is this your group?**

1. Select **Claim this listing →**.
2. Fill in **Business name**, **Your role at this business** and **Verification details**, such as a website or a social profile.
3. Select **Submit claim**.

Famlo reviews the claim and emails you the outcome. Once it's approved, you own the listing and it's marked verified. See [Claim an existing listing](/docs/organizations/getting-started/claim-your-listing).

**If it isn't listed**, create it:

1. Open the account menu and choose **List your organization**. If you already run one, choose **Add organization**.
2. Pick your type, such as **Co-op**, **Learning pod** or **Community**, and say where you serve families: a place, or online.
3. Choose who can join: **Open**, **Closed** or **Private**. You can change this later.
4. Add a name, then a description, a cover photo and a logo if you have them. Those three can wait.
5. Pick at least one category. Families filter by these.
6. On the review screen, select **Publish**.

Famlo saves your progress once you've said where you are, so you can stop and come back. For every screen in detail, see [Create your listing](/docs/organizations/listing-your-organization).

## Step 2: Work through the Get ready card

After you publish, you land on **Today** in your console. A **Get ready** card lists the steps left for your type of organization, each with its own button. For a community it looks like this:

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/start-homeschool-group-get-ready.webp" alt="The Get ready card on Today, with Add a class, Set dates and Invite families highlighted" url="famlo.co/community/oak-hollow-homeschool-community/manage" width={556} height={652} />

- **Add a class** (1) starts your first class (Step 6).
- **Set dates** (2) opens **Schedule**, where you name your semester and give its first and last day.
- **Invite families** (3) opens your invitations (Step 4).

Groups that take payments also see **Decide how you'll take money**. Its **Choose** button opens your Famlo plan (Step 7).

Only you and your co-leaders see the card. It goes away for good once your group takes its first booking or gains its first member. You can also select **Skip setup** to hide it for everyone on your team.

To get back to your console later, open the account menu and choose **Manage organizations**. See [Your console and Today](/docs/organizations/getting-started/your-console).

## Step 3: Add your teachers and helpers

You can add the people who teach or help with just their name. They don't need an email address or a Famlo account yet.

1. Open **People**, then **Staff & teachers**.
2. Select **Add a teacher or staff member**.
3. Type their **Name** (1). A title and a short bio are optional.
4. Under **What can they do?** (2), pick a starting point: **Co-leader**, **Class organizer**, **Teacher**, **Front desk**, **Bookkeeper** or **Profile only**. **Teacher** is the default.
5. Select **Add to staff** (3).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/start-homeschool-group-add-staff.webp" alt="The Add a teacher or staff member panel with Name, What can they do? and Add to staff highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/members" width={1280} height={900} />

Nothing is sent to anyone. They show on your public Staff page, and you can assign them to classes right away. When you're ready for them to sign in, add their email from their profile. Each starting point can be adjusted area by area later.

See [Add staff, connect their accounts, and your Staff page](/docs/organizations/team/add-staff) and [What staff can do](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

## Step 4: Invite your families

Invitations go by email address. There are no invite codes.

1. Open **People**, then **Invitations**. The **Invite families** button on **People** or on the **Get ready** card opens the same page.
2. Paste their emails, one per line, or upload a CSV file. In Google Sheets, use File › Download › CSV.
3. Select **Send invitations**.

Families who already have a Famlo account join straight away. Everyone else gets an email and joins when they sign up with that address. You can send up to 500 at a time. Unused invitations expire after 30 days, and you can **Resend** or **Revoke** any of them.

To add a single family, open **People** and select **Add a family**. By default only owners can invite. See [People: families, children, join requests and invitations](/docs/organizations/team/people).

## Step 5: Decide who can join

The choice you made when you created the listing lives in **School settings** › **Joining**. Each row has its own **Save**.

| Row | What you decide |
| --- | --- |
| **Visibility** | **Open** (anyone can see everything), **Closed** (listed publicly, but events and the member roster are for members only) or **Private** (hidden from search and reachable only by a link you share) |
| **How people join** | **Anyone can join instantly**, **They request to join and you review each one**, or **Invite only (they join from a link you send)** |
| **Join rules** | Optional rules, and whether members must accept them before joining |
| **Screening questions** | Up to three questions people answer when they ask to join |

Closed and Private groups always review requests. Requests wait in **People** › **Join requests**. To charge membership dues, your visibility must be Closed or Private. See [School settings](/docs/organizations/settings) and [Membership dues](/docs/organizations/membership-dues).

## Step 6: Publish your first class

A group with something on the calendar gives families a reason to say yes.

1. Open **Classes** and select **New class**, or select **Add a class** on the **Get ready** card.
2. Choose how to start: a shape such as **Weekly class**, **One day** or **Camp**, or **Start blank**.
3. Add a name, when and where it meets, and a price or free.
4. Add a cover photo, a short description and a category.
5. Select **Publish**. To come back to it later, select **Save draft** instead.

The **Families will see** box lists anything still missing before families can book. A paid class also needs refund terms and a way for families to pay (Step 8). Free classes don't. See [Create and publish a class](/docs/organizations/activities).

## Step 7: Choose a Famlo plan

Every group starts on **Free**, which has no time limit and covers listing, classes, registrations, rosters, attendance, messages and card payments. Two paid plans add more:

- **Scholarship** ($49 a month) is for approved Step Up providers. Families can pay with their Step Up scholarship, and Famlo writes and tracks the invoices.
- **Website** ($99 a month or $990 a year) adds your own website and everything in Scholarship.

To change plans, open **School settings** › **Plan & billing**. By default the Owner and the Bookkeeper can do this.

See [Famlo plans](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans), and for Step Up, [Accept Step Up and ESA payments](/docs/organizations/money/step-up-and-esa).

## Step 8: Set up getting paid

To take card payments, connect Stripe. Only the person who created or claimed the listing can do this.

1. Open **School settings** › **Getting paid**.
2. Select **Connect with Stripe**.
3. Follow Stripe's steps. Have your bank account, your EIN (or SSN) and a photo ID ready.

Card payments go straight into your own Stripe account. Families pay a booking fee on paid bookings (3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus Stripe's processing), so you receive the full price you set. You can choose to pay it yourself instead. Free classes and scholarship invoices never need Stripe.

See [Set up getting paid with Stripe](/docs/organizations/getting-paid) and [The booking fee](/docs/organizations/money/booking-fee).

## Troubleshooting

**Publish says my listing isn't ready.** It needs a name of at least three characters, a location (or online states), and at least one category. The reasons are listed above the summary, and your draft is kept.

**I closed the tab partway through.** Open the account menu and choose **List your organization** again. Under **In progress**, select **Continue** on your draft.

**I don't see the Get ready card.** It shows only to owners and co-leaders, and only until your group takes its first booking or gains its first member, or until someone selects **Skip setup**. Everything on it is still in the console.

**There's no Invite families button.** Inviting needs permission to add staff, which only owners have by default. Ask an owner to invite people or to change what you can do.

**I can't find Getting paid.** Only the person who created or claimed the listing sees it, even if you're also an owner.

**My group doesn't show in search.** Check **School settings** › **Joining** › **Visibility**. **Private** groups aren't listed anywhere, and **Published (visible in the directory)** must be ticked.
