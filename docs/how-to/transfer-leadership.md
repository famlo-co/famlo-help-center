# How Do I Transfer Group Leadership?

To hand over your organization, you make the new leader an owner, overlap for a while, and then step back. Only an owner can make someone else an owner. Payouts and closing the listing stay with the person who created or claimed the listing (the creator) until the creator hands them over from the **Danger zone**.

## Quick answer

There are two steps:

1. **Make the new leader an owner.** Open **People** › **Staff & teachers**, select the person, choose **Change what [name] can do**, tick **Owner**, and select **Save**. You stay an owner too: an organization can have more than one, and promoting someone never demotes you.
2. **If you're the creator, hand over the creator role.** Open **School settings** › **Danger zone** and select **Hand it over**. Once the new leader accepts, payouts and the **Danger zone** are theirs, and you stay an Owner.

## Make someone else an owner

The person needs to be on your staff list first. If they belong to your organization as a family, open **People** › **Parents**, then **Open [name]** from the **⋯** menu on their row. If they aren't on Famlo with you yet, add them with **Add a teacher or staff member** and their email. See [Add staff, connect their accounts, and your Staff page](/docs/organizations/team/add-staff).

1. Open **People** (1) in your console.
2. Select the **Staff & teachers** tab (2).
3. Select the person's row (3). Their details open in a side panel.

4. Select **Change what [name] can do**.
5. Tick **Owner** (5), near the bottom of the page.
6. Select **Save** (6).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/transfer-leadership-owner-box.webp" alt="The access page for a teacher, with the Owner box and the Save button highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/members/20/access" width={1280} height={900} />

When you save, the new owner gets a notice in Famlo, "Your role changed at [your organization]", that says "You're now Owner." The change is also recorded under **Recent changes** at the foot of the **Staff & teachers** tab, with who made it and when.

If the person has no Famlo account connected yet, the page says "[name] has no account yet, so none of this applies until one is connected." Their new access starts once their account is connected.

:::note
The **Owner** box is grayed out unless you are an owner yourself. Staff who can add and edit staff see "Only an owner can make someone an owner." under it.
:::

## What a new owner gets, and what stays with the creator

An owner can open every part of the console: classes, attendance, families, messages, money, the website, staff and **School settings**. They can also make other people owners, or take it away.

Two things stay with the person who created or claimed the listing (the creator), even when other people are owners:

| | Owners | The creator |
|---|---|---|
| Every console area, including **Money** and **Plan & billing** | Yes | Yes |
| Make or remove other owners | Yes | Yes |
| **School settings** › **Getting paid** (Stripe, payouts and payment methods) | No | Yes |
| **School settings** › **Danger zone** (closing the listing) | No | Yes |

The **Owner** box describes itself as "Everything, including payouts and closing the school." In practice, only the creator can open **Getting paid** and **Danger zone**.

The creator also keeps full access to the organization even if their own **Owner** box is unticked or they are removed from the staff list.

## Hand over payouts and closing the listing

If you're the creator, you can make someone else the creator in your place. The role brings **Getting paid**, the **Danger zone**, deleting the organization, and the emails Famlo sends an organization's creator. You stay an Owner and can still run everything else.

You can hand it to someone who is already an **Owner**, or whose access is the **Co-leader** preset, and who has a Famlo account connected. Make them one first if you need to (see [Make someone else an owner](#make-someone-else-an-owner)).

1. Open **School settings**, then **Danger zone** at the bottom of the settings list.
2. Under **Hand over this organization**, select **Hand it over**.
3. Under **Confirm it's you**, enter **Your password** and select **Continue**. If you don't have a password, or don't remember it, select **Email me a code**, then enter the **Six-digit code** and select **Continue**.
4. In **Hand it to**, choose the person.
5. Select **Send the request**.

You see "Sent. We've emailed [name] to accept it." Nothing changes unless they accept. The request stays open for 14 days, and the **Danger zone** shows "Waiting for [name] to accept, until" the date, with a **Withdraw** button. Only one request can be open at a time. To ask someone else, select **Withdraw** first. They're emailed that you withdrew it, and you stay the creator.

If nobody can take it yet, the page says **Nobody to hand it to yet**: "Only an Owner or Co-leader with a Famlo account can take over as creator. Make someone one in Staff roles first."

### What the new creator does

The person you asked gets an email titled "[Your name] wants to hand [organization] over to you", with a **Review the request** button.

1. Select **Review the request** in the email, and sign in to Famlo if asked.
2. Confirm it's you, the same way as above.
3. Read what changes, then select **Accept** or **Decline**.

After **Accept**, they see "You're now the creator of [organization]." and **Getting paid** opens. You both get an email, "[name] is now the creator of [organization]". After **Decline**, nothing changes, and you're emailed that they declined.

### What happens to payouts

The organization keeps the Stripe account it already has, so payouts keep going where they go now. On the new creator's **Getting paid**, a **Stripe account** row marked "Connected by" your name says: "Payouts still go to the Stripe account [your name] connected. To manage it, [your name] adds you in Stripe (Settings › Team), or contact support to move payouts to your own account."

- **To let the new creator manage that Stripe account,** add them in your Stripe Dashboard under **Settings** › **Team**.
- **To move payouts to the new creator's own Stripe account,** they select **Contact support** on **Getting paid**, or open a ticket at famlo.co/support.

Payout setup is explained in [Set up getting paid with Stripe](/docs/organizations/getting-paid).

### When a handover can't go through

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "[organization] is scheduled for deletion. The deletion has to be cancelled first." | Cancel the deletion in the **Danger zone**, then send the request. |
| "That request expired. Ask [name] to send a new one." | The 14 days ran out. The creator sends a new request. |
| "You need to be an Owner or Co-leader of [organization] to take it over." | The person's access changed after the request was sent. Make them an Owner or Co-leader again, then they can accept. |
| "That request has already been answered or withdrawn." | The request was withdrawn, or already accepted or declined. |

## Step down as an owner

The order matters. Famlo always keeps at least one owner, so make your successor an owner first.

1. Make the new leader an owner, as above.
2. On your own row in **Staff & teachers**, select **Change what [your name] can do**.
3. Untick **Owner**, pick what you still want to do (or **Profile only** to keep just your Staff page card), and select **Save**.

If you are the only owner, Famlo refuses with "[your name] is the only owner. Make someone else an owner first." The same rule protects the last owner from being removed: the panel says "The last owner can't be removed — make someone else an owner first."

Another owner can also change your access or remove you for you.

## Leave the organization

Once you are no longer an owner, you can leave on your own:

1. Open **Account settings** › **Memberships**.
2. Find the organization under **Free groups** and select **Leave**.
3. Confirm.

Owners don't appear in that list, so they can't leave this way. Step down first, or ask another owner to select **Remove from [your organization]** on your row. You can't remove yourself from the staff panel.

If you're the creator, leaving or being removed doesn't take away your access, **Getting paid** or **Danger zone**. To pass those on, [hand over the creator role](#hand-over-payouts-and-closing-the-listing) first.

## Deleting your Famlo account after a handover

If you created the listing, you can't delete your Famlo account while the organization has other members. Famlo shows: "You own [organization], which has N other members. Transfer ownership or delete the organization first." Paid bookings for classes that haven't happened yet also block it.

To clear this, either [hand over the creator role](#hand-over-payouts-and-closing-the-listing) to the new leader, or close the listing from **School settings** › **Danger zone**. Once the handover is accepted, the organization no longer blocks your account. See [Closing your listing](/docs/organizations/closing-your-listing) and [Download your data or delete your account](/docs/account/closing-your-account).

## Plan the handover

- **Overlap.** Make the new leader an owner while you are still around, so you both have full access and they can learn on the real thing.
- **Tell your families.** Say who is taking over and when. You can send one message to everyone. See [Announcements](/docs/organizations/communication/announcements).
- **Sort out the money.** After a handover, payouts still go to the Stripe account the organization already has. Decide whether the new creator gets access to it in Stripe, or opens a ticket at famlo.co/support to move payouts to their own account.
- **Tidy the staff list.** Check who has which access in **Staff & teachers**, so the new owner inherits a list that makes sense. See [What staff can do: roles and access](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).
- **Write down what isn't in Famlo.** Venues, contacts, field trip hosts, and anything you promised families.

## Troubleshooting

**The Owner box is grayed out.** Only owners can make someone an owner. Ask an owner to do it.

**I can't untick my own Owner box.** You are the only owner. Make someone else an owner first.

**I made someone an owner and I still have full control.** That's expected. Promoting someone never demotes you.

**The person I want isn't on the staff list.** If they belong as a family, open **People** › **Parents**, then **Open [name]** from the **⋯** menu on their row. Otherwise add them with **Add a teacher or staff member**.

**The new owner can't see Getting paid or Danger zone.** Those belong to the creator only. The creator hands them over from **Danger zone** › **Hand it over**, and they move once the new owner accepts.

**I can't find Hand over this organization.** It's in **School settings** › **Danger zone**, which only the creator sees.

**The person I want isn't in the Hand it to list.** Only Owners and Co-leaders with a connected Famlo account are listed. Make them an owner first, and check that their status on **Staff & teachers** is **Signed in**.

**The new creator can't change the bank account or finish Stripe setup.** The Stripe account still belongs to the person who connected it. That person can add the new creator in Stripe (**Settings** › **Team**), or the new creator can open a ticket at famlo.co/support to move payouts to their own account.

**I can't find the organization in Memberships to leave.** Owners aren't listed there. Step down as an owner first.
