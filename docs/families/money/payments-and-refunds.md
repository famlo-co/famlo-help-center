# Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel a booking yourself until the activity starts, and Famlo shows you exactly what comes back before you confirm. Each organization sets its own refund terms, and you'll see them on the activity before you book. Once an activity has started, you can ask the organization for a refund instead.

Only the Famlo account that made a booking can cancel it or ask for a refund on it.

## Cancel a booking

1. Open the activity you booked. You can find it in **Calendar**: select the booking in **List** view and then **View activity**.
2. Select **Cancel registration** (1) under **You're going**. The same link is in the booking's panel on your calendar.
3. Read the **Cancel booking** screen. It shows **Amount paid**, **Activity refund**, **Service fee refund** and **Total refund**, with one line explaining the result.
4. Select **Cancel booking** (2) to confirm, or **Keep booking** to leave it as it is.

You're asked to confirm once more ("Cancel this booking? This can't be undone."). Cancelling gives up the spot. To come back, you book again, if there's still room.

**Service fee refund** is the booking fee. The row shows how much of it, if any, comes back on this cancellation.

### Other kinds of bookings

| What you booked | How to cancel |
|---|---|
| A free activity you RSVP'd to | Select **Cancel RSVP** on the activity page. There's no refund screen, because nothing was paid. See [RSVP to a free activity](/docs/families/booking/free-rsvp). |
| An appointment | Open the appointment and select **Cancel appointment**. You can do this up to the time the organization allows; after that, message them. See [Booking a service or appointment](/docs/families/booking/services-and-appointments). |
| A class you pay for over time | Open **Account settings** › **Memberships**, open the class under **Classes you're paying over time**, and select **Cancel** beside your child. See [Paying for a class over time](/docs/families/money/pay-over-time). |
| A visit booked with a pack | Cancel it like any booking. Until that date starts, the visit goes back to your pack. |
| A pack itself | Cancelling the pack also cancels the dates booked with it that haven't started. The cancel screen shows what comes back for the visits you haven't used. |
| A shop purchase, or a payment request with no date | You can't cancel these yourself. Message the organization, which can send you a refund. |

## Refund terms: what the organization offers

Each organization chooses the refund terms for its paid activities. You'll find them on the activity page's booking card and at checkout, as one of these sentences:

| Terms | What you'll read |
|---|---|
| Flexible | "Full refund any time before the first class." (For a single date: "Full refund any time before the date.") |
| Stepped | "Full refund until *a date they choose*. Half until the first class. None after." |
| Committed | "Enrolling commits you to the full term. Tuition is not refunded." (For a single date: "A single date is not refunded once booked.") |
| None | "No refunds if you cancel." |
| Notice, for programs you pay for monthly | "Cancel any time. Billing stops N days after you tell us." See [Enrolling in a program](/docs/families/booking/programs). |

Some charges, such as a registration fee, also carry "This fee is not refundable."

If an organization hasn't chosen terms, the activity page says so: "The organizer hasn't set a refund policy, so Famlo's default applies: a full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before it starts."

Your booking keeps the terms that were shown when you booked. If the organization changes its terms later, your refund doesn't change.

## What comes back, and where

A refund is worked out on what was charged to your card, after any promo code, scholarship award or account credit.

| How you paid | Where it goes back |
|---|---|
| Card | The card you paid with. |
| Account credit | Back to your account credit. |
| A scholarship award from the organization | Back on the award. See [Scholarships from an organization](/docs/families/scholarships). |
| Step Up or an ESA, already paid by the program | Famlo never held this money. After you cancel you'll see: "Booking cancelled. Your scholarship had already paid for this spot, so any refund is between you and the organizer — Famlo never held that money." See [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa). |

When nothing comes back, the cancel screen says why, for example "This activity has a no-refunds policy. Contact the organizer for help." or "No refund available — this activity has already started."

## Ask for a refund after an activity has started

Once an activity has started, you can no longer cancel it, but you can ask the organization for money back. The organization decides, and the answer arrives in **Messages**.

1. Open **Calendar**, switch to **List**, and select the booking. Past bookings are under **Past**. For an appointment, open the appointment instead.
2. Select **Request a refund**.
3. Under **How much?**, choose **All of it** or **Part of it**. For part, enter the amount in **How much of it**.
4. Under **What happened?**, choose **Ended early**, **Didn't happen**, **Not as described** or **Something else**.
5. Add a note in **Tell them more** if it helps. Please leave out anything medical.
6. Select **Send request**.

**Request a refund** appears only while there's card money left to return on the booking, and only one request can be open at a time. If it isn't offered, message the organization.

Your request shows in your conversation with the organization, and on the booking as "Refund requested", with a **Withdraw request** button. If you withdraw it, you can ask again later while the booking still allows it.

The organization can:

- **Approve it**: you get an email, "*Organization* approved your refund", with the amount. It may be a different amount from the one you asked for. Your booking stays as it is.
- **Decline it**: you get an email, "*Organization* declined your refund request", and you can read their reason in the conversation. You can reply there to talk it through.

## When the organization cancels or sends a refund

- **The organization cancels the activity or your booking.** Your booking is cancelled and you get an email, "Your registration for *activity* was cancelled". Any refund goes back to the card you paid with. An organization can also cancel a booking without a refund.
- **The organization sends money back without cancelling.** Your booking stays. You get an email, "*Organization* sent you a refund", with the amount, and a note about it appears in your conversation with them.
- **One date of a class moves or is cancelled.** You choose between credit and a refund. See [When a booked date moves or is cancelled](/docs/families/money/class-changes-and-credits).

## How long a refund takes

A card refund usually reaches your card within 5–10 business days. When you cancel and money comes back, you'll see "A refund of *amount* is on its way." and get an email, "Your refund for *activity* is on the way".

If a refund from an organization fails, Famlo tells you in your notifications: "Your *amount* refund from *organization* didn't go through". The money hasn't left the organization, they've been told, and they can send it again.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't find Cancel registration.** It shows only before the activity starts, and only on bookings you made yourself. After the start, use **Request a refund**. For a shop purchase or a payment request, message the organization.

**I cancelled but got less back than I paid.** The refund follows the organization's terms, and the cancel screen shows each part. Money paid with credit or a scholarship award goes back there, not to your card.

**My refund hasn't arrived.** Allow 10 business days, then check with your bank. If it's still missing, message the organization in **Messages**, or open a ticket at famlo.co/support. See [Getting help](/docs/account/support).

**The organization declined my request and I disagree.** Reply in your conversation with them first. If you can't sort it out together, open a ticket at famlo.co/support with the activity name and date.
