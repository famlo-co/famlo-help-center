# Scholarships from an organization

Some organizations on Famlo set money aside to help families pay for their classes. If one awards your family a scholarship, the amount comes off your checkout automatically at that organization, so you don't need a code or a form. You can also ask an organization for help, if it's taking requests.

This page is about an organization's own scholarship fund. Paying with a state program such as Step Up or an education savings account works differently: see [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).

## What an award is

An award is money off, given by the organization from its own scholarship fund. It is set up in one of two ways:

- **For one class.** It only comes off that class.
- **For any class at the organization.** It comes off whichever of their classes you book next, until it runs out.

Every award belongs to the organization that gave it. It can't be spent anywhere else on Famlo. Some awards have an expiry date, and some don't.

## How you hear about it

When an organization awards you a scholarship, it can email you. The subject reads "You've been awarded a $… scholarship" and the email comes in the organization's name. It says which class the award is for (or "any class at" the organization), the date it runs out if there is one, and any note the organization wrote. Its button, **See the class** or **See classes**, takes you to what you can spend it on.

The amount is private. As the email says, "Only you and" the organization can see it.

There's nothing to accept or turn on. The award is ready to use as soon as it's made.

## Where to see your balance

Open the avatar menu and choose **Payments & invoices** (famlo.co/family/payments). If you hold an award, a **Your scholarships** card sits at the top.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/scholarships-your-scholarships.webp" alt="The Your scholarships card showing the fund and organization name, an expiry date, the amount left of the award, and a Where it went list" url="famlo.co/family/payments" width={908} height={364} />

Each award shows:

- the fund's name and the organization;
- **Expires** with a date, or **No expiry**;
- how much is **left of** the full amount, with a bar showing how much is used;
- what it can be used on;
- **Where it went**: the date it was awarded, each class it paid toward, and anything that came back when a booking was cancelled.

If the organization has its own website, the same card appears in your **Parent portal** there, showing only that organization's awards. See [Your Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal).

## Using it when you book

You don't need to do anything different. Book the class as usual. See [Registering and checking out](/docs/families/registering).

**On the class page.** If your award can be used on a class, the class page shows a green note that only you can see. It shows the regular price crossed out, your price with "for you", and either "Your scholarship covers it" or how much is off. Other families see the regular price.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/scholarships-class-note.webp" alt="A class page note showing the regular price crossed out, $50.00 for you, and $100.00 off with your scholarship" url="famlo.co/activities/chess-club" width={346} height={166} />

**At checkout.** Under **What it costs**, the award appears as its own green line named after the fund (1). Below the **Order total**, the **Use my scholarship on this class** box is ticked (2). It shows how much is available and how much this order uses, and **Use it by** a date if the award expires.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/scholarships-checkout.webp" alt="The checkout's What it costs section with a scholarship line taking $100.00 off, the booking fee, the order total, and the ticked Use my scholarship on this class box" url="famlo.co/checkout/chess-club" width={519} height={452} />

1. Check the scholarship line under **What it costs** (1).
2. To save the award for a different class, untick **Use my scholarship on this class** (2). The line disappears and the full price comes back.
3. Finish checkout as usual. If the award covers everything, you'll see "No card needed. Your seat is reserved the moment you confirm."

Afterwards, your confirmation page and email show how much the scholarship covered and how much is left for another class.

**If you hold more than one award** from the same organization, the one that expires soonest is used first.

**Other discounts.** A promo code comes off first, then the organization's scholarship award, then any account credit you have with that organization. Your card pays the rest.

## No booking fee on the scholarship part

Paid bookings include a booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus the cost of processing your payment. The fee is worked out only on what's charged to your card, after any promo code, scholarship award or credit. If your award covers the whole class, there's no booking fee at all. See [What families pay](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid).

## When an award stops working

An award stops coming off at checkout when:

- it's used up;
- its expiry date has passed (it works through the end of that day);
- the organization closes the fund it came from, or its Famlo plan no longer includes scholarships.

In the last case the award isn't deleted. It works again if the organization turns scholarships back on.

## If you cancel

Scholarship money never went through your card, so it doesn't come back to your card. Instead, when a booking is cancelled, the part your award paid goes back onto your award, ready for another class at that organization. This happens whether you cancel or the organization does. Any card money comes back under the organization's refund terms, as usual. See [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).

On the **Cancel booking** screen, **Total refund** shows only card money. If your award covered the whole booking, it shows $0.00 and "No card payment to refund." That's expected. The **Where it went** list on your **Your scholarships** card then shows the amount returned.

If the award has expired by the time it comes back, it shows on your card but can't be spent.

## Asking for help paying

Some organizations let families ask for a scholarship. When an organization is taking requests, you'll see **Need help paying? Request a scholarship** on its Famlo page and on its class pages. It only appears when the organization has switched requests on and has a scholarship fund open.

1. Select **Request a scholarship**. Sign in if you're asked to.
2. Under **For which children?**, tick each child the request is for.
3. Under **Which classes? (optional)**, tick any classes you have in mind.
4. In the **Anything you'd like [organization] to know** box, say a little about what would help (up to 1,000 characters). Only the organization reads it.
5. Select **Send my request**.

You'll see "Sent to" and the organization's name, then "They'll email you if they award a scholarship." There's no amount to fill in: the organization decides how much.

You can have one open request with each organization. If you open the form again before they decide, it shows your request with **Update my request** instead, so you can change it.

The organization may award you a scholarship or decline. It doesn't send an email when it declines, so if you haven't heard back, message it. See [Messages](/docs/families/messages).

**On an organization's own website.** The same link appears on the website's **Classes** page and class pages, and opens the same form on that site. You sign in with your Famlo account, and the request goes to the same place.

## Troubleshooting

**My award didn't come off at checkout.** Check that you're booking with the organization that gave it, and that the award isn't tied to a different class. Open **Your scholarships** to see whether it has expired or is used up. Then check that **Use my scholarship on this class** is ticked.

**I was told I had a scholarship but there's no email.** The organization can award without emailing you. Look for the **Your scholarships** card on **Payments & invoices**.

**I don't see "Need help paying?"** The organization isn't taking scholarship requests right now. You can still message it to ask.

**I cancelled and nothing came back to my card.** If your award covered the booking, nothing was charged to your card. The amount went back onto your award instead.
