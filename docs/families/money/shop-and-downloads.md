# Buying from a shop, and your downloads

Some organizations sell guides, printables, videos and bundles from a shop. You buy them with the same Famlo account and checkout you use for classes, and they stay in your account under **Library & resources**, ready to download or watch any time.

## Find an organization's shop

An organization's shop is on its own website: select **Shop** in the website's menu. The menu shows **Shop** only while the organization has something on sale. Organizations can also share a direct link to any one item.

The shop page is headed "Guides, kits and workshops". Where they apply, filters at the top narrow it to **Downloads**, **Videos** or **Free for members**. Each card shows the price, with "Free" for free items, and a **Get it** or **Buy** button. Open a card to see the item's page: what it is, and for a bundle, what's in it.

## Buy or get an item

1. Open **Shop** in the organization's website menu (1).
2. Select **Buy** on a paid item, or **Get it** on a free one (2). You can also open the item first and use the button on its page.
3. Sign in with your Famlo account if you aren't already.
4. Under **Who is this for?**, leave **Just us (the family)**, or pick a child. One copy covers your whole family.
5. For a paid item, choose how to pay and select **Pay $… today** (**Confirm order** when nothing is charged to a card today). For a free item, select **Get it**.

On an organization's website, checkout stays on that website. It is the same checkout as for classes, so promo codes, saved cards and account credit work the same way. See [Paying at checkout](/docs/families/money/paying-at-checkout).

When the order goes through, the confirmation shows **Your order** with **Open it in your parent portal**. If your payment is still going through, it says "It will be in your parent portal as soon as the payment goes through."

You also get an email from the organization titled "It's yours:" followed by the item's name, with the same **Open it in your parent portal** button. The files aren't attached to the email. They stay in your account, so sign in with the same account you bought with.

### The booking fee

Paid items include a booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79, plus the cost of processing your payment. It's already in the price you see in the shop, and some organizations pay it for you. Free items have no fee. See [What families pay](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid).

### Paying with Step Up

Some items can be paid with a Step Up scholarship. Those show "Paying with a Step Up scholarship? Name the child it's for, then choose it in the next step." Pick the child under **Who is this for?**, then choose your scholarship when checkout asks who pays. Scholarship bookings carry no booking fee. See [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).

## Where your items live

Everything you have from a shop is listed under **Library & resources**:

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Your **Dashboard** on famlo.co (**Home** on a phone) | Items from every organization, each with the organization's name. The card appears once you have something. |
| An organization's **Parent portal**, on the **Overview** tab | Only that organization's items, plus **Browse the shop** when it sells anything. |

Each item shows what it is and how you got it: "Bought" with the date, "In a bundle you bought", or "Included with membership". The same item opens from either place.

## Download or watch

In **Library & resources**, select **Download** on an item, or **Watch** on a video. An item with a single file downloads straight away. One with several files opens the item's page, where **Your downloads** lists each file with its own **Download** button (1).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/shop-and-downloads-item.webp" alt="The Field Trip Journal page from Sunnyside Learning Co., with Your downloads listing two PDF files and the first Download button highlighted as 1" url="famlo.co/account/resources/field-trip-journal-8u6n45b157" width={1280} height={800} />

- **Download links last 5 minutes.** Each time you select **Download**, Famlo makes a fresh private link. A link you copied or forwarded stops working after 5 minutes. Save the file to your device, and come back to download it again whenever you need to.
- **Videos** play on the item's page. If the organization hosts the video somewhere else, the page says so ("… hosts this video. It opens in a new tab.") and **Watch** opens it in a new tab.
- **Bundles** are listed as the items inside them. A bundle's page shows **In this bundle**, with **Open** or **Watch** for each item.
- **If the organization stops selling an item** you bought, you keep it.

## Items that are free for members

Some items are marked "Free for" followed by the organization's name and "members". If you're a member, they appear in your **Library & resources** as "Included with membership" without buying anything. On the item's page, select **Get it free** to open your copy.

If you aren't a member, **Get it free** shows "This is free for … members, and it isn't in your account yet." You can buy it on its own with **Buy for $…**, or select **About membership** to join. See [Your memberships](/docs/families/memberships).

Items included with membership last as long as your membership. If your membership ends, or the organization stops offering the item, it leaves your library. Items you bought outright stay.

## Refunds

You can't cancel a shop purchase yourself. To ask for your money back, message the organization (see [Messages](/docs/families/messages)). The organization decides and sends any refund.

If the organization refunds the full price, the item is removed from your account. A partial refund leaves it in place. Refunds reach your original card in 5–10 business days.

Shop purchases are listed in **Payments & invoices** with your other payments, where you can open each one for its receipt. See [Payments & invoices and your receipts](/docs/families/money/payments-and-receipts).

## Buying on a phone

Buy paid items in your phone's web browser, on the organization's website or famlo.co. Free items work everywhere.

## Troubleshooting

**I selected Buy, and it took me to my copy instead.**
You already have it, from an earlier purchase, a bundle, or your membership. Famlo shows "It's already yours." and opens your copy, so you're never charged twice.

**The page says "This isn't in your account."**
The item belongs to a different Famlo account. The page says: "If you bought it with a different email, sign in with that one."

**A download link doesn't work.**
Links last 5 minutes. Go back to **Library & resources** or the item's page and select **Download** again.

**An item disappeared from my library.**
It was refunded in full, or it came with a membership that has ended. If neither applies, message the organization.

**I can't find Library & resources on my Dashboard.**
The card appears only once you have at least one item. Check you're signed in with the account you bought with.
