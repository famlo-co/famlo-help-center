# Payments & invoices and your receipts

**Payments & invoices** is one list of everything you've paid for on Famlo:
activities, memberships and scholarship invoices, at every organization. Open
any line to see what it covered and to get its receipt. You need to be signed
in, and it shows payments made from your own Famlo account.

## Open Payments & invoices

On a computer, use either of these:

- Select your picture at the top right, then **Payments & invoices**.
- Select **Family** at the top of the page. In the left column, under
  **Money**, select **Payments & invoices**.

On a phone, tap **Family** in the bar at the bottom, then
**Payments & invoices** (1). It's also in your account, under
**Your family**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/payments-and-receipts-phone.webp" alt="Jordan's family on a phone, with the Payments & invoices link below the children highlighted as 1" url="famlo.co/family" width={390} height={844} />

The address is famlo.co/family/payments. A number next to the link means
you have scholarship invoices waiting on you (see
[Scholarship invoices](#scholarship-invoices) below).

## Find a payment

The page opens on the most recent year that has payments in it, newest
first, grouped by month. Each month's heading shows how many payments it
holds and how much went on a card.

1. Choose what to show with the tabs (1): **All**, **Bookings**,
   **Memberships** or **Scholarship**. A tab only appears when you have
   something of that kind.
2. If you have payments in more than one year, select a year beside the
   tabs.
3. Type in **Search by activity, organization or child** (2) and press
   Enter. The search also matches how you paid and the status.
4. To narrow it further, pick a month in **All months** (3) or a status in
   **Any status** (4). They only offer months and statuses that are in the
   list.
5. Select **Clear** to remove the search and filters. The tab and year stay.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/payments-and-receipts-ledger.webp" alt="The Payments & invoices page with the All, Bookings and Memberships tabs as 1, the search box as 2, All months as 3 and Any status as 4, above a table of payments grouped by month" url="famlo.co/family/payments" width={1280} height={800} />

With no search or filters, the foot of the list shows **Paid by card in**
the year, with the total. That total counts only card money that wasn't
refunded, booking fees included.

## What the columns mean

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | When it was paid, or when the invoice was issued. |
| **Description** | The activity, membership or invoice, with the organization underneath. |
| **For** | The children it was for. |
| **Paid with** | Card, the organization's own scholarship, or your scholarship program's name. |
| **Status** | Where it stands. See the tables below. |
| **Amount** | What you paid after any promo code, scholarship award or account credit, including the booking fee when there is one. Money refunded to you is taken off. A fully refunded line is crossed out and shows what was charged. |

On a phone the table keeps the description, status and amount, and shows the
date under the description.

Card amounts on this page include the booking fee, so they match what was
charged to your card. Open a payment to see the fee on its own line. For how
the booking fee works, see
[What families pay](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid).

### Bookings

A booking is anything you paid for through checkout: an activity, an
appointment, a shop purchase or a payment request. A booking for several
children is one line.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | Charged to your card. |
| **Covered by scholarship** | The organization's own scholarship paid all of it, so nothing went on your card. |
| **Scholarship pending** | Your scholarship program is paying, and it hasn't paid yet. |
| **Paid by scholarship** | Your scholarship program has paid. |
| **Partly refunded** | Part of the order was refunded, for example one child's place, or part of the money was sent back on a booking you kept. |
| **Refunded** | The whole order was refunded. |

If you paid for one order two ways, say one child by card and one with a
scholarship program, it shows as two lines, one for each way you paid.

### Memberships

Each membership dues charge is its own line, with the period it covers.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | The dues for that period are paid. |
| **Due** | The invoice is open and still to be paid. |
| **Void** | The invoice was canceled. Its amount is crossed out. |
| **Uncollectible** | The invoice was marked as one that won't be collected. |

### Scholarship invoices

When a scholarship program such as Step Up or an ESA pays for a booking,
Famlo issues an invoice for it. The invoice line stands in for those
bookings, so they aren't listed twice. The status tells you whose move it is.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Ready to submit** | Your turn: send the invoice to your program. |
| **Reserve funds** | Your turn: reserve the funds for the organization in your program's portal. |
| **Submitted** or **Funds reserved** | You've marked your step done. Now it's up to the program. |
| **Paid by program** | The program has paid the organization. |
| **Void** | The invoice was canceled. |

The **Scholarship** tab shows how many invoices are waiting on you. For the
steps, see [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).

## Open a payment for its receipt

Select any line, and its details open right under it. Select it again to
close it. Select the title to open the payment on a page of its own.

For a booking, **Order** lists each child's place and what it cost (a
refunded place is crossed out), any promo code, scholarship award or account
credit taken off, and the **Booking fee** when there is one. For a card
payment, the total is **Paid by card**. If anything came back, **Refunded**
shows how much, and **Kept** shows what stayed on your card. When a
scholarship program paid instead, the total reads **Covered by scholarship**.
**Details** shows how you paid, the date it was paid, any refund date, and
the organization you paid.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/payments-and-receipts-booking-detail.webp" alt="A Robotics Club booking opened in the list, showing Order (Max, $550.00) and Details (Paid with Card, Paid September 28, 2026, Paid to Sunnyside Learning Co.)" url="famlo.co/family/payments" width={874} height={604} />

Depending on how you paid, a booking also has these buttons:

- **View Stripe receipt** opens the card receipt in a new tab.
- **Open scholarship invoice** appears instead when a scholarship program
  is paying.
- **Receipt (PDF)** downloads a receipt for a card payment, for your records
  or a reimbursement request. When an order covers more than one child,
  there's one for each child, labeled with the child's name, for example
  "Max's receipt (PDF)".
- When the organization accepts Step Up but isn't paid through EMA directly,
  a **Copy into EMA** panel below the details lists the fields Step Up's
  reimbursement form asks for, each with a copy button. See
  [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).
- **View activity** opens the activity's page.

For a membership invoice, you see the dues, the period and how they were
paid. Select **Pay now** to pay a **Due** invoice, or **View Stripe invoice**
to see a paid one. **Manage membership** opens
[your memberships](/docs/families/memberships).

For a scholarship invoice, you see what it covers and the total, when it
was issued, when you did your step, and when the program paid. Select
**Open invoice** to see it, or **Download PDF** to save it.

### Each payment has its own link

Every payment's page has its own address, so you can bookmark it and come
back to it later. The link only opens when you're signed in to the Famlo
account that paid. To give a receipt to someone else, such as a spouse or a
scholarship reviewer, download the PDF and send that.

## Receipts in your email

When a card payment goes through, the confirmation email has your receipt
attached as a PDF, one for each child. This applies to activities, shop
purchases, appointments and payment requests. When a scholarship program is
paying, the email also has the scholarship invoice attached, with a link to
open it.

## What isn't listed here

- Free activities and free RSVPs. Only bookings that cost something appear.
- A checkout you didn't finish.
- A booking you canceled before anything was charged.
- Payments made from another person's Famlo account, even for your
  children. They appear in that person's **Payments & invoices**.

## Your scholarships card

If an organization has given your family a scholarship from its own funds,
a **Your scholarships** card sits at the top of the page. It shows how much
is left, when it expires, and where it went. See
[Scholarships from an organization](/docs/families/scholarships).

## Your saved cards

Saved cards live in a different place: select your picture, then
**Account settings**, then **Payment methods**. A card saved there works at
every organization on Famlo. To add, change or remove one, see
[How do I update my payment method?](/docs/how-to/update-payment)

## On an organization's own website

If an organization has its own website, its **Parent portal** has a
**Payments & receipts** tab. It lists only what you paid that organization,
with **Year**, **Status** and **Paid with** filters, and links back here
with **See payments everywhere on Famlo**. See
[Your Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal).

## Troubleshooting

**I paid, but I don't see the payment.**
Check the year and the tab first, then select **Clear**. If you still don't
see it, make sure you're signed in to the account you booked with. Free
bookings never appear here.

**The amount here doesn't match my card statement.**
The amount includes the booking fee and takes off anything refunded to you.
Open the payment to see **Paid by card**, **Refunded** and **Kept**, or
select **View Stripe receipt** for the original charge.

**I need a receipt for a reimbursement.**
Open the booking and select **Receipt (PDF)**, or your child's
receipt if there are several. The same PDF is attached to your
confirmation email.

**I want money back.**
Refunds are handled from the booking, not from this page. See
[Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).
