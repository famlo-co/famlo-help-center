# Booking and buying on an organization's website

On an organization's own website you can find its classes, register, book its
services and buy from its shop without leaving the site. You use your free
Famlo account and the same checkout as on famlo.co, so prices, payment and
refund terms work the same way.

## Find a class

Select **Classes** in the website's menu (the organization may give it another
name). The page lists the organization's upcoming classes as cards, each with
its days and time, ages, place, price, seats left and a **Register** button.

To narrow the list:

1. Use the filters at the top of the page (1). Each one starts on its "any"
   choice, such as **Any age**, **Any day** or **All subjects**. For ages,
   select **Any age**, drag the two ends of the **Ages** slider to your
   child's age range, then select **Apply**.
2. Use **Show by** (2) to group the cards differently, for example by
   **Category** or **Day**.
3. Select a class's card to open its page.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/booking-on-a-website-classes.webp" alt="The Classes page on Sunnyside Learning Co.'s website, with the age, day and subject filters highlighted as 1, Show by with Category and Day highlighted as 2, and class cards with prices and Register buttons" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/classes" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

The organization chooses which filters to offer. They can include **Ages**,
**Town**, **Day**, **Time of day**, **Starts in**,
**Subject** and **Instructor**, plus two switches, **Pays with Step Up / ESA**
and **Has open seats**. A filter only appears when the classes differ on it,
so a co-op that meets in one town has no **Town** filter.

- **Clear filters** puts the full list back.
- On a phone, select **Filters** to open them, then **Show** with the number
  of matching classes. **Clear all** resets them.
- A filtered page has its own web address, so you can copy it and send it to
  another parent.

If nothing matches, the page says "No classes match all of those yet. Try
another day or town, or see everything."

### Which classes you'll see

The organization decides where each class appears:

- Most classes show both on famlo.co and on its website.
- Some show only on its website, so you may find classes there that you
  didn't see on famlo.co.
- Some open only from a direct link the organization shares. They're not in
  the list, but the link works.
- Members-only classes never appear on the website's public pages. See
  [Members-only pages and memberships on a website](/docs/families/organization-websites/members-only-pages).

## Register for a class

A class page shows its details on the left and a booking panel beside them (at
the end of the page on a phone).

1. Check the price, the session dates and how many seats are left.
2. If the panel asks, choose a **Section**, an **Option** or **Hours**.
   Then choose how many **Children** (1) you're registering. Tick any add-ons
   you want.
3. Select **Sign In to Register** (2). If you're already signed in, it reads
   **Register**. For a free class it reads **RSVP**.
4. Sign in with your Famlo account if you're asked. You come straight back and
   go on to checkout.
5. Finish checkout. See [Registering and paying](/docs/families/registering)
   for each step.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/booking-on-a-website-class-panel.webp" alt="The booking panel on a class page: the price for all 10 sessions, 7 seats left, the session dates, the Children picker highlighted as 1, and Sign In to Register with You won't be charged yet highlighted as 2, then the Total, the refund line and Ask a question" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/classes/young-writers-workshop" width={428} height={737} dark={false} />

What the panel tells you:

- **The price** already includes the booking fee. Paid bookings include a booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a
  ticket, plus the cost of processing your payment. Some organizations pay it
  for you. See [What's free and what's paid](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid).
- **"You won't be charged yet"** and the **Total**: nothing is charged until
  you pay at checkout.
- **The refund line**, such as "Full refund any time before the first class."
  These are the organization's own terms for this class.
- **A scholarship line**, when the organization accepts scholarship funds for
  the class. You choose your program at checkout. Some organizations also show
  "Need help paying?". See [Scholarships and help paying](/docs/families/scholarships).
- **Ask a question** opens the website's contact form with the class name
  already filled in.

On a phone, a bar at the bottom of the screen keeps the price and a **Sign
In** (or **Register**) button in view.

The button is grayed out when you can't register: **This class has started**,
or **Not open for registration**.

### When a class is full

When nothing is left to book, the panel shows **Full** and the button reads
**Join waitlist**, with the note "When a seat opens, we email every family who
saved it at the same moment, and the first to register gets it." Class cards
show **Join waitlist** too.

1. Select **Join waitlist**. Sign in with your Famlo account if you're asked.
2. The page that opens, **Join the waitlist**, says the class is full. Select
   **Join the waitlist**.
3. You see "You're on the waitlist. We'll alert everyone the moment a spot
   opens — first to register gets it." The button changes to
   **You're on the waitlist**. Select it again to leave the waitlist.

The class is saved to your account, the same as joining the waitlist on
famlo.co, so it also appears on your **Saved** page there. See [Saved
activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

## Checkout stays on the website

Checkout opens on the organization's website, in its look, with the
organization's name and Famlo's at the top. It's the same checkout as on
famlo.co: the same prices, booking fee, promo codes, credit and ways to pay.

- The top of the page shows who's signed in, with **Not you?** to switch
  accounts, and **Back to the class**.
- A website only sells its own classes, services and shop items.
- If you add a child at checkout with **Add someone new**, they join your
  family everywhere, on famlo.co and on every other website.
- When you're done, **View my bookings** opens the **Bookings** tab of your
  [Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal) on that
  website, and **Back to** the organization's name returns you to its site.

Your booking also appears on famlo.co, in your **Calendar** and in **Payments
& invoices**, with everything you booked elsewhere.

### Canceling a class you booked here

Open the **Parent portal** on the website, then **Bookings**, and select
**Cancel** beside the class. The **Cancel booking** page shows your refund
before you confirm, and the organization's refund terms for the class decide
what comes back. **Cancel** shows until the class starts. You can also cancel
from **Calendar** on famlo.co. See [Your Parent
portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal#cancel-a-class-booking)
and [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).

## Book a service or appointment

If the organization offers one-on-one services, its menu shows **Services**.
Each service card shows how long it takes, how it happens (in person, Zoom or
by email), the price, and one button:

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Book** | Pick a day and a time on the website, choose the children, then select **Continue to payment**. Checkout stays on the website. You get a confirmation and a calendar invite. |
| **Request** | The service is by request, or has no open times right now. **Request** opens famlo.co, where you send the organization your request. |

The line under the price says when it's next open ("Next open: Mon, Oct 12"),
"By request", or "By email, no time to pick".

Booked appointments appear in your Parent portal, under **Bookings**. For
moving or canceling one, and the time limits for changes, see [Booking a
service or appointment](/docs/families/booking/services-and-appointments).

## Buy from the shop

If the organization sells guides, printables or videos, its menu shows
**Shop**. Select **Buy** or, for a free item, **Get it**. You pay through the
same checkout, on the website.

What you buy stays in your account. On the website, it's in your Parent
portal under **Library & resources**, ready to download or watch any time. See
[Buying from a shop, and your downloads](/docs/families/money/shop-and-downloads).

## Emails from the organization

When you book on an organization's website, your confirmation email can come
in the organization's name and colors instead of Famlo's. Its **View your
booking** button opens your bookings in that website's Parent portal. Not every
organization's website sends it this way; otherwise you get Famlo's usual
confirmation.

If you book the same organization's class on famlo.co instead, you get Famlo's
usual confirmation. Reminders and other emails from the organization can also
come in its name. See [Contacting an organization, and emails from its
website](/docs/families/organization-websites/messages-and-emails).

## Reserve on Famlo buttons on other websites

Some organizations have their own website somewhere else, such as Wix,
Squarespace or WordPress, and add a Famlo button to it. The button reads
**Reserve on Famlo**, **Check out on Famlo** or **Register on Famlo**, and
sometimes shows the price. Organizations can also share a short famlo.co/r
link that does the same thing.

- **When the class is open**, the button opens checkout in a new tab. Sign in
  first if you're asked. If the organization also has a website run on Famlo,
  checkout opens there; otherwise it opens on famlo.co.
- **When the class is full**, the button reads **Join the waitlist** and opens
  the class's page on famlo.co, where you can join the waitlist.
- **When registration has closed**, the button reads **Registration closed**
  and can't be selected.
- **If the class isn't open yet**, the link opens the organization's page on
  famlo.co with "That class isn't open for registration yet."

When you've finished checkout, close the tab to go back to the organization's
page.

## Troubleshooting

**I'm signed in, but the class page still says Sign In to Register.** Select
it anyway. You come straight back signed in and go on to checkout. Each
organization's website signs you in separately, even when you're signed in on
famlo.co. See [Organizations with their own website](/docs/families/organization-websites/overview).

**I can't find a class the organization told me about.** It may open only from
a direct link, or be for members only. Ask the organization for the link, or
check whether you need to be a member.

**My booking isn't in this website's Parent portal.** The portal shows only
what you booked with that organization. Bookings with other organizations are
in **Calendar** on famlo.co. If you signed in with a different email, sign out
and sign in with the account you booked with.

**I selected Request on a service and ended up on famlo.co.** That's expected.
Requests are sent from famlo.co, with the same account.
