# Organizations with their own website

Some organizations have their own website, on their own web address, where you
can see their classes, book and pay. Famlo runs these websites, so you use the
same free Famlo account there as on famlo.co. This page explains how to spot one,
how signing in works, and what you still do on famlo.co.

## How to recognize one

An organization's own website looks like the organization: its name, logo,
colors and menu. Look at the very bottom of any page. A website run on Famlo
says "Bookings and accounts powered by Famlo. Your Famlo account works at every
hub."

Every page also has a **Parent portal** button at the right of the menu. That
is your own area on the website: your children, and your bookings and payments
with that organization. See [Your Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal).

## One Famlo account everywhere

You don't create a separate account for each website. The account you use on
famlo.co works on every organization website run on Famlo, and on famlo.co
itself. If you don't have one yet, you can create it, free, when you first sign
in on a website.

What you share across them:

- **Your children.** They're the same children everywhere. Adding a child or
  editing their details on one website changes them on famlo.co and on every
  other website too.
- **Your bookings and payments.** A website shows only what you booked or paid
  with that organization. famlo.co shows everything, from every organization.

## Signing in on an organization's website

1. On the organization's website, select **Parent portal** (1), or **Sign in**
   if the website shows it.
2. If you're already signed in to famlo.co in this browser, you come straight
   back signed in. There's nothing to type.
3. If you're not, a famlo.co sign-in page opens, showing the organization's
   name and "Sign in with your Famlo account. Your Famlo account works at every
   hub." Sign in the way you usually do on famlo.co, for example with a code
   by email, your password or a passkey.
4. You go back to the website, to the page you were on or to your **Parent
   portal**.

The sign-in page is famlo.co's own, wearing the organization's name and colors,
with a **← Back to** link to the website if you change your mind.

Pages that need you signed in, such as checkout or a members-only page, take you
through the same sign-in first, then on to where you were going.

### Each website signs you in on its own

Signing in on one organization's website doesn't sign you in on another. Each
website does its own quick sign-in the first time you need it. While you're
signed in to famlo.co in that browser, this happens without you typing anything.

### Signing out

- **Sign out** is at the top right of the **Parent portal**'s **Overview**. Signing out on an
  organization's website also signs you out of famlo.co in this browser, then
  takes you back to the website's home page.
- Signing out of famlo.co also signs you out of every organization website you
  signed in to from that browser.

## What you still do on famlo.co

The website handles booking, paying and your children's details. A few things
live only on famlo.co, and the website's links take you there:

| To do this | Go to famlo.co |
| --- | --- |
| Cancel a class you booked | **Calendar**. See [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds). |
| Remove a child from your family | **Family**. See [Your family and children](/docs/families/your-family). |
| Print a transcript, hours log or other record document | Your child's school record. See [Transcripts, diplomas and other documents](/docs/families/school-records/transcripts-and-documents). |
| Change your card, fix a failed dues payment or cancel a membership | **Account settings** › **Memberships**. See [Your memberships](/docs/families/memberships). |
| Change your password, notifications or other account settings | **Account settings**. See [Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security). |
| Ask for a service by request (the **Request** button) | The request opens on famlo.co. See [Booking a service or appointment](/docs/families/booking/services-and-appointments). |

## When a website is "taking a short break"

Sometimes an organization's website is offline for a while. Every public page
then shows the organization's name and "This website is taking a short break",
with two buttons:

- **See classes on Famlo** opens the organization's page on famlo.co, where you
  can still see its classes and book.
- **Already a member? Sign in** still works. Your **Parent portal**, your
  children's details, your bookings and payments, and checkout keep working
  while the rest of the site is paused.

Nothing you booked is affected. Everything is also on famlo.co.

## Troubleshooting

**I see "That sign-in link has expired".** Sign-in links work once, for a
minute. This also shows if the sign-in started in a different browser or on
another website. Select **Sign in** and start again. It only takes a second.

**I see "Sign-in is paused on this site".** The website's address isn't
connected to Famlo right now, so signing in there is turned off. Select **Sign
in at famlo.co**. Your account and bookings are all there. The website's public
pages keep working.

**I'm signed in on famlo.co, but the website shows me signed out.** Each website
signs you in separately. Select **Parent portal** and you'll come straight back
signed in.

**I signed out on the website and now famlo.co has signed me out too.** That's
expected: signing out on an organization's website signs you out of Famlo in
that browser. Sign in again on whichever one you need.

**The buttons say "Booking is turned off on the demo".** You're on [demo.famlo.co](https://demo.famlo.co),
Famlo's made-up example website. Nothing there can be booked. Find a real
organization on famlo.co instead.
