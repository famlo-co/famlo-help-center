# Contacting an organization, and emails from its website

You can write to an organization from its own website without a Famlo account,
and its replies come to you by email. This page also covers the inbox bell for
signed-in families, the organization's email list, the emails it sends in its
own name, and the cookie and language choices you may see on its website.

## Send the organization a message

The contact form needs no account and no sign-in. You can find it on the
organization's contact page, and some organizations also put it on other pages.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/messages-and-emails-contact.webp" alt="Sunnyside Learning Co.'s contact page, headed Send Sunnyside Learning Co. a message, with the note We'll reply by email. No account needed., the First name, Email and What can we help you with? fields, and the Send button highlighted as 1" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/contact" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

1. Open the contact form on the organization's website.
2. Type your name in **First name**.
3. Type the address you want the reply sent to in **Email**.
4. Write your message in **What can we help you with?** (up to 5,000 characters).
5. Select **Send** (1).

The form changes to "Thanks, [your name]. Your message is on its way.", with
the address the organization will reply to.

Two shortcuts start the message for you:

- **Ask a question** on a class page opens the form with the class name already typed.
- The message button on a teacher's page opens the form with "About [first name]:" already typed.

If you write again later from the same email address, your new message joins
the same conversation, so the organization sees everything in one place.

## Read and answer the reply

When the organization answers, you get an email called "[Organization]
replied to your message". The reply is in the email itself.

1. Open the email and select **Reply to [Organization]**.
2. The organization's website opens on **Your messages**, with the whole conversation between you and the organization.
3. Type in **Write back**, then select **Send**.

You don't need an account for any of this. Keep the email, because its button
is how you get back to the conversation.

If the organization closes the conversation, the page says "This conversation
is closed. To write again, use the contact form."

**If you already have a Famlo account.** A message sent through the contact
form stays an email conversation. The replies come by email, not to Famlo
**Messages**. To keep everything in Famlo **Messages**, sign in and use the
bell instead (see the next section).

**If you create a Famlo account later** with the same email address, the
conversation moves into your Famlo **Messages** once you confirm that address.
After that, the **Reply to [Organization]** link in the old emails stops
working, so use **Messages** to carry on.

## Messages on the website when you're signed in

Once you're signed in on the organization's website, a bell appears in its
header beside **Parent portal**. It shows how many messages you haven't read.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/messages-and-emails-inbox.webp" alt="The Inbox on Sunnyside Learning Co.'s website, with the bell in the header highlighted as 1 and the new-message button beside Inbox highlighted as 2, and a conversation with Sunnyside Learning Co. open showing a payment request" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/messages" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

1. Select the bell (1) to open your **Inbox** on the website.
2. To write to the organization, select the pencil button (2) beside **Inbox**.
3. Type your message and send it.

This inbox holds the same conversations you have with this organization on
famlo.co, including its announcements and payment requests. It shows only this
organization, never your other organizations. Reading a message here marks it
read on famlo.co too.

To choose which emails Famlo sends you, go to famlo.co: **Account settings** ›
**Notifications**. That page isn't on the organization's website. For
everything else about **Messages**, see [Messages](/docs/families/messages).

## Join the organization's email list

Many organization websites have an email signup, often near the bottom of the
page. The heading is the organization's own, and the note under the field reads
"Emails from [Organization]. Unsubscribe any time."

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/messages-and-emails-signup.webp" alt="An email signup on Sunnyside Learning Co.'s website, headed Hear when new classes open, with an Email field, a Subscribe button and the note Emails from Sunnyside Learning Co. Unsubscribe any time." url="www.sunnysidelearning.example" width={1272} height={287} dark={false} />

1. Type your address in **Email** and select **Subscribe** (on some websites the button says **Sign up**).
2. The form says "Check your email. We sent a link to confirm. Nothing is added until you click it."
3. Open the email "Confirm you want emails from [Organization]" and select **Yes, email me**.
4. On the page that opens, select **Yes, email me** again.
5. The page says "You're on the list."

This list belongs to the organization, not to Famlo. It doesn't change your
Famlo notifications, and every email the organization sends to the list has a
link to unsubscribe. If you didn't sign up, ignore the confirmation email and
nothing is added.

## Emails sent in the organization's name

When an organization's website is live on its own address, some emails can
come from the organization's name and wear its logo and colors. Famlo appears
only at the foot, as "Powered by Famlo". Not every organization's website sends
all of these in its own name, so some may still come from Famlo.

| Email | When you get it | Button |
|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | You booked on the organization's website | **View your booking** |
| Reminder | The day before a class you booked | **See class details** |
| "A spot opened for [class]" | A full class you saved has a free spot | **Register now** |
| "[Organization] replied to your question" (or "replied about [class]") | The organization answered you in **Messages** | **Open your messages** |
| "[Organization] replied to your message" | The organization answered your contact form | **Reply to [Organization]** |
| "Confirm you want emails from [Organization]" | You signed up for its email list | **Yes, email me** |
| "How was [class]?" | The organization asks for a testimonial | **Share your experience** |
| "You've been awarded a … scholarship" | The organization gave you one of its scholarships | **See the class** or **See classes** |

If you book the same class on famlo.co, the confirmation comes from Famlo as
usual.

**Pressing Reply.** When the organization has a contact email on its website,
replying to one of these emails writes to that address, so your answer goes
to the organization, not to Famlo.

**Opening your messages.** **Open your messages** takes you to the
conversation on the organization's website.

**Stopping emails.** Reminders have an **Email preferences** link, and
spot-opened emails have **Stop these alerts**. Replies to your questions,
booking confirmations and scholarship awards always come, because they are
about something you did.

## Weather in reminders and on class cards

In-person classes on an organization's website can show a **Forecast** line,
on class cards and in the **Parent portal**. The organization chooses whether
its class cards show it.

When the organization's website shows weather, the day-before reminder can
carry it too. The subject line adds the temperature and the sky, and the email
has a forecast box with the high, the low and the chance of rain. Forecasts
come from the National Weather Service and are for in-person classes only. When
no forecast is available, the reminder arrives without one.

## The cookie choices banner

Some organizations measure how families find their website with Google
Analytics or Meta Pixel. On those websites, a **Cookie choices** box asks you
first: "[Organization] would like to use … to see how families find this site.
They set cookies. Nothing loads unless you press Accept."

- **Decline**: nothing loads.
- **Accept**: the organization's analytics load on its public pages.

The box appears only on public pages such as the home page, classes, teachers,
contact, services and the shop. It never appears in the **Parent portal**,
checkout, **Messages** or members-only pages, and nothing is loaded there.

Your choice is remembered in that browser only. To change it, select the small
**Cookie choices** button in the bottom corner of the page. If your browser
sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the website treats that as no and
doesn't show the box at all.

## Spanish pages

Some organizations offer their website in Spanish too. When they do, an
**EN | ES** switch appears in the header on pages that have a Spanish version.
The organization writes those pages itself, and only its home page and its own
pages are translated. Classes, teachers, the contact form, checkout and the
**Parent portal** stay in English.

## Troubleshooting

**I sent a message but no reply has come.** Check your spam or junk folder for
"[Organization] replied to your message", and check that you typed your email
address correctly. Organizations answer on their own schedule. If you're
signed in on the website, you can also message them from the bell.

**The form says "This form expired. Reload the page and try again."** Reload
the page, then type your message again and select **Send**.

**The reply link says "This link has expired."** The link in an older email
no longer works. Send a new message from the contact form. If you've since
created a Famlo account with that address, the conversation is in your Famlo
**Messages**.

**The confirmation link says "This link has been used up."** You've already
confirmed, or the link was replaced. To sign up again, use the email signup on
any page of the website.

**I don't see the bell.** The bell appears only when you're signed in on that
organization's website. Select **Parent portal** to sign in with your Famlo
account.

**I don't see the cookie box.** The organization may not use analytics, or your
browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, which counts as no.
