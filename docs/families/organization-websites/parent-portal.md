# Your Parent portal

The **Parent portal** is your area on an organization's own website. It shows your children, bookings, payments and membership with that organization only. You sign in with your Famlo account, so there's nothing new to set up. To open it, select **Parent portal** at the right of the website's menu.

If you haven't signed in on that website yet, see [Organizations with their own website](/docs/families/organization-websites/overview).

## Find your way around

The portal has four tabs. The overview is **Overview** (1). Your children are under **Children** (2), what you've booked is under **Bookings** (3), and what you've paid is under **Payments & receipts** (4). On a phone, the last tab reads **Payments**. Once you've written a testimonial for this organization, a fifth tab, **Testimonials**, appears.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/parent-portal-overview.webp" alt="The Parent portal on Sunnyside Learning Co.'s website, with the Overview, Children, Bookings and Payments & receipts tabs numbered 1 to 4" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/portal" width={1280} height={660} dark={false} />

Your email address shows above the **Parent portal** heading ("Signed in as …"), and **Sign out** is on the right.

## What's on the Overview

Cards only show up when there's something to put in them. On a phone they appear in this order, top to bottom:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| A **Next step** card | A scholarship invoice from this organization that's waiting on you, for example "Next step: send this to …". Select **Open invoice**. |
| A testimonial request | The organization would like to hear how a class, appointment or membership went. Select **Leave a testimonial**, or **Not now** if you'd rather they didn't ask about it again. |
| **From** *the organization* | A shortcut the organization chose, such as **See classes**. |
| **Coming up** | Your upcoming appointments here, with **Reschedule** or **Details**, and **Join Zoom** for an online appointment. |
| **This week at** *the organization* | Your classes and events here in the next seven days, with your children's names and the place. **All bookings at** *the organization* opens the **Bookings** tab. |
| **Library & resources** | Guides, downloads and videos you bought or got free from this organization's shop, each with **Download** or **Watch**. **Browse the shop** appears when the organization has a shop. |
| **Membership** | Only at organizations that charge membership dues. See [Your membership card](#your-membership-card). |
| **Member pages** | Pages on this website that you can open because you're signed in or a member. See [Member pages](#member-pages). |
| **Children** | One card per child, and **Add a child**. |
| **Your scholarships** | Scholarships this organization awarded you from its own funds: what's left, when it expires and where it went. |
| **Recent payments** | Your last three payments to this organization. **All payments & receipts** opens the full list. |

At the bottom, **See everything on Famlo** opens your family's page on famlo.co. That's where you'll find every organization you deal with, not just this one.

## Children

Your children are the same everywhere on Famlo. If you change a detail here, it changes on famlo.co and on every other organization's website too.

Each child's card shows their age and grade, and a reminder when something is missing. It might say "Add allergies", "Add an emergency contact", "Add pickup & photos", or how many details are missing. Select the reminder to go straight to that form. When nothing is missing, the card says "All set".

**To add a child:**

1. Open **Parent portal**, then **Children**.
2. Select **Add a child**.
3. Enter their **Full name**, and their birthdate and **Grade** if you like.
4. Select **Add child**.

You can add allergies, pickup and emergency details afterwards.

**To change a child's details**, select the child. From there you can:

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/parent-portal-child.webp" alt="A child's page in the Parent portal, with Edit name, birthday or grade, Health & safety and School record numbered 1 to 3" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/portal/children" width={1280} height={846} dark={false} />

- Change their name, birthday or grade with **Edit name, birthday or grade** (1), then select **Save**.
- Update their health and safety details under **Health & safety** (2). The rows are **Basics**, **Allergies**, **Special needs & accommodations**, **Medications & diet**, **Pickup & photos** and **Emergency contact**, the same ones as on famlo.co. Organizations your family is a member of, and any organization you register this child with, can see their profile, including allergies, medical notes, special needs and emergency contact. The people running the organization see it, and a teacher sees it for the classes they teach. Other parents don't.
- See their **School record** (3) for this school year. You can add courses or activities with **Add a course or activity**, and edit, hide or remove the lines you added. A line that came from a class you booked can be hidden but not removed. **Transcript**, **Hours log** and **Scholarship spending** open on famlo.co, where they print. More in [Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record).

The child's **Overview** also lists their classes with this organization.

:::note
You can't remove a child or upload a child's photo in the Parent portal. Do both from **Family** on famlo.co. See [Your family and children](/docs/families/your-family).
:::

## Bookings

The **Bookings** tab lists your appointments first. Below them, under **Coming up**, are your classes with this organization: the date and time, the class, which child is booked, the place, and how it was paid. Past bookings appear under **Past**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/parent-portal-bookings.webp" alt="The Bookings tab in the Parent portal, listing an appointment and three upcoming classes, with See bookings everywhere on Famlo numbered 1" url="www.sunnysidelearning.example/portal/bookings" width={1280} height={875} dark={false} />

There isn't a cancel button for a class here yet. For now, to cancel a class booking, select **See bookings everywhere on Famlo** (1), which opens **Calendar** on famlo.co. You can cancel from there. See [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).

You can move or cancel an appointment from its own page (**Reschedule** or **Details**), as long as you're within the organization's time limit for changes. See [Booking a service or appointment](/docs/families/booking/services-and-appointments).

## Payments & receipts

**Payments & receipts** lists every payment you've made to this organization, one year at a time. Use **Year**, **Status** and **Paid with** to narrow the list. When you've paid by card, the year's total appears beside the filters.

Select a row to see its details. Depending on the payment, these include **View Stripe receipt**, **View Stripe invoice**, **Open scholarship invoice** or **Pay now**.

To see payments to every organization, select **See payments everywhere on Famlo**. More in [Payments & invoices and your receipts](/docs/families/money/payments-and-receipts).

### Scholarship invoices

If you pay with a state scholarship program, the organization's invoice for your booking opens from the **Next step** card on the Overview, or from the payment in **Payments & receipts**. The invoice shows its progress and your step: submit the invoice to your program, or reserve the funds in the program's portal. When you've done it, mark it there so the organization knows. It's the same invoice you'd see on famlo.co. The steps for each program are in [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).

## Your membership card

At an organization that charges membership dues, the **Membership** card shows where you stand:

| Status | What it means | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Your membership is paid. The card shows the price, renewal date and the card you pay with. | **Manage membership** |
| **Covered** | The organization is covering your membership. "Nothing to pay." | **Manage membership** |
| **Payment due** | Your last renewal didn't go through. Update your card to keep your membership. | **Update your card** |
| **Not active yet** | You're on the organization's roster, but your membership isn't paid. | **Pay on Famlo** |
| **Applied** | You asked to join and the organization is reviewing it. Your card is charged only once you're approved. | **See your request** |
| **Not a member** | You haven't joined. | **See membership** |

Managing a membership, changing your card and canceling all happen on famlo.co. More in [Your memberships](/docs/families/memberships).

## Member pages

The **Member pages** card lists the pages on this website you can open, each marked **Members** or **Signed-in families**. If the organization sells membership, it also lists the members' pages you can't open yet, with **See membership**. Who can open which page is covered in [Members-only pages and memberships on a website](/docs/families/organization-websites/members-only-pages).

## Testimonials

Once you've shared a testimonial with this organization, the **Testimonials** tab shows what you wrote and where it stands: **Waiting for** *the organization*, **Published**, **Not published**, or **Private**, which means only the organization reads it. Select **Change it** to edit your words, which sends them back to the organization to review. Select **Withdraw** to take it down. **Share another** starts a new one. More in [Share your experience (testimonials)](/docs/families/community/testimonials).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see a booking, payment or child from another organization.**
The Parent portal shows one organization only. Select **See everything on Famlo** to see everything in one place.

**There's no Membership card.**
It only appears at organizations that charge membership dues.

**I can't find how to cancel a class.**
For now, class bookings are canceled from **Calendar** on famlo.co. Select **See bookings everywhere on Famlo** on the **Bookings** tab.

**The portal shows the wrong account.**
Check the "Signed in as" line on the Overview. Select **Sign out**, then sign in again with the Famlo account you booked with. Signing out on the website also signs you out of Famlo in that browser.
