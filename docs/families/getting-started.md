# Getting started as a new family

This guide takes you from no account to your first booking. It's for the
parent or guardian who sets Famlo up for the household. A Famlo account is
free, and you need only an email address you can open.

## Create your account

Signing up and logging in start on the same screen, **Log in or sign up**.
Famlo starts by emailing you a code to confirm your address. You'll also choose
a password, but you can always sign in with an emailed code instead.

1. Go to famlo.co/login, or open the menu at the top right of any page and select **Log in or sign up**.
2. Type your address in **Email** and select **Continue**.
3. Open the email "Your Famlo confirmation code is …", enter the six digits on **Confirm it's you**, and select **Continue**. The code works for 10 minutes.
4. On **Let's create your account**, fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Password** (at least 8 characters).
5. Select **Agree and continue**. This confirms you're 18 or older and agree to Famlo's Terms of Service.
6. On **Everyone belongs here**, read the Community Commitment and select **Agree and continue**.

You're signed in straight away, and Famlo shows "Welcome to Famlo! Your account
is ready." Entering the code confirms your email address, so there's no
separate verification email to open.

:::note
**Decline** on **Everyone belongs here** removes the account you just created.
It's a real choice, not a way to skip the step.
:::

Children don't get their own Famlo accounts. You add them to yours, as the next
sections show. For passkeys, Google, Facebook, Apple and other ways in, see
[Signing in and creating your account](/docs/account/signing-in).

## Your first look at Home

After you sign up, you land on Home with "Welcome to Famlo" at the top. (If you
signed up partway through booking something, Famlo takes you back there
instead.) Home is called **Dashboard** in the menu on a computer, and **Home**
on a phone's tab bar.

New accounts get a **Getting started** checklist with five steps. Each step
ticks itself off when you actually do it, wherever you do it:

| Step | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Create your account** | Already done when you arrive. |
| **Add your first child** | "Their age decides what Famlo shows you." |
| **Health & safety for organizers** | "Allergies, pickup people and an emergency contact." |
| **Start the school record** | "Log this week in one grid; classes you book fill in on their own." |
| **Find a first activity** | "Classes, co-ops and field trips near you." It closes after your first booking. |

The checklist goes away when every step is done. **Hide** removes it sooner,
and there's no way to bring it back, but every step can still be done from
**Family** and **Discover**. For everything else on Home, see
[Your Home page](/docs/families/home). For the school record step, see
[Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record).

## Add your first child

The checklist opens on this step with a short form, so you can add a child
without leaving Home.

1. Type your child's name in **First name** (1).
2. Choose their **Birth month** (2).
3. Choose their **Birth year** (3).
4. Select **Add child** (4).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/getting-started-checklist.webp" alt="The Getting started checklist on Home, 1 of 5 done, with the First name, Birth month and Birth year fields and the Add child button numbered 1 to 4" url="famlo.co/dashboard" width={776} height={629} />

You don't need an exact birth date. Famlo uses your child's age to suggest
activities that fit them, and to fill in their details when you register.

To add a brother or sister, or an adult in your household, use the full form:
select **Add several at once** under the quick form, or open **Family** and
select **+ Add family member**. The full form asks for **Full name** ("As it
should appear on a transcript, diploma or student ID."), **Relationship**
(Child, Spouse or Other) and **Birthdate**, then **Add**. You can also add a
child during checkout. [Your family and children](/docs/families/your-family)
covers both forms and everything you can edit later.

## Why Health & safety matters

Any organization your family is a member of, and any organization you book
your child with, can see your child's profile, including allergies, medical
notes, special needs and emergency contact, so whoever is in the room can keep
them safe. Organizers look for three things before your child arrives:
allergies, who may pick them up, and an emergency contact.

Select **Health & safety for organizers** on the checklist to go straight to the
first child who's missing something, or open **Family** and choose your child.
Each section saves on its own, so you can do one now and the rest later. If
your child has no allergies, tick **No known allergies**. That's a real answer,
and organizers see it.

The people who see it are the people running that organization: its owner,
co-leaders, class organizers and front desk, and a teacher for the classes they
teach. Other parents in the group don't. Allergies are also shared in general
terms with other attendees, so an activity can be kept safe.
[Your family and children](/docs/families/your-family) lists every section and
who sees it.

## What you'll pay

A Famlo account is free, and free events are free. Paid bookings include a
booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus the cost of
processing your payment. It's already in the price you see, and some
organizations pay it for you. When a booking fee applies, activity pages say
**Includes booking fee** under the price, so the number you see is the number
you pay. [What families pay](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid) explains
the fee and when there isn't one.

## Book your first activity

1. Select **Find a first activity** on the checklist, or **Discover** in the menu.
2. Open an activity that suits your child and check its dates, ages, price and refund line.
3. Select **Register** (for a paid activity) or **RSVP** (for a free one).
4. Under **Who's coming**, choose your child. If they're not listed, choose **+ Add someone new…**.
5. Work through the remaining steps: how you're paying, any questions from the organizer, and their terms.
6. Select the button that shows your total, such as **Pay $55.56 today**, or **Complete free RSVP** for a free activity.

You'll see **Booking Confirmed!** and get an email headed "You're registered for
…" with the details. The booking then shows in **Calendar**. For search and
filters, see [Finding activities](/docs/families/finding-activities). For every
checkout step, see [Registering and checking out](/docs/families/registering).

Some organizations have their own website. When you book there, you sign in
with this same Famlo account. See
[Organizations with their own website](/docs/families/organization-websites/overview).

## Emails and a passkey offer

Soon after you sign up, you get a "Welcome to Famlo!" email. It suggests three
things to try: browse activities near you, add your family members, and follow
the organizations you like. Its **Explore activities** button opens the
activity search.

On a later visit to Home or the famlo.co homepage (not the welcome visit right
after you sign up), Famlo may offer to **Set up a passkey**, so next time you
can sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. On some devices the button names the device's own method, such as Touch
ID. It appears only on a device that can use a passkey, and only once. If you'd
rather wait, select **Set it up later in settings**; you can add one any time
from [Login and security](/docs/account/login-and-security).

## Using Famlo on your phone

Famlo works in your phone's web browser. Signed in, the bar along the bottom of
the screen has five tabs: **Home**, **Discover**, **Calendar**, **Messages** and
**Family**. Account settings and the rest of the menu are under your picture at
the top right.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/getting-started-phone.webp" alt="Famlo on a phone showing Jordan's family, with the tab bar of Home, Discover, Calendar, Messages and Family at the bottom" url="famlo.co/family" width={390} height={909} />

To open Famlo from an icon like an app, add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** open famlo.co, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open famlo.co, tap the menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

The icon opens Famlo full screen. It still needs an internet connection, and it
doesn't send notifications of its own. Choose which emails Famlo sends you in
[Notifications](/docs/account/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

**The code never arrived.** Check your spam or junk folder, then select **Send a
new code** on **Confirm it's you**. A code lasts 10 minutes, and sending a new
one cancels the old one. If you mistyped your address, go back to **Log in or
sign up** and start again.

**I hid the checklist by mistake.** It can't be shown again, but nothing is
lost. Add children from **Family**, fill in their safety details from each
child's page, and find activities from **Discover**.

**My child isn't listed at checkout.** Under **Who's coming**, choose **+ Add
someone new…**, enter their **First name** and **Birthdate**, and select **Add
to my family**. They join your family without losing what you've already
chosen, and you can pick them for this booking.

**I want an account for my child.** Children don't have Famlo accounts. Add them
to your own account instead; you book for them and keep their details.

**Can my partner share the family?** Not today. Each adult has their own
account and their own family.
