# Saved activities and the waitlist

Tap the heart on any activity to save it for later. If the activity is full, saving it puts you on its waitlist, and Famlo tells you when a spot opens. You need a free Famlo account to save.

## Save an activity

The heart sits on every activity card and at the top of each activity page, next to **Share**. Select it once to save the activity and again to unsave it.

If you're signed out, the heart opens a box that says "Sign in to save" followed by the activity's name. Sign in or create your account right there. The activity is saved and you're back where you started.

You can only save activities. To keep up with an organization, use **Follow** on its page instead (see [Finding organizations](/docs/families/organizations)).

## Find what you've saved

Open the avatar menu and choose **Saved**. You can also use the **Saved · N** link on **Discover** and on your **Dashboard** (**Home** on a phone).

The page is headed **Saved & watchlist**. At the top, a **Location alerts** row leads to your area alerts (see [Location alerts](/docs/families/discover/location-alerts)). Below it, **Saved activities** lists everything you've saved, newest first, with the organization, date and time, and price.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/saved-and-alerts-saved-page.webp" alt="The Saved & watchlist page with three saved activities, each with a status badge, a button and a filled heart; the first heart is highlighted" url="famlo.co/watchlist" width={1280} height={800} />

Each activity has a badge that shows where things stand, and a button that matches it:

| Badge | What it means | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You're registered** | You have a confirmed booking. | **View activity** |
| **You're on the waitlist** | The activity is full. | **View activity** |
| **This activity has ended** | Its start time has passed. | None |
| "Only 3 spots left" (or another number) | Five or fewer spots remain. | **Register** |
| **Spots open** | There's room. | **Register** |

**Register** takes you straight to checkout. A repeating activity also shows how often it repeats, for example "Repeats weekly".

### Remove a saved activity

Select the filled heart at the end of its row (1). It's removed right away, with no confirmation. You can also unsave it with the heart on the activity's card or page.

Activities stay on the list after they end, usually badged **This activity has ended**, until you remove them. An activity the organizer takes down no longer appears.

### Saved organizations

Organizations you saved in the past still appear under **Saved organizations**, below your activities. Select an organization's heart to remove it. You can't add new ones.

## Join the waitlist

When an activity is full, its page shows **Join the waitlist** instead of a register button.

1. Open the full activity.
2. Select **Join the waitlist** (1).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/saved-and-alerts-join-waitlist.webp" alt="A full activity page with the Join the waitlist button highlighted and the line This event is full, be the first on the waitlist" url="famlo.co/activities/robotics-club-cape-coral-fl-z71hv9swq3" width={1280} height={800} />

Joining the waitlist is the same as saving the activity. The button changes to **You're on the waitlist**, and the activity appears on your **Saved** page with that badge. To leave the waitlist, select **You're on the waitlist** again, or unsave the activity.

The line under the button shows how many families are waiting. It doesn't count families who are already booked.

| You see | When |
| --- | --- |
| "This event is full — be the first on the waitlist." | Nobody is waiting yet. |
| "N families are already waiting." | Others are waiting and you haven't joined. |
| "N families are waiting for a spot." | You're on the waitlist. |

:::warning
The waitlist isn't a queue. Nobody holds a place in line and no spot is held for you. When a spot opens, Famlo tells everyone on the waitlist at the same moment, and the first family to register gets it. Joining early doesn't put you ahead.
:::

## When a spot opens

Famlo sends a spot-opened alert to everyone who saved a full activity whenever a spot frees up. That happens, for example, when someone cancels, when a refund frees a seat, or when the organizer adds spots.

- **In Famlo:** "A spot opened in" and the activity's name, or "N spots opened in…" when more than one is free. The number updates to what's free right now. It adds: "Everyone waiting was alerted at the same time — first to register gets it."
- **By email:** the subject is "A spot opened for" and the activity's name, with a **Register now** button. Some organizations send this email in their own branding.

The alert in Famlo links to the activity page, so you can check the details before you register.

There's a new alert for every opening, even if you didn't open the last one. If several spots open within 15 minutes, you get one alert, not one for each. Older unread spot alerts for that activity are marked read, so only the latest one shows as new.

If the spot is taken before you look, the alert in Famlo changes to say the activity "filled up again", with "It has already been taken. We'll tell you if another opens." You stay on the waitlist.

Famlo doesn't send these alerts to families who already have a booking for the activity.

## When an activity is almost full

You don't have to wait for a full activity. If something you saved starts to fill up, Famlo sends you one almost-full alert:

| Activity size | You're told when |
| --- | --- |
| 50 spots or fewer | 5 or fewer spots are left |
| More than 50 spots | 10% or fewer of the spots are left |

The alert in Famlo says the activity "is almost full", followed by how many spots are left and "secure yours before it goes." The email has a **Secure your spot** button.

You get this alert only once for each activity, and not if you've already booked it. If the activity fills before you look, the alert changes to say it "is now full", with "It filled up. We'll alert you if a spot opens." Your saved activity is now on the waitlist.

## Full dates on a drop-in class

Some weekly classes let you book a single date. At checkout, a full date is grayed out with a note such as "Thu Oct 22 is full." and a **Tell me if a spot opens** link. The link opens that date's page. If the date is still full, select **Join the waitlist** there.

## Choose how these alerts reach you

Spot-opened and almost-full alerts are part of the **Activity updates** notification category. Change how you get them in **Account settings** › **Notifications** by checking or clearing **In-app** or **Email** on the **Activity updates** row, then select **Save preferences**. That row also covers new activities from organizations you follow and matches for your location alerts. See [Notifications](/docs/account/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

**I joined the waitlist. What's my position?**
You don't have one. Everyone on the waitlist hears about an opening at the same moment, and the first to register gets the spot.

**I got a spot-opened alert, but the activity was full again when I looked.**
Another family registered first. You're still on the waitlist and will be told about the next opening.

**I'm not getting spot-opened emails.**
Check that **Email** is checked for **Activity updates** in **Account settings** › **Notifications**, and that **Receive notification emails** is turned on under **Email** on the same page. Also look in your spam folder. You won't get the alert if you already have a booking for that activity.

**I only got one almost-full alert.**
That's expected. Famlo sends it once for each activity.

**Old activities are cluttering my Saved page.**
Select the filled heart on each one to remove it. Ended activities stay until you remove them.

**I can't find a heart on an organization's page.**
Organizations can't be saved any more. Use **Follow** on the organization's page.
