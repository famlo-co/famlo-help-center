# Your child's school record

Each child in your family has a **School record**: one page per school year
listing what they learned and for how many hours. Classes you book on Famlo
fill it in on their own, overnight. You add everything else, like curriculum
at home, books and sport. It's free and there's nothing to set up.

## Open a child's school record

1. Select **Family** in the top bar (1). On a phone, tap **Family** in the bar at the bottom.
2. Select your child (2).
3. Select **School record** (3).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/school-record-page.webp" alt="Max's school record, with Family, Max and School record highlighted, four totals for the 2026–27 school year and the documents built from the record" url="famlo.co/family/members/2/record" width={1280} height={800} />

You can also get there from:

- the **School year so far** card on your **Dashboard** (**Home** on a phone). Tap a child, or select **Transcripts, diplomas & student IDs**;
- the **School record** card on your child's **Overview**, through the link **Transcript, hours log & IDs**.

The page is titled with your child's name, for example "Max's school
record", with their grade and the school year under it. Two buttons sit at
the top: **Transcript** and **Log a week**.

## What's on the page

At the top are four totals for the year:

| Total | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Hours this year** | All recorded and logged hours on the page. Hours that come only from a class schedule show underneath as "+ 6.0 scheduled" and aren't added in. |
| **Recorded by groups** | Hours on the rows from your Famlo bookings. |
| **Logged by you** | Hours on the rows you added. |
| **Credits** | The credit you've set across all rows. Until you set any credit, this shows **Subjects covered** instead: how many different subjects the year includes. |

Below them is **Documents built from this record**: the transcript, hours
log, diploma, student ID and the rest. See
[Transcripts, diplomas and other documents](/docs/families/school-records/transcripts-and-documents).

Then come two lists:

- **From your Famlo bookings**: classes Famlo added for you ("Added automatically · edit or hide any of them").
- **Added by you**: your own rows ("Work at home, books, sport, classes elsewhere").

Each row shows the course, the organization and instructor when there is
one, then the dates, sessions, hours, credit and grade, for example
"Sep 2026 – Oct 2026 · 5 of 6 sessions · 7.5 hours".

### What the badges mean

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Provider records** | Filled in from the organization's attendance or its class schedule. |
| **Instructor confirmed** | The organization confirmed your child completed the course. A line underneath says who, for example "Confirmed by …" or "Confirmed under …'s completion policy", and the date. |
| **Added by you** | A row you added yourself. |
| **Hidden** | A row you hid. It's left off your documents and totals. |

## How classes fill in the record

Once a night, Famlo updates each child's record from the classes you booked
for them on Famlo:

- **When the organization takes attendance**, sessions marked **Present** or **Late** count. **Absent** and **Excused** don't. Hours come from each session's start and end times.
- **When a class you booked and paid for has no attendance**, Famlo uses the organization's published schedule instead. It counts only meetings that have already happened, and shows those hours as "scheduled hours", kept apart from hours an organization recorded. If you cancel partway through, the count stops at the day you cancelled.
- **A weekly class is one line**, not one per meeting. A 30-week co-op class shows as a single course with its sessions counted, like "27 of 30 sessions".

The hours keep up as the term goes on. Each night they're brought up to date
with every session the organization has counted so far, including any
attendance it corrected. Once an organization takes attendance on a class that
was showing scheduled hours, the recorded hours take their place.

The nightly update never undoes your changes. If you rename a class, set a
subject or add credit, your version stays. Hours you typed in yourself stay
too, unless the organization records hours for that class.

## Add a course or activity

Use this for anything Famlo can't see: curriculum at home, reading, sport, a
class you took somewhere else. To fill in a whole week at once, use
[Log a week](/docs/families/school-records/log-a-week) instead.

1. Open your child's **School record**.
2. Under **Added by you**, select **Add a course or activity**.
3. Enter the **Course or activity**. It's the only required field.
4. Fill in what else you want to keep:
   - **Provider**: who taught it. "Leave blank for work done at home."
   - **Subject**: pick a suggestion or type your own.
   - **Grade**: "A, B+, or leave blank."
   - **Started** and **Finished**.
   - **Hours**: "Total time spent across the year."
   - **Credit**: for high school only. Leave it blank below grade 9.
   - **Notes**: "Materials used, books read, anything an evaluator or a district would want by title."
5. Select **Add to record**.

To change a row later, select **Edit** on it, then **Save**.

### Subjects

The **Subject** box suggests English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies,
Foreign Language, Fine Arts, Physical Education, Health, Technology and
Electives. Using these keeps the same subject together on the transcript, but
you can type anything.

### Credit

Famlo never fills in credit for you. When a row has hours, the **Credit** box
suggests a number at 150 hours per credit, for example
"7.5 hours → 0.05 credit at 150 hours per credit". It's only a suggestion:
type whatever your state or your own plan uses. Credit can be from 0 to 20.

## What you can change on a class from Famlo

You can edit most of a row Famlo added: its name, subject, dates, credit and
notes. Two things belong to the organization:

- **Hours the organization counted.** The form shows them with "Counted by … from their own check-in. If it is wrong, ask them to correct it — their record is what a college would verify against."
- **A grade the organization gave.** The form says "Given by …. Ask them to change it if it is wrong."

If the organization didn't record hours or a grade, those fields are yours
to fill in.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/school-record-edit-provider-row.webp" alt="Editing Math Circle: a What Sunnyside Learning Co. recorded box, editable name, subject, grade, dates and credit, and hours shown as counted by Sunnyside Learning Co." url="famlo.co/family/members/2/record_entries/1/edit" width={940} height={952} />

Your changes only affect your documents. The organization's own record
stays as it is.

## Hide or remove a row

Rows from your Famlo bookings have **Hide** (1). Rows you added have **Remove** (2).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/school-record-rows.webp" alt="The From your Famlo bookings list with Hide highlighted on Math Circle, and the Added by you list with Remove highlighted on Reading at home" url="famlo.co/family/members/2/record" width={892} height={468} />

| | **Hide** (1) | **Remove** (2) |
|---|---|---|
| Shows on | Rows from your Famlo bookings | Rows you added |
| What happens | The row stays on the page, crossed out, and is left off your documents and totals. The organization's attendance doesn't change. | The row is deleted. Famlo asks you to confirm first. |
| Undo | Select **Show** on the row. | Add it again. |

You can't remove a row from a class you booked. Hiding it keeps it off your
documents without changing what the organization recorded.

## The school year

The school year runs from August 1 to July 31, so the 2026–27 record covers
August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027. A new year starts on its own. Once your
child has entries in more than one year, buttons with each year (like
**2025–26** and **2026–27**) appear at the top so you can switch between them.

## Who sees this

The page says it plainly. For Max, it reads: "You see everything, across every provider Max
attends. Each organization sees only the record it created — never
what another organization recorded, and never what you added yourself.
Hiding a row keeps it off your documents; it does not change the attendance
a provider took."

Only children have a school record. Adults in your family don't.

If an organization you belong to has its own website, you can also see and
edit this record from its **Parent portal**. It's the same record. See
[Your Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal).

## Troubleshooting

**I booked a class, but it isn't on the record.**
Famlo updates the record overnight. A class shows up the night after its
first meeting has happened, or after the organization takes attendance. A
term you booked ahead of time won't appear until classes begin. Also check
that you picked this child when you booked: a class only goes on the record
of the child it was booked for. See [Registering and checking out](/docs/families/registering).

**A class shows no hours.**
If you see "… no recorded hours — the provider didn't log session
times", the organization took attendance but its sessions have no start and
end times. Select **Edit** on the row and add the hours yourself.

**The hours say "scheduled".**
The organization didn't take attendance, so Famlo counted the class's
published schedule. Scheduled hours are listed apart from recorded hours on
the page and on your documents.

**A booking I started isn't counting.**
A booking whose checkout wasn't finished, so it's still unpaid, doesn't
count. Check your [calendar](/docs/families/calendar) to see whether the
booking went through.

**The hours or grade from an organization are wrong.**
You can't change them yourself. Ask the organization to correct their
record. Corrected attendance reaches your child's record the next night.
