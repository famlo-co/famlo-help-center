# Transcripts, diplomas and other documents

Famlo builds printable documents from your child's school record: a
transcript, an hours log, a scholarship spending report, a school profile, an
enrollment letter, a diploma and a certificate for any one course. They're free
and they update as the record does. You print them, or save them as a PDF,
from your browser.

To fill the record itself, see [Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record)
and [Log home learning with Log a week](/docs/families/school-records/log-a-week).

## Open a document

1. Select **Family** at the top of famlo.co (on a phone, **Family** in the bottom bar).
2. Select your child, then **School record**.
3. Under **Documents built from this record**, pick a document. On a computer, the same list
   sits under your child's name in the left-hand menu.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/transcripts-documents-grid.webp" alt="Max's school record with the Documents built from this record grid: Transcript (highlighted), Hours log, Scholarship spending, School profile, Enrollment letter, Diploma and Student ID" url="famlo.co/family/members/2/record" width={1280} height={800} />

You can also reach the documents from the **Transcripts, diplomas & student IDs**
link on your **Dashboard** (**Home** on a phone).

Rows you've hidden on the school record are left out of every document and
every total. Student ID cards have their own page:
[Student ID cards and verification](/docs/families/school-records/student-id).

## Print or save as PDF

Every document has a **Print or save as PDF** button above it. It opens your
browser's print window. To get a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** (or your
browser's equivalent) as the printer. There's no separate download.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/transcripts-transcript.webp" alt="A transcript for Max showing the homeschool name, date of birth, current grade, cumulative GPA, credits earned, the 2026–27 courses with their record codes, and the Print or save as PDF button (1)" url="famlo.co/family/members/2/record/transcript" width={1280} height={900} />

Select **Print or save as PDF** (1). The menus and buttons around the document
don't print.

Most documents cover one school year. When your child has entries in more
than one year, a year switcher appears next to the button. The school year
runs from August 1 to July 31.

| Document | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Transcript** | Every year on record |
| **Hours log** | One school year |
| **Scholarship spending** | One school year |
| **School profile** | Every year on record |
| **Enrollment letter** | One school year |
| **Diploma** | Every year on record |
| **Certificate** | One course |

## What the documents carry about you

A few details come from your account and family settings, not from the record:

- **Homeschool name** names your homeschool at the top of every document. Set
  it in **Family** › **Edit** (see [Your homeschool name and school profile](#your-homeschool-name-and-school-profile)).
- **Your name** prints as the supervising parent on signature lines, and
  your city and state print in the headers. Both come from
  **Account settings** › **Personal information**
  ([Personal information](/docs/account/personal-information)). The transcript
  also prints your email and phone number once your name is filled in.
- **Your child's date of birth and grade** come from your child's details in
  **Family**.

## Transcript

The **Transcript** ("Every year, with credits and GPA. For college
applications.") is headed "Secondary school transcript". It lists every school
year on record, with no grade filter, so a high-school credit earned in 8th
grade still shows.

It shows:

- Your homeschool name, "Parent-directed homeschool", and your contact details.
- **Date of birth**, **Current grade**, **Cumulative GPA** and **Credits earned**.
- A table for each year: **Course**, **Provider & instructor**, **Credit**,
  **Grade** and **Record**.
- **Credit summary by subject**, when credits fall under two or more subjects.
- **How each record was established**, **Grading scale** and **Certification**
  ("I certify that this transcript is accurate and complete."), with a line for
  your signature and the date.

The **Record** column says where each course came from:

| Code | Meaning | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **IC** | Instructor confirmed | The named instructor at the organization confirmed attendance and completion. |
| **PR** | Provider records | Attendance from the organization's own check-in, or a paid booking's schedule. Not individually confirmed. |
| **P** | Parent reported | A line you added yourself. |

### How credits and the GPA work

- **One credit is 150 contact hours.** Famlo suggests a credit from the hours
  when you edit a line, but never fills it in for you. You decide the credit.
- **The GPA is unweighted, on a 4.0 scale**, with no honors or AP weighting.
  Each grade counts in proportion to its credit.
- **Only lines with both a letter grade and a credit count toward the GPA.**
  A graded course with no credit has no weight.
- **Pass marks earn their credit but stay out of the GPA:** P, Pass, CR,
  Credit, S, Sat, Complete, Completed.
- **Open marks earn no credit yet and don't count against the GPA:** I, Inc,
  Incomplete, W, WD, Withdrawn, IP, Scheduled, Enrolled. A course with a credit
  and no grade is treated the same way. Their credit shows as "in progress" next
  to **Credits earned**, and **Cumulative GPA** reads **In progress** until
  something is graded.
- Any other mark you type prints as you typed it and stays out of the GPA.

| Grade | Points | Grade | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| A+ | 4.0 | C+ | 2.3 |
| A | 4.0 | C | 2.0 |
| A− | 3.7 | C− | 1.7 |
| B+ | 3.3 | D+ | 1.3 |
| B | 3.0 | D | 1.0 |
| B− | 2.7 | D− | 0.7 |
| | | F | 0.0 |

You give grades and credits on the school record. A grade an organization gave
can only be changed by that organization. See
[Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record).

## Hours log

The **Hours log** ("Every activity and hour for the year.") is the document
most states ask for when they want instruction hours counted. For one school
year it shows:

- **Total hours**, **Provider sessions** and **Courses & activities**. Hours
  that come from a booked class's published schedule, because no attendance was
  taken, are shown separately as "+ N scheduled".
- **Recorded by providers**: each class with **Activity**, **Provider**,
  **Dates**, **Sessions** and **Hours**, from the organization's own attendance.
- **Recorded by the supervising parent**: what you added, with **Activity**,
  **Materials used, by title**, **Dates** and **Hours**. The materials column
  shows the notes you wrote on each line, so name your books and programs there.
- A signature line, and the reminder "Retain with the portfolio; your state may
  require it for a fixed period."

An activity with no recorded hours is still listed but adds nothing to the
total.

## Scholarship spending

**Scholarship spending** ("Every invoice a program paid for, itemised.") is for
the person who reviews your scholarship or ESA spending. For one school year it
lists every scholarship invoice an organization raised through Famlo for your
child, with the date, provider, description, category, invoice number and
amount. It totals **Claimed**, **Paid** and **Outstanding**, and breaks the
money down **By category** and **By status**.

If your child had no scholarship-funded seats that year, it says so. More on
paying this way: [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa).

## Your homeschool name and school profile

The **School profile** ("The context an admissions office needs to read the
transcript.") is a support document to send with the transcript, for example
with a college application. It explains how credit and grades are assigned,
lists each organization your child took classes with (with the courses and
what Famlo recorded there), explains IC, PR and P, and includes an
**Accreditation** section.

Most of it is filled in for you. Four sections are yours to write, on the
**Edit family** page:

1. Select **Family**, then **Edit**. (On the school profile, the **Add them**
   link opens the same page.)
2. Fill **Homeschool name** (1). This is what every document calls your
   homeschool.
3. Under **Your school profile** (2), fill any of **Educational philosophy**,
   **Curriculum**, **Assessment and evaluation** and **Graduation requirements**.
4. Select **Save**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/transcripts-edit-family.webp" alt="The Edit family page with the Homeschool name field (1) and the Your school profile section (2), followed by Educational philosophy, Curriculum and Assessment and evaluation" url="famlo.co/family/edit" width={1280} height={1000} />

- Leave a school profile section blank and it's left out. A blank
  **Curriculum** lists the year's course titles instead.
- If **Homeschool name** is blank, documents use your last name followed by
  "Home Education", for example "Rivera Home Education".

## Enrollment letter

The **Enrollment letter** ("For a learner's permit, a work permit, an insurance
discount.") confirms that your child is enrolled in your home education
program for the school year. It includes their date of birth and grade, the
number of courses and hours recorded so far, and the organizations that teach
them. You sign it as the supervising parent.

To address it to someone:

1. Open **Enrollment letter** and, if you see the year switcher, pick the year.
2. Open **Address this letter to somebody**.
3. Fill **Recipient**, for example a person and the office they work for.
4. Fill **Purpose**, for example "a dual-enrollment application".
5. Select **Update the letter**.
6. Select **Print or save as PDF**.

:::note
The recipient and purpose aren't saved. Print the letter, then change them for
the next one. With no recipient, the letter opens "To whom it may concern,".
:::

## Diploma

The **Diploma** ("Parent-signed and typeset.") shows the diploma type, your
child's name, the credits earned across every year, any honors, and the date and
place it was awarded, with lines for your signature and the date.

1. Open **Diploma**.
2. Open **Graduation date, diploma type and honors** under the diploma.
3. Set **Graduation date**, **Diploma type** (it defaults to High School
   Diploma) and **Honors** (for example "with honors"; leave it blank for none).
4. Select **Save**.

The award line appears once a graduation date is set.

## Certificate for one course

Every line on the school record that isn't hidden has a **Certificate** link. It opens a
certificate of completion: "Awarded to" your child "for completing" the course,
with the provider, sessions, hours and school year. A line at the foot says
how the record was established: confirmed by the instructor, recorded by the
organization's own check-in, or recorded by you.

## Famlo doesn't accredit anything

Your documents are issued by you, as the supervising parent, under your
state's home education law. The school profile says so in its own words:
"Famlo is record-keeping software and accredits nothing". The diploma and the
enrollment letter carry the same note. What Famlo adds is a clear record of
which hours and courses an organization recorded and which you entered, so a
college or district can tell them apart and contact the organizations directly.

Check what your state, umbrella school or college asks for. A
[free state-by-state summary](/docs/tools/homeschool-records) is a good place to
start.

## Troubleshooting

**The transcript shows no GPA.**
Only lines with both a letter grade and a credit count. Add a credit and a
grade to the lines that should count, on the school record. **In progress**
means some credited lines have no grade yet.

**A class is missing from a document.**
Check that the line isn't hidden on the school record. Booked classes reach the
record the night after a meeting takes place or attendance is taken. See
[Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record).

**My documents say "Home Education" with no name, or the wrong name.**
Set **Homeschool name** in **Family** › **Edit**, and your first and last name
in **Account settings** › **Personal information**.

**The enrollment letter lost the recipient I typed.**
That's expected. Recipient and purpose are never saved. Type them again, then
select **Update the letter**.

**The PDF includes extra pages or cuts off.**
In your browser's print window, check the paper size and margins, and turn off
headers and footers if your browser adds them.
