# Reading an activity page

Every class, field trip, park day and event on famlo.co has its own activity
page. On it, the booking card shows the price, lets you make your choices and
holds the one button that tells you what you can do next. This page explains
each part of the card, every label the button can show, and the tools around it.

## The booking card, top to bottom

On a computer the booking card sits on the right and stays in view as you
scroll. (On a phone it works differently. See [On a phone](#on-a-phone).)

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/activity-page-booking-card.webp" alt="A booking card showing $28.86 per person, Includes booking fee, the Option and Children fields, an Add Materials Kit checkbox, the Register button, You won't be charged yet, the Total and the refund line" url="famlo.co/activities/nature-journaling-fort-myers-fl-t7ehf4yij9" width={416} height={582} />

**The price.** Every price you see already includes everything you'll pay.
Paid bookings include a booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket,
plus the cost of processing your payment. When the fee applies, the line
**Includes booking fee** sits under the price. Some organizations pay the fee
for you, and then that line isn't there. For the full story, see
[What families pay](/docs/families/whats-free-and-whats-paid).

- The words beside the price say what it buys, such as "per person", "per hour"
  or "for all 10 sessions".
- **From** above the price means the options cost different amounts, and
  this is the lowest.
- A weekly class may add a line such as what one session works out to, or
  the price "for the term".
- A free activity shows **Free** and "No cost to attend".

**The fields.** Only the ones that apply to this activity appear:

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Section** | The class is split into groups, for example by age. Pick one and the whole card, including the dates and price, switches to that group. Under it: "Booking children of different ages? Each one is matched to their own section at checkout." |
| **Session** | For anything that meets more than once: the days and dates, then the time and the number of sessions. |
| **Option** | The ways to join and their prices, such as a standard and a sibling ticket. When there's no dropdown, up to four options are listed under the price, then "N more options at checkout." |
| **Date** | Shown when you choose the "Just one date" option of a weekly class. A full date is listed but can't be picked. A note below names the other option, such as the whole term. |
| **Hours** | For options charged by the hour. |
| **Children** | How many places you want, up to 10, or fewer when fewer spots are left. |

**Add-ons.** Extras such as a materials kit show as checkboxes, each with
**Add** and its name, and its price beside it.

**"N attending".** How many people have booked so far, with a few small
profile pictures of the parents who booked. No names are shown.

**You won't be charged yet** and **Total.** The total changes as you pick an
option, children, hours and add-ons. Nothing is charged until you finish
checkout, and checkout checks your choices again.

**The refund line.** The organization's refund terms, for example "Full refund
any time before the first class." If the organization hasn't chosen terms, it
reads: "The organizer hasn't set a refund policy, so Famlo's default applies: a
full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before it starts."

**Your scholarship.** If an organization has given you a scholarship you can
use on this class, a green box shows the crossed-out price and your price,
with "From your … scholarship. Only you see this; other families see the
regular price." Some organizations also show **Need help paying? Request a
scholarship**. See [Scholarships from an organization](/docs/families/scholarships).

To book from the card:

1. Choose an **Option** (1), if there's more than one.
2. Set **Children** (2) to the number of places you need.
3. Check any add-ons you want (3).
4. Select **Register** (4). Checkout opens with your choices filled in.

The rest happens at checkout. See
[Registering and checking out](/docs/families/registering). What each way to
join means (sections, single dates, packs and more) is in
[Sections, single dates, packs and other ways to join](/docs/families/booking/ways-to-join).

## What the button says

The button changes with the activity and with you. These are all its labels:

| Button | When you see it |
|---|---|
| **This Event Has Passed** (grayed out) | The activity has started and isn't taking bookings any more. |
| **Registration closed**, with "Started *date*", and **Save it on Famlo** | A class that has started and isn't taking new families or selling single dates. Save it to keep it in your Saved list. Once saved, the button reads **Saved**. |
| **Register for the class**, with "*Class* is sold as a whole class, not by the date." | You're on the page for one date of a class that's sold only as a whole. The button takes you to the class. If the class is closed, you see a short note instead of a button. |
| **Not open for registration** (grayed out) | There's nothing on sale to join right now. |
| **Ask about a spot** | The organization can't take payments on Famlo at the moment. See the next section. |
| **Remind me the day before** | You're signed out, on some free listings. It makes a free account and books your RSVP in one step. See [RSVP to a free activity](/docs/families/booking/free-rsvp). |
| **Sign In to Register** / **Sign In to RSVP** | You're signed out. Sign in, or create a free account, to continue. |
| **You're going** | You've booked. See [After you book](#after-you-book). |
| **Join the waitlist** / **You're on the waitlist** | The activity is full. Joining saves it, and you're alerted when a spot opens. See [Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts). |
| **RSVP** | A free activity. When there's nothing to choose, you RSVP right on the page. Otherwise it opens checkout. See [RSVP to a free activity](/docs/families/booking/free-rsvp). |
| **Register** | A paid activity. It opens checkout. |

Class pages on an organization's own website work a little differently. See
[Booking and buying on an organization's website](/docs/families/organization-websites/booking-on-a-website).

## When the organization takes payment directly

Sometimes an organization lists a paid activity but can't take payments on
Famlo yet. The card then says "*Organization* takes payment directly" and "Not
through Famlo right now. Ask about a spot and they'll answer you here — we
won't take your money for this one."

- Select **Ask about a spot** to send the organization a message. If you're
  signed out, you sign in first ("You'll sign in so they can reply to you").
- Under **Or reach them directly** you may see the organization's email
  address or website.
- The organization replies in [Messages](/docs/families/messages). You arrange
  the place and the payment with them, and there's no booking fee.

## Listings that take registration elsewhere

Some listings come from organizations that keep their own registration list,
such as libraries. The page then shows "*Organization* takes registration on
their own site", explains that your Famlo RSVP saves the date and reminds you
but doesn't hold a spot, and offers **Check availability at** *their site*.

1. Select **RSVP** and check "I'll check with *Organization* — Famlo can't hold
   my spot".
2. Select **Confirm RSVP**. The card shows **You're going** with a two-step box: **Save it on
   Famlo** is crossed off, and the second step reads **Get a place with
   *Organization*.**
3. Select **Check availability at** *their site* and register with them. "The
   link is in your confirmation email too."

The day after, Famlo emails you to ask "Do you have a place for *activity*?"
There's no booking fee on these listings.

## After you book

Once you've booked, the card shows **You're going** and how many spots are
reserved, with the child's name when you named one.

- **Cancel registration** opens the cancel screen, where you can see what
  you'll get back before you confirm. See
  [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).
- **Cancel RSVP** appears instead for a free one-tap RSVP. It cancels straight
  away.
- **Add to calendar** opens a box with two choices.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/activity-page-add-to-calendar.webp" alt="The Add to calendar box with two choices: Just this event and Everything you RSVP to" url="famlo.co/activities/intro-to-robotics-fort-myers-fl-x6yxj748m4" width={496} height={372} />

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Just this event** | "Downloads a file. It won't update if the details change." |
| **Everything you RSVP to** | "Subscribe once. New RSVPs appear on their own, and changes follow." It shows your private calendar link, **Add to my calendar app** and **Setup instructions** for Apple, Google and Outlook. |

If your calendar link is switched off, you see **Turn on my calendar feed**
instead. Keep the link private: anyone with it can see your calendar. For more,
see [How Do I Export My Calendar to Google or Apple?](/docs/how-to/export-calendar).

## Share and embed

Select **Share** next to the title to open **Share this activity**. Its tiles
are **Copy Link**, **Email**, **Messages**, **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**,
**Facebook**, **X** and **Embed**, plus **More options** when your browser
has its own share menu.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/activity-page-share.webp" alt="The Share this activity box with the Copy Link, Email, Messages, WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Embed and More options tiles" url="famlo.co/activities/nature-journaling-fort-myers-fl-t7ehf4yij9" width={560} height={492} />

**Embed** gives you code to show the activity as a card on another website,
such as a group's blog:

1. Select **Embed**. The box changes to **Embed this activity**.
2. Under **Customize your code**, check **Hide the price** or **Create "no
   follow" links** if you want them. The preview updates.
3. Select **Copy HTML** and paste it into the other website.

The card shows the photo, title, details, price (or Free) and a
**View on Famlo** link, and nothing about you. Select **Go back** to return
to the tiles.

## Where you'll be, and the badges

**Where you'll be**, lower on the page, shows the place name, room, address
and a map. **Get directions** opens Google Maps, or Apple Maps on an iPhone
or iPad. When a listing gives only a city, you see "Approximate — this listing
gives a city, not a street address." and no directions link. A class whose
sections meet in different places shows each place. Online activities have no
map.

Badges on the photo give you a quick status:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Event Today!** | It's happening today. |
| **Starts tomorrow** / **Starts in N days** | It's coming up within a week. |
| **Almost Full** | Few spots left: 5 or fewer when the activity holds 50 or fewer people, or 10% or less of the places when it holds more. |
| **Full** | No spots left. The button offers the waitlist. |
| **Past Event** | It has already taken place. |

## On a phone

On a phone there's no booking card. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows
the price and the button, and some labels are shorter: **Sign In**, **See the
class**, **Remind me** and, on listings that take registration elsewhere,
**Now get a place**. You choose the option, children and add-ons at checkout.
**Share** is the icon at the top right, and it opens your phone's own share
menu. **Add to calendar** isn't on the phone bar. Use the subscribe
link in your confirmation email, or **Calendar**, instead.

## Troubleshooting

**The total on the card is different at checkout.** Checkout uses what you
actually pick there, for each child, including any promo code, scholarship or
credit. The card is a starting point.

**I don't see Includes booking fee.** The organization pays the fee for you,
the activity is free, or the organization takes payment or registration
somewhere else.

**The Children field won't go higher.** It stops at 10, or at the number of
spots left. At checkout you can add more children with
**Add another attendee** while spots remain.

**I booked but the card still says Register.** Make sure you're signed in to
the account you booked with. Your bookings are also on your
[calendar](/docs/families/calendar).
