# RSVP to a free activity

Many free activities on Famlo book right on the activity page. Select
**RSVP**, say how many are coming, and you're going. There's nothing to pay
and no checkout. If you don't have a Famlo account yet, some free listings
let you RSVP with just your email address.

## RSVP in a few taps

This works when the activity is free, has no add-ons and has at most one
ticket type.

1. Open the activity and select **RSVP**.
2. In **How many are coming?**, set the number of spots with **−** and **+**. You can also type the number.
3. Select **Confirm RSVP**.

The booking card then says **You're going**, with how many spots you
reserved. Your RSVP is on your [calendar](/docs/families/calendar), and
Famlo emails you a confirmation.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/free-rsvp-dialog.webp" alt="The How many are coming? box over a free Chess Club activity, with the number of spots set to 1, of 20 spots left, and the Confirm RSVP button" url="famlo.co/activities/chess-club-fort-myers-fl-v7lyx4h6sx" width={1280} height={720} />

A few things to know:

- **How many you can reserve.** When the organizer has set a number of
  places, the box shows how many you can choose, for example "of 20 spots
  left". One RSVP holds up to 30 spots, so that number never goes above 30.
- **When only one spot is left** and the organizer asks nothing else,
  there's no box. Select **RSVP** and the spot is yours.
- **When it's full**, the button changes to **Join the waitlist**. See
  [Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).
- **If you're already going**, the card shows **You're going** instead of
  **RSVP**, so you can't book twice by mistake.

## When the organizer asks questions first

Some organizers ask a few questions or ask you to agree to terms, such as a
waiver, before you come. The box is then titled **Before you RSVP**. It
shows **Number of spots**, the organizer's questions and their terms.

1. Set the **Number of spots**.
2. Answer the questions. Ones marked with * are required.
3. Check the terms. If any term is required, sign with your full name, typed or drawn.
4. Select **Confirm RSVP**.

Famlo keeps a copy of your answers and of exactly what you agreed to, with
your booking.

If the organizer asks you to sign a waiver or agreement, **RSVP** takes you
to checkout instead, where you read and sign it in a step titled **Before you
pay**. Nothing is charged. See
[Signing waivers and agreements](/docs/families/booking/signing-agreements).

## An RSVP is for your household, not a named child

A one-tap RSVP reserves a number of spots for your family. It doesn't ask
which children are coming. That matters if the organizer uses QR code
check-in: the check-in page lists only children named on a booking, so an
RSVP's spots won't appear there. A leader can still sign your child in from
their own attendance list. See
[Reminders and checking in on the day](/docs/families/booking/on-the-day).

## When a free activity goes to checkout instead

If a free activity has more than one ticket type, or add-ons, **RSVP**
takes you to checkout so you can make those choices. You pick who's coming there, and the last button reads
**Complete free RSVP**, with "Nothing is charged." underneath. For the steps,
see [Registering and checking out](/docs/families/registering).

If a class has sections, choose a section on its page first. See
[Sections, single dates, packs and other ways to join](/docs/families/booking/ways-to-join).

## Cancel an RSVP

1. Open the activity.
2. Under **You're going**, select **Cancel RSVP**.

It's canceled straight away, and the card goes back to **RSVP**. There's
no refund screen, because nothing was paid. You can also cancel from
**Calendar**: open the activity there, select **Cancel registration**, then
**Cancel booking**. You can cancel until the activity starts. If your plans change again, you can RSVP again
while spots are left.

## RSVP without an account: Remind me the day before

On some free listings, signed-out visitors see **Remind me the day before**
instead of **Sign In to RSVP**. You get:

- an email reminder the day before;
- the activity on your Famlo calendar, with everything else you've planned.

It's offered on free listings from organizations that haven't claimed
their Famlo page yet, often libraries and museums whose events are listed
on Famlo, as long as the listing asks no questions or terms and isn't full.
Everywhere else, sign in first with **Sign In to RSVP**.

1. Enter **Your email** (1).
2. If the listing takes registration on the organizer's own site, check "I'll check with …" (2). See the next section.
3. Select **Remind me the day before** (3). Famlo emails you a 6-digit code.
4. On **Check your email**, enter the code and select **Confirm and RSVP**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/free-rsvp-remind-me.webp" alt="A free library activity's booking card for a signed-out visitor, with the Your email field, the I'll check with Larkspur Hollow Library box and the Remind me the day before button highlighted" url="famlo.co/activities/lego-builders-club-boise-id-bupu9n11pp" width={424} height={846} />

You're back on the activity page, signed in, with one spot booked (on a
listing that takes registration elsewhere, the card says "1 spot on your
calendar") and the message "You're going! We'll email you a reminder the day before."

- **New to Famlo?** Confirming creates a free Famlo account for that email,
  with no password. You sign in with an emailed code. By selecting
  **Confirm and RSVP**, you confirm you're 18 or older, agree to Famlo's
  Terms of Service and acknowledge its Privacy Policy.
- **Already have an account?** Use the same email, or select **Log in**. The
  code signs you in to your account and the RSVP goes there.
- **What should we call you? (optional)** appears on the page afterwards. Type
  your first name and select **Save**, or skip it and add it later in
  [Account settings](/docs/account/personal-information).
- **Wrong address?** Select **Change email**. If the code doesn't arrive,
  select **Resend code**.
- **On a phone**, the button reads **Remind me** and opens a panel titled
  "Get a reminder for" the activity's name.

This way books one spot. If more of you are coming, cancel the RSVP and
select **RSVP** again, now signed in, to choose the number.

For other ways to create an account and sign in, see
[Signing in and creating your account](/docs/account/signing-in).

## Activities that take registration somewhere else

Some listings come from organizations, often libraries, that keep their own
sign-up list. The activity page says "[Organization] takes registration on
their own site", usually with a **Check availability at** button that
opens their site.

Your Famlo RSVP still saves the date on your calendar and sends your
reminder, but **it does not hold a spot**. The organization never receives
it. These programs often fill up well before the date, so check with them
too.

- **Before you RSVP**, you check "I'll check with [Organization] — Famlo can't
  hold my spot". Famlo won't take the RSVP without it.
- **After you RSVP**, the card shows a second step, **Get a place with
  [Organization].**, with the **Check availability at** button. On a phone,
  the button reads **Now get a place**.
- **The confirmation email** is titled "[Activity] is on your calendar" and
  includes the same link.
- **About a day later**, if the activity hasn't started yet, Famlo emails
  you to ask "Do you have a place for [Activity]?", with a **Check
  availability** button.

There's never a booking fee on these listings.

## Troubleshooting

**I see Sign In to RSVP, not Remind me the day before.**
The email option is only on some free listings (see above). Select **Sign
In to RSVP**, sign in or create your account, then open the activity again
and select **RSVP**.

**The RSVP button took me to checkout.**
The activity has a choice to make, such as a ticket type or an add-on. Complete the free checkout; nothing is charged.

**It says "This one's full."**
The last spot went while you were choosing. Select **Join the waitlist** to
hear when a spot opens.

**I need more spots than the box allows.**
The box stops at the number of spots left, and at 30. For a bigger group,
message the organizer.

**My child isn't on the check-in page.**
An RSVP doesn't name a child. Ask a leader to sign your child in from their
attendance list.
