# Sections, single dates, packs and other ways to join

Many classes offer more than one way to join: a section for each age group,
one date at a time, a pack of visits, or a lower price for members or for a
second child. The organization decides which ways a class offers. You choose
on the activity page and again for each child at checkout. This page explains
what each choice means and what happens after you book.

## Sections: one class, split into groups

Some organizations split one class into sections, for example by age, day,
place or teacher. Each section has its own dates, its own price and its own
spots.

- **On the activity page**, the booking card has a **Section** dropdown. Pick
  one to see its dates, time, place and price. The note
  under it reads "Booking children of different ages? Each one is matched to
  their own section at checkout."
- **At checkout**, each attendee card has its own **Section** field. When you
  choose a child in **Who's coming**, Famlo picks the section that fits their
  age, and you can change it.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/ways-to-join-section-checkout.webp" alt="An attendee card at checkout with Max, age 11, in Who's coming, the Section field set to Ages 9-12, the Ticket field, and the note The only section for Max's age" url="famlo.co/checkout/nature-explorers-fort-myers-fl-2a4t2rudq8" width={678} height={250} />

The **Section** field (1) shows the section chosen for your child. The note
under the card (2) tells you why:

| Note | What it means |
|---|---|
| "The only section for Max's age." | One section fits, and your child is in it. |
| "2 sections fit Max's age. Also for them: …" | More than one fits. Switch the **Section** field if you prefer another. |
| "No section fits Max's age. They're in the closest one, and you can switch it or remove them." | Your child is outside every section's ages. Check with the organization, pick another section, or remove the child. |

Siblings can be in different sections in the same checkout. Add each child
with **+ Add another attendee**, and each one is matched to their own section.
A child can be in only one section of a class. Your confirmation email lists
each section's day, time and place.

## Just one date of a weekly class

Some weekly classes let you come to a single date instead of the whole term.

1. On the activity page, open **Option** (1) and choose **Just one date**.
2. Choose the day in **Date** (2). When the class has a limit, each date shows
   how many spots are left.
3. Select **Register**. Checkout opens with **Just one date** and your date
   already chosen.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/ways-to-join-single-date.webp" alt="A booking card with Option set to Just one date and the Date field showing Wed Oct 28, 12 left, above the note Other option: Whole term and Coming to 6 or more Wednesdays? The whole term is cheaper" url="famlo.co/activities/open-art-studio-fort-myers-fl-k6zvqbcpwq" width={416} height={635} />

If you open the page for one particular date, the card starts on
**Just one date** with that date already chosen. Under the fields, a note
shows the whole-term price and, when it applies, how many dates make the whole
term the cheaper choice ("Coming to 6 or more Wednesdays? The whole term is
cheaper.").

**When a date is full.** A full date stays in the list but can't be chosen.
At checkout the note under **Date** names it, for example "Wed Oct 28 is
full. **Tell me if a spot opens**."
That link opens the date's own page, where you can join the waitlist. To
learn how the waitlist works, see
[Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

**When your child already has the whole term.** A child booked for the whole
term isn't offered a single date as well. Checkout says so,
for example "Max is already in for the whole term, so isn't offered here and
can't buy a date twice."

**When a date is full and you buy the whole term.** The whole term leaves out
any date that's already full, and you aren't charged for it. The note under
the ticket names the full dates, for example "Oct 28 is full, so it's left out
and not charged.", with a link such as **Tell me if a spot opens on Oct 28**.

**After the class has started.** Some organizations still take whole-term
sign-ups after the first date. The option then reads **Rest of the term**,
priced for the dates that are left. If the organization doesn't take late
sign-ups and sells no single dates, the card shows **Registration closed**
with the date it started. Select **Save it on Famlo** to keep the class in
your **Saved** list. If the organization runs it again, Famlo emails you when
the next term is listed. See [Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

## Packs of visits

A pack is a set of visits you buy once and spend on the dates you choose,
for example a 5-visit pack.

### Buy a pack

1. At checkout, choose the pack in the attendee card's **Ticket** field (1).
2. To book your first visit now, choose a date in
   **Use a visit now? (optional)** (2). To save every visit for later, choose
   **Not now**.
3. Finish checkout as usual.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/ways-to-join-pack-checkout.webp" alt="An attendee card at checkout with the Ticket field set to 5-visit pack and the Use a visit now? (optional) field set to Wed Oct 21, first day, 12 left, above a note about the pack" url="famlo.co/checkout/watercolor-sketch-club-fort-myers-fl-nzbolh1mdz" width={678} height={278} />

The note under the fields sums up the rules: "One pack for your family, for
any of your children." A visit you cancel before its date starts comes back
to the pack, and the visits end on the date the class ends.

### Spend a visit

- From **Account settings** › **Packs**, select **Use a visit** on the pack.
  Checkout opens with the pack as the **Ticket**, shown as **Pack visit** with
  the visits left. Choose the child and the **Date**. A visit costs nothing at
  checkout, because you paid when you bought the pack.
- If you book **Just one date** of the same class, you can also choose
  **Use a visit from my pack** when checkout asks who pays for that spot.

### See your pack

**Account settings** › **Packs** opens **Your packs**. **Packs** appears in
Account settings only after you've bought a pack. Each pack shows the
organization, the class, the visits left, which children can use it and when
it ends. Under **What's happened** is a list of every change, such as
"Bought 5 visits", each date booked, each visit attended and each visit that
came back.

### Cancel a visit or a pack

| What you cancel | What happens |
|---|---|
| A visit, before its date starts | "Cancel now and the visit goes back to your pack." |
| A visit, after its date has started | "This date has started, so the visit stays used." |
| The whole pack | Any dates you booked with it that haven't started are canceled too. You get a refund for the visits you haven't used, following the class's refund terms. Once the class's last date has started, there's no refund. |

A pack ends when the class's last date ends, and any visits left then can't
be used. For refunds in general, see
[Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).

## Members price

Some organizations offer a lower price to their own members. Checkout offers
it only to families who are members of that organization, so you need to be
signed in with the account that holds the membership. If you aren't a member,
you see the other prices. To learn how to join an organization, see
[Your memberships](/docs/families/memberships).

## Sibling rate

A sibling rate makes a second or later child on the same price cost less. You
don't choose it. Checkout applies it on its own when you register the
children together, and the order summary shows the lower amount before you
pay. On a class with sections, it shows as its own line, for example
"2nd child 10% off".

The rate counts the children in one checkout, so add every child with
**+ Add another attendee** before you pay.

## Hourly options

Some options are priced by the hour. The price shows "per hour", and you set
**Hours** on the booking card and again in the attendee card at checkout. The
option's price is multiplied by the hours you choose.

## Add-ons in sizes

Add-ons, such as a T-shirt or a materials kit, are chosen for each attendee
under **Add-ons for this attendee**. When an add-on comes in sizes, you choose
**None** or one size for each child, so siblings can each have their own size.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't choose the date I want.**
It's full. At checkout, select **Tell me if a spot opens** next to the date to
open its page and join the waitlist.

**Just one date isn't offered for my child.**
Your child is already booked for the whole term, so they can't buy a single
date as well.

**My child is in the wrong section.**
Change the **Section** field in their attendee card before you pay. If the
note says no section fits their age, ask the organization which one to choose.

**I don't see the members price.**
Make sure you're signed in with the account that holds your membership. Only
members of that organization see it.

**The sibling rate didn't apply.**
The rate applies only to children registered in the same checkout, on the
same price. Your order can't be changed after you book, so message the
organization if you booked your children separately.

**I can't use my pack.**
Check **Account settings** › **Packs**. A pack with no visits left, or one
whose class has ended, has no **Use a visit** button.
