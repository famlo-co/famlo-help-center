# Your calendar

Your calendar holds everything your family has booked on Famlo, in one place. Open a booking to see the details, get directions, cancel it or ask for a refund, and keep track of dates that have moved.

## Open your calendar

Select **Calendar** in the navigation (1). On a phone, it is the **Calendar** tab in the bar at the bottom. The page is headed **My calendar**.

Old links to your bookings, such as **View my bookings** in an email, open the calendar in **List** view.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/calendar-week.webp" alt="My calendar in Week view, with Calendar in the navigation, the Week and List switch, and the Subscribe button highlighted" url="famlo.co/calendar" width={1280} height={800} />

At the top you'll find:

- The **Week** and **List** switch (2).
- **Subscribe** (3), which puts your calendar in Apple, Google or Outlook.
- In **Week** view, the **Previous week** and **Next week** arrows, the dates you're looking at, and **Today** to jump back to this week.

## Week and List

**Week** lays out one week with the times down the side, so you can spot clashes and gaps. Each booking is a colored block showing its title, place and time. On a phone, **Week** shows the coming days as a list, one day at a time, because a seven-day grid doesn't fit a small screen.

**List** shows every booking in date order, under **Upcoming** and **Past**. It's the quickest way to answer "what have we got coming up?" Each row shows the date, time and place, the organization, and which of your children are going.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/calendar-list.webp" alt="My calendar in List view, with the Add to calendar icon highlighted on the first row" url="famlo.co/calendar?view=list" width={1280} height={800} />

## Filter by child or organization

On a computer, the column on the left has three parts:

- **A small month.** Dots mark the days with something booked. Select a day to jump to it.
- **Who's going.** One row for each person in your family, each with their own color and a count of their upcoming activities.
- **Communities.** One row for each organization you follow or have something booked with, also with a count.

Every row starts checked. Uncheck a person or an organization to hide their bookings, and check it again to bring them back. To see one child's schedule, uncheck everyone else.

The month and the filters appear on wider screens only. On a phone, you see all of your bookings.

## What appears on your calendar

Your bookings appear on your calendar, including those made on an organization's own website. It shows the bookings made from your Famlo account.

- **One entry per activity.** If you booked two children into the same activity, you see one entry with both names on it.
- **Past bookings from the last 90 days.** Older ones drop off.
- **Not on your calendar:** activities you've saved, activities with no date yet, appointments with an organization, and bookings you've canceled.

Saved activities are on your **Saved** page. See [Saved activities and alerts](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

### Labels you might see

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cancelled** | The organization canceled the activity. In **Week** view the block is struck through. |
| **Field trip** | The activity is a field trip. A small bus icon marks it in **Week** view. |
| **Moved** | The organization moved a date you booked. The row says which date moved and to when. |
| A time with **event time** after it, such as "10 AM CDT event time" | The activity takes place in a different time zone from yours. It shows the start time where the activity happens. |

When a date has moved, the row also shows **Can't come? Choose credit or refund** until you decide. Select it to choose a credit, a refund where the organization offers one, or to keep the place. See [When a booked date moves or is cancelled](/docs/families/money/class-changes-and-credits).

### Times and time zones

Your calendar shows times in the time zone saved in **Account settings** › **Preferences** › **Time zone**. If you haven't chosen one, it uses your device's time zone. When an activity happens somewhere with a different time, the row shows its local start time as well, as described above.

## Open a booking

Select a block in **Week** view or a row in **List** view. A panel slides in from the side. In **List** view, selecting the activity's title opens the activity page instead.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/calendar-sheet.webp" alt="The booking panel for Math Circle, showing the date, place, your order, Cancel registration and View activity" url="famlo.co/calendar?view=list" width={448} height={880} />

The panel shows:

- The activity's title, organization and booking status, such as **Confirmed**.
- The date and time, in the activity's own time zone.
- **Join online** for an online activity, or the address. When the address is complete, **Directions** opens a map.
- **Your order**: each child you booked, what you paid, and **Receipt & details**.
- **Cancel registration** (1), before the activity starts.
- **Request a refund**, once it has started, if you can still ask.
- **View activity** (2), which opens the activity page, and an **Add to calendar** icon beside it.

### Cancel a booking

1. Open the booking on your calendar.
2. Select **Cancel registration**.
3. Check the refund on the **Cancel booking** page. It follows the organization's refund terms for that activity.
4. Select **Cancel booking**, or **Keep booking** to go back.

**Cancel registration** appears only before the activity starts. Shop purchases and payment requests can't be canceled here. Only the organization can refund those. For what you get back, see [Cancelling and refunds](/docs/families/payments-and-refunds).

### Ask for a refund after it has started

Once an activity has started, the panel shows **Request a refund** instead, while you can still ask. The organization decides, and its answer arrives in **Messages**.

While your request is open, the panel says "Refund requested" with the amount, and that the organization hasn't answered yet. Select **Withdraw request** to take it back.

## Add one activity to your calendar app

Each row in **List** view has an **Add to calendar** icon (1 in the List screenshot) at its right-hand end. The panel has the same icon next to **View activity**. It downloads a file with that one activity, which you open in your calendar app.

The file is a one-time copy. If the organization changes the time or place later, your copy doesn't update. To keep everything up to date, subscribe instead.

## Subscribe from Apple, Google or Outlook

**Subscribe** opens **Subscribe to this calendar**. It gives you a private link that adds your Famlo calendar to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook, so new bookings and changes appear there on their own. Your calendar app decides how often it checks for changes. If you see **Enable calendar feed**, select it first. Anyone with the link can see your family's schedule, including your children's names, so keep it private. If the link gets out, **Regenerate link** replaces it, and **Turn off feed** switches it off. For step-by-step setup, see [How do I export my calendar to Google or Apple?](/docs/how-to/export-calendar).

## Bookings made on an organization's website

When you book on an organization's own website, you sign in with your Famlo account, and the booking appears on your Famlo calendar like any other. The **Parent portal** on that website lists only your bookings with that organization. Its **See bookings everywhere on Famlo** link opens this calendar.

You can cancel a class you booked on an organization's website either here or from **Cancel** on the **Bookings** tab of that website's Parent portal. Both show the same refund before you confirm.

See [Your Parent portal](/docs/families/organization-websites/parent-portal).

## Troubleshooting

**Something I booked isn't on my calendar.**
Check the left column first. The child or organization may be unchecked. The calendar shows bookings made from your account, so a booking made from someone else's account appears on theirs. Then check that the booking went through: your confirmation email and **Payments & invoices** show it. Appointments, activities with no date, and bookings you canceled don't appear.

**I can't find the filters on my phone.**
**Who's going**, **Communities** and the small month appear only on wider screens. Open the calendar on a computer or tablet, or turn your tablet sideways.

**The times look wrong.**
Check **Account settings** › **Preferences** › **Time zone**. If it's set to somewhere you're not, change it, or clear it to use your device's time zone.

**There's no Cancel registration on a booking.**
It doesn't appear once the activity has started (look for **Request a refund**), or on shop purchases and payment requests. Message the organization if you need help.

**My Week view on a phone says "Nothing coming up."**
On a phone, **Week** shows only what's ahead. Switch to **List** to see past activities.
