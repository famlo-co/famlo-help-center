# Your Home page

Home is the page you land on when you sign in to Famlo. It shows what needs
your attention, what your family has booked this week, each child's school
year and new activities nearby. Anyone with a Famlo account has one.

## Where to find Home

- **On a computer:** select **Dashboard** in the top bar.
- **On a phone:** tap **Home**, the first tab in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
- You can also go straight to famlo.co/dashboard.

When you sign in, Famlo brings you here, unless you were on your way to a
particular page.

## The greeting and the child chips

The top of the page shows today's date and a greeting with your first name.
The first time you arrive after creating your account, it says **Welcome to
Famlo** instead.

Next to the greeting is a row of chips: **Everyone**, then one chip for each
child, each with its own color dot. In the picture above, **Dashboard** is (1)
and the chips are (2).

- Select a child's chip to show only that child in **This week** and **School
  year so far**. Bookings with no child named stay in the list, so you can
  still say who's going.
- Select the same chip again, or **Everyone**, to see the whole family.
- Select **+ Add** to add another child. For the details you can record, see
  [Your family and children](/docs/families/your-family).

## Before you add a child

Until your family has at least one child on it, Home is a single column:

- The **Getting started** checklist, opened at **Add your first child**, with a
  short form: **First name**, **Birth month**, **Birth year** and **Add
  child**. No birth date is needed. **Add several at once** opens the full form.
  If you hid the checklist, an **Add your first child** card shows the same
  form.
- A **Where you are** card with the town Famlo thinks you're in, or
  **Set location** if it can't tell. Select the town (or **Set location**),
  then type a town or ZIP code in **Search a city or ZIP** and pick it, or
  select **Use my current location**. Home and Discover both follow your
  choice. If Famlo knows your state, the card links to that state's pages, for example **Homeschooling in
  Florida**, **Funding and ESAs in Florida** and **School records in Florida**.

Once you add a child, Home shows the full page described below. Anything you
own from an organization's shop still appears in the meantime.

## Getting started

New accounts see a **Getting started** checklist with a progress bar and "N of
5 done". Each step ticks itself off when you do it:

1. **Create your account** (already done).
2. **Add your first child**.
3. **Health & safety for organizers**: allergies, pickup people and an emergency contact.
4. **Start the school record**: log this week in one grid; classes you book fill in on their own.
5. **Find a first activity**: done after your first booking.

The checklist disappears when every step is done. **Hide** removes it sooner,
and it can't be brought back. For a walk-through of your first days, see
[Getting started as a new family](/docs/families/getting-started).

## Needs you

**Needs you** lists things waiting on you, and a few you're waiting on. It shows up to three
cards at a time, with a count next to the heading and "And N more things
after these" when there are more. Each card is marked **To do** (your move) or
**Waiting** (someone else's). When there's nothing, it says **Nothing needs you
right now**.

| Card | What it means | Button |
|---|---|---|
| "We're confirming your … payment with Stripe" (**Waiting**) | Your place is held. Don't pay again; the card clears on its own. | **View class** |
| "Next step: send this to …" or "Next step: reserve funds in …" (**To do**, ESA invoice) | A Step Up or ESA invoice needs your step. | **Open invoice**, and **I've submitted it** or **I've reserved the funds** once you've done that step |
| "… is waiting on …" (**Waiting**, ESA invoice) | You've done your part; the program or organization has the next step. | **View invoice** |
| "… has no emergency contact" | You enter it once, and it carries across every organization you join. | **Add** |
| "$X expires …" | Account credit or a scholarship award runs out soon. Unspent balance is written off. | **Spend it** |
| "Nobody has asked you about …'s photos" | Nothing is published until you answer. | **Answer** |
| "File …'s letter of intent" | Your state asks families to tell the school district (or the office it names) that they homeschool, and Famlo can fill in the letter. | **Write the letter** |
| A note from an organization, such as "You finished …" | The organization would like to hear how it went. Nothing is shared unless you say so. | **Leave a testimonial** or **Not now** |

While the **Getting started** checklist is showing, its health step covers the
emergency contact and photo questions, so those two cards wait until it's done
or hidden. The letter-of-intent card stops once you mark the letter sent, and
comes back a year later.

For more, see [Paying with Step Up or an ESA](/docs/families/money/step-up-and-esa),
the [Letter of Intent Generator](/docs/tools/letter-of-intent) and
[Share your experience](/docs/families/community/testimonials).

## Coming up and Library & resources

These two cards appear only when you have something to put in them.

- **Coming up** lists appointments you've booked with any organization. Each
  shows the service, the organization, the time and the format. Use
  **Reschedule** or **Details** to open the appointment, and **Join Zoom** when
  the organization has given a Zoom link. See
  [Booking a service or appointment](/docs/families/booking/services-and-appointments).
- **Library & resources** lists guides and videos you own from any
  organization's shop, with **Download** or **Watch**. See
  [Buying from a shop, and your downloads](/docs/families/money/shop-and-downloads).

## This week

**This week** lists your bookings for today and the next seven days, one row
per activity, with a name tag for each child going (on a wider screen).

- If you booked without naming a child, the row shows **Who's going?** instead.
  Select it to open the activity.
- **Open calendar** opens your full [calendar](/docs/families/calendar).
- On a quiet week it says **Nothing booked this week**, names your next booking
  if you have one, and offers **Find activities**.

## School year so far

**School year so far** shows each child's hours for the current school year,
labeled like 2026–27.

- A child with hours shows the total, a bar, and "N h confirmed by groups · N h
  you logged". Select the row to open that child's school record.
- A child with no hours yet shows **Log this week**, which opens the weekly
  grid for home lessons. Classes you book on Famlo fill the record in on their own.
- **Transcripts, diplomas & student IDs** opens your Family page, or your
  child's record if you have one child.

See [Your child's school record](/docs/families/school-records/school-record)
and [Log home learning with Log a week](/docs/families/school-records/log-a-week).

## New near you

**New near you** suggests up to three upcoming activities near you, starting
with ones that fit your children's ages. It never suggests something you've
already booked.

- "Fits" and a name, such as "Fits Max", says which child an activity suits.
- **Saved · N** appears once you've saved anything, and opens your saved list.
- **Discover** opens activity search. If nothing is nearby, **Look further
  afield** does the same.

See [Finding activities](/docs/families/finding-activities) and
[Saved activities and the waitlist](/docs/families/saved-and-alerts).

## You run (for organizers)

If you help run an organization on Famlo, a **You run** row sits at the top of
Home, above your family's cards. Each organization gets a card, the busiest
first, showing "N activities today", "Next up" and a date, or "Nothing scheduled",
and its website address if it has one.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/home-you-run.webp" alt="A You run card for Sunnyside Learning Co. with the Manage Sunnyside Learning Co. button and the Edit your website, View your website and Add a class links" url="famlo.co/dashboard" width={760} height={196} />

- **Manage** followed by the organization's name (for example **Manage
  Sunnyside Learning Co.**) opens the first part of the console you have
  access to.
- **Edit your website**, **View your website** ↗ and **Add a class** appear
  underneath when they apply. **View your website** shows when the
  organization has a website. **Add a class** shows to people who can change
  the organization's settings (by default, Owners and Co-leaders), and so does
  **Edit your website** when the organization has a website.

See [Your console and Today](/docs/organizations/getting-started/your-console).

## Troubleshooting

**A card I expected isn't there.**
**Coming up**, **Library & resources** and **You run** only appear when they
have something to show. **Needs you** shows three cards at a time, so check
the "more things" line under it.

**Only one child's activities are showing.**
A child's chip is selected. Select **Everyone**.

**New near you shows activities far from me.**
Home works out roughly where you are from your internet connection. To correct
it, open famlo.co/activities and select **Change** (or **Set location**) next to
the place name, then choose your town. Home follows. While your family has no
children added yet, you can also select the town on the **Where you are** card. The location in your
profile doesn't change this. See
[Finding activities](/docs/families/finding-activities).
