# Messages

Everything on Famlo that you can reply to lands in one inbox: answers from organizations, announcements about activities you booked, one-to-one conversations, and your support tickets. Messages are free and work the same with every organization.

## Open your inbox

- **On a computer:** select the bell (1) in the header. It shows a count when something is unread.
- **On a phone:** tap **Messages** (1 in the phone picture below) in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

The inbox is also at famlo.co/messages. New messages and the unread count appear without reloading the page.

Across the top of the list are three buttons: search (2), **Messaging settings** (3), which holds **Archived** and **Notification settings**, and **New conversation** (4).

## What is in the list

Each row is one conversation with one person or organization, not one message. The newest is at the top, and unread rows stand out.

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| An activity you booked | The organization's announcements about it and your own questions about it, together in one conversation |
| An organization | Your conversation from its **Message** button, plus its announcements that aren't about one activity |
| A person | A one-to-one conversation with someone you already message |
| Support | Your Famlo support tickets and notices from Famlo |
| Notifications | Updates with nobody to reply to |

When rows of different kinds are in your inbox, the list is split into lanes with headings such as **You booked these** and **From Famlo**.

**All** shows everything that isn't archived. Select **Unread** to see only what you haven't opened yet. If you also help run an organization, you can switch between **All**, **Parent** for your family's rows, and each organization's name for its rows. If you have support tickets, **Support** shows just those.

Opening a row marks it read.

## Search all messages

1. Select the search button at the top of the inbox.
2. Type in **Search all messages**.

Search looks inside every message in every conversation, as well as names and activity titles. Select **Cancel** to close the search.

## Message an organization

Use this for a general question, or for an organization you haven't booked with yet.

1. Open the organization's page on Famlo.
2. Select **Message**.
3. Type your question and send it.

The conversation goes to the organization, not to one person. Anyone on its team who handles messages can read it and answer, so your question doesn't wait for one volunteer to come back. Answers show the organization's name, not the name of the person who typed them.

The organization sees nothing until you send your first message. If you open the conversation and change your mind, nothing reaches them. Selecting **Message** again reopens the same conversation.

You can also find an organization by name with **New conversation**. See [Start a new conversation](#start-a-new-conversation).

## Ask about an activity

An activity you booked gets its own row once something is on it, such as an announcement or a message. Open the row and type in the box at the bottom, which reads "Ask a question…". The organization answers with the activity in front of them.

You don't need to book first. If an activity has no row yet, use the organization's **Message** button.

Some organizations aren't taking payment through Famlo for a while. Their activity then shows a box such as "Sunnyside Learning Co. takes payment directly", with an **Ask about a spot** button. Selecting it opens a conversation with the organization, and nobody charges you anything. If you aren't signed in, you sign in first so they can reply to you.

## Start a new conversation

1. Select **New conversation**, the pencil button at the top of the inbox.
2. In the **New Conversation** box, type at least two letters of a name.
3. Choose a result.

Search finds:

- Organizations, by name.
- People you already have a one-to-one conversation with.

It doesn't find families you have never talked to. You can't start a new one-to-one conversation with another family on Famlo. Conversations you already have keep working.

Choosing someone you already talk to reopens that conversation rather than starting a second one.

## Inside a conversation

- **Who sees it.** A line above the box confirms it, for example "Replies are private between you and Sunnyside Learning Co.".
- **Show details.** On an activity conversation, **Show details** at the top opens a side panel with the date, the place and what you booked. It needs a wide screen. Select **Hide details** to close it.
- **Jump to latest.** If you scroll up through older messages, **Jump to latest** takes you back to the newest.
- **The time there.** If you write to an organization before 8 AM or from 9 PM onward where it is, a note above the box tells you the time there, for example "It's 9:40 PM for Sunnyside Learning Co.". You can still send. They will see it when they're next on.

## Send photos

Select the photo button at the bottom left of the message box, then choose your pictures.

- Up to 4 photos in one message.
- JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 10 MB each.
- Location data is removed from photos. Only the people in the conversation can see them.

You can send photos without any text. Photos can't be added to a support ticket.

## React to a message

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/families/messages-menu.webp" alt="A conversation with the menu next to a message open, showing six reactions, Report message and Block this person" url="famlo.co/messages" width={1280} height={800} />

1. Select **…** (1) next to a message.
2. Pick a reaction from the row at the top: 👍 ❤️ 🎉 😂 😮 🙏.

On a computer you can also use the smiley button beside the message.

Your reaction appears under the message with a count. Select it again to take it back. Reactions show counts only, not who reacted, and they don't send anyone a notification.

The same menu has **Report message**, and in a one-to-one conversation **Block this person**. See [Report a message or block someone](/docs/families/community/blocking-and-reporting).

## Archive a conversation

Archiving takes a row out of your inbox without deleting anything.

- **On a computer:** point at the row and select the archive button at its right edge.
- **On a phone:** swipe the row to the left.

To see archived conversations, open **Messaging settings** (the gear), then **Archived**. To bring one back to your inbox, use the same button, or swipe the row, in that list.

If someone writes in an archived conversation, it comes back to your inbox by itself, so you don't miss it.

## Health and medical details

Famlo doesn't send a message that reads like a health record, for example a line such as "Allergies: peanuts" or "Medication - inhaler". Instead you see that your message "can't include health, medical, allergy, or emergency information — please add it to your family profile instead, where it stays private."

Put those details in [your family profile](/docs/families/your-family). Organizations you book with see what they need there.

Ordinary questions are fine. You can still ask "Is Friday's snack nut-free?".

## Organizations that haven't claimed their listing

Some organizations are listed on Famlo but nobody from them has claimed the listing yet. Their page has no **Message** button. If you reach a conversation with one, you see **There's nobody here to reply** in place of the message box, along with their website when Famlo has it. Contact them there.

## Support tickets

When you open a ticket with Famlo Support, it appears in your inbox, and the support team's replies arrive there too. The box reads "Reply to support…", and only you and the support team can see the ticket. To open a ticket, see [Getting help](/docs/account/support).

## Emails about your messages

| Email | When it comes | Can you turn it off? |
|---|---|---|
| "New message from" the sender's name | Someone sends you a one-to-one message | Yes, with **Messages & followers** |
| "You have N unread conversations on Famlo" | Once a day, if new messages are waiting unread | Yes, with **Messages & followers** |
| "Sunnyside Learning Co. replied about" the activity, or "replied to your question" | An organization answers you | No. Answers to your questions always arrive |
| The organization's name, then the subject | An organization sends an announcement | Yes, with **Activity updates** |

Change these in **Account settings** › **Notifications**, or from **Messaging settings** › **Notification settings** in the inbox. See [Notifications](/docs/account/notifications).

### Reply by email

You can answer a "New message from" email, or an email saying an organization replied to you, by replying to it. Your reply goes into the conversation, and the organization sees it in its inbox.

- Send it from the same email address you use for Famlo. Replies from other addresses don't reach the conversation.
- Quoted text and signatures such as "Sent from my iPhone" are removed.
- Up to 4 photos come through. Other attachments are dropped, and the message says so.
- Each email's reply address works for 90 days.

You can also use the button in the organization's email (**View and reply**, or **Open your messages** if the organization has its own website) and write your reply in your inbox.

## On an organization's own website

If an organization has its own website, its pages have a bell next to **Parent portal**. It opens your messages with that organization only. Anything you read there is marked read on famlo.co too. See [Contacting an organization, and emails from its website](/docs/families/organization-websites/messages-and-emails).

## Troubleshooting

**My message won't send and mentions health information.**
Part of it looks like a medical or emergency form field. Rewrite it as a sentence, or put the detail in your family profile.

**It says I'm "sending messages too quickly".**
Famlo limits how many messages, searches and reactions you can send in a few minutes. Wait a minute and try again.

**An archived conversation is back in my inbox.**
Someone wrote in it. Archive it again when you're done.

**I can't find someone in New conversation.**
Search finds organizations and people you already talk to. Use the **Message** button on a new organization's page. You can't start a conversation with a family you haven't talked to before.

**There's no box to type in.**
The organization hasn't claimed its listing (**There's nobody here to reply**), or a block is in place ("You can't reply to this conversation."). Or the conversation was closed. You can still read everything in it.

**I don't see an activity I booked.**
Its row appears once there's something on it. To ask something now, use **Message** on the organization's page.

**I'm not getting emails about messages.**
Check that **Email** is ticked for **Messages & followers** in **Account settings** › **Notifications**.
