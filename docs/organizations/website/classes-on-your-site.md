# How classes show on your site

Famlo fills your website's Classes page from the classes you run, so there's nothing to type in. In the on-page editor you choose how they look: the card style, what each card shows, how the page is grouped and which filters parents can use. You need the [Website plan](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans) and **Edit settings** on the **School settings** line, which Owners and Co-leaders have.

## Change how your class cards look

1. Open **Website** in the console, then select **Edit your website**.
2. Under **Pages** on the left, select **Classes** (1). The editor shows your real Classes page, and the **Classes page** panel opens on the right.
3. Stay on the **Cards** tab (2).
4. Pick a **Card style**, a choice for **When a parent clicks a card**, a **Group by** order and the details under **On each card**. The page in the middle redraws as you go.
5. When you're happy, select **Publish**.

:::note
Everything on the **Cards** and **Filters** tabs is a draft change, like the rest of the editor. Families see it only after you publish. See [Publish, schedule and restore versions](/docs/organizations/website/publishing-and-history).

## What each card setting does

**Card style.** The editor shows only the styles your site's look can draw, so you may see fewer than these.

| Card style | What families see |
|---|---|
| **Photo grid** | "Big photos, three across." |
| **Compact list** | "One line per class. Best for a long schedule." |
| **Side-by-side** | "Photo beside the details, two across." |
| **Weekly timetable** | "Monday to Friday in columns. Classes on set dates are listed under it." |
| **Detail cards** | "A photo with the price on its corner, and the facts in a small grid." |
| **Coloured cards** | "Each subject gets its own soft colour. No photo needed." |
| **By date** | "A row per class under its date, the date large on the left." |
| **Photo cards** | "The photo fills the card, with the title over it." |
| **Poster cards** | "The subject in big type, with the title and facts under it." |

**When a parent clicks a card.**

- **Register button** (the starting choice): each card has a **Register** button that goes to sign-in and checkout on your site.
- **Open the class page**: there's no button. The whole card opens the class page, where families see the full details before they book.

**On each card.** Tick the details you want: **Photo**, **Day and time**, **Weather**, **Ages**, **Place**, **Price**, **Seats left**, **Teacher**, **Subject**, **Weeks** and **Register by**. The class title always shows. At first, Photo, Day and time, Weather, Ages, Place, Price and Seats left are ticked.

**Weather** is the forecast for in-person classes this week. When there's no forecast for a class, its card simply leaves it out. More in [Spanish, weather, branded emails and link previews](/docs/organizations/website/extras).

## Choose how the Classes page is grouped

**Group by** sets how your Classes page opens: **Category**, **A–Z**, **Teacher**, **Town**, **Day** or **Date**. It starts on **Category**.

- Families can still switch the grouping with **Show by** at the top of the page. They always get **Category**, **Day** and **Town**, plus your own choice if it's another one.
- **Category**, **Teacher** and **Town** need your classes to differ on them. If every class is in one town, for example, grouping by town won't take, and the page opens by category (or by day) instead.
- With the **Weekly timetable** card style, the page is laid out by weekday, so **Group by** and **Show by** don't apply.

## Choose the filters parents can use

Filters sit at the top of your Classes page. Parents use them to narrow the list, for example to classes for a 4th grader on Tuesdays.

1. In the editor, with the **Classes** page open, select the **Filters** tab (1).
2. Turn each filter on or off with its switch.
3. Select **Publish** when you're ready.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-classes-on-your-site-filters.webp" alt="The Filters tab of the Classes page panel, with the Town filter highlighted and its line Hidden for now: all your classes are in one town" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/editor" width={343} height={944} dark={false} />

| Filter | What it does for parents | On at first |
|---|---|---|
| **Your child’s grade** | Shows only the classes that fit the grade they pick | Yes |
| **Town** | Shows only the classes in the town they pick | Yes |
| **Day of the week** | Picks the day that works for their family | Yes |
| **Time of day** | Mornings, afternoons or evenings | No |
| **Starts in** | Picks the month they want to start | No |
| **Subject** | Narrows the list to one subject | Yes |
| **Instructor** | Finds every class one teacher runs | No |
| **Pays with Step Up / ESA** | "Only the classes parents can pay for with their scholarship." | Yes |
| **Has open seats** | "Hides full classes." | Yes |

### Hidden for now

A filter only shows when your classes differ on it. A filter that's on but can't show yet says **Hidden for now** and the reason (2), for example "all your classes are in one town", "none of your classes take Step Up / ESA" or "none of your classes is full". You don't need to do anything. It appears by itself once your classes differ, such as when you add a class in a second town.

On a phone, parents open the filters with **Filters**. A filtered page has its own web address, so you can share a link to it, such as your Tuesday classes.

## Classes on your homepage

Your homepage has its own card settings.

1. In the editor, open **Home**.
2. Select the page name at the top of the editor, then **Page settings…**.
3. Select the **Class cards** tab.
4. Choose the **Card style**, **When a parent clicks a card** and **On each card**, and how many classes the homepage lists under **Classes on the homepage**: 3, 4, 6, 9 or 12. It starts at 6.

A **Classes** section on another page sets its own number. See [Sections you can add](/docs/organizations/website/sections).

## Which classes are listed on your site

Whether a class appears on your website is set on the class itself, not in the website editor. Open the class in **Classes**, then **Who can see it** in the **Everything else** card. See [Create and publish a class](/docs/organizations/activities).

| Who can see it | On your website's Classes page | In Famlo search |
|---|---|---|
| **Famlo + your website** | Listed | Listed |
| **Your website only** | Listed | Not listed |
| **Link only** | Not listed, but its link still opens | Not listed |
| **Members only** | Not listed | Shown only to your members when they're signed in |

A class also has to be published (not a draft) and still to come, or a repeating class that's still running. Because these are class settings, a change shows on your site without publishing the website.

## Troubleshooting

**I changed my cards, but families still see the old ones.**
Card and filter changes are drafts. Select **Publish** in the editor's top bar.

**A filter I turned on isn't on my site.**
Look under it on the **Filters** tab. If it says **Hidden for now**, your classes don't differ on it yet, so there's nothing to narrow.

**A class is missing from my Classes page.**
Check that it's published, that **Who can see it** is **Famlo + your website** or **Your website only**, and that it hasn't already finished.

**My Group by choice isn't how the page opens.**
Your classes may not differ on it (one town, one teacher or one subject), or you're using **Weekly timetable**, which is laid out by weekday.
