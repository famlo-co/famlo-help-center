# Set up your website

Website setup walks you through five short steps: pick a look, add your logo and colors, choose a welcome photo, say how families reach you, then publish. It takes about five minutes. Your classes, teachers and reviews are already on the site, so you only add what makes it yours. You need the [Website plan](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans) and **Edit settings** on the **School settings** line, which Owners and Co-leaders have.

## Start website setup

1. Open **Website** in the console. You land on the **Pages** tab.
2. Find the **Set up your website · about 5 minutes** card beside your page list (under it on a phone).
3. Select **Start** (1).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-setup-wizard-start.webp" alt="The Set up your website card with the Start button highlighted and the list of what the site still needs open below it" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website" width={352} height={799} />

Setup is never forced on you. You can build your site in the [on-page editor](/docs/organizations/website/editor) instead, or come back to setup at any time.

## The welcome screen

The welcome screen, **Let’s build your website**, shows two lists before you begin.

- **What you’ll need**: your logo ("No logo yet? We’ll use your name."), one good photo, your phone, address and hours, and social links if you want them.
- **Already done for you**: your **Classes, seats and prices**, **Teachers and bios**, **Reviews from families**, and **Booking and checkout**, with a count or **Ready** for each. If one shows **None yet**, the link beside it (**Add a class**, **Add a teacher**, **See reviews** or **Set up payments**) opens that part of the console in a new tab. These update on your site by themselves when you change them in Famlo.

Select **Start with a look** to begin, or **I’ll do this later** to go back to **Website**.

## The five steps

Each step saves as you go, and the top bar says **Saved**. On a computer, a **Live preview** of your homepage sits beside each step, with buttons to see it at desktop, tablet and phone size. Nothing changes for families until you publish.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-setup-wizard-look.webp" alt="Step 1 of website setup, Pick a look, with the five steps and the Save and exit button highlighted in the top bar" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/setup/look" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

The steps across the top (1) show where you are. Select any of them to move to that step. **Save and exit** (2) keeps your progress.

1. **Look.** Pick one of ten looks, or **Original (the Famlo design)**. The chips above the cards (**Co-op**, **Microschool**, **Nature** and more) filter them. **Full preview** opens your own site drawn in that look in a new tab. Select **Use** and the look's name to carry on. If you already chose colors of your own, Famlo asks whether to use the new look's own colors or **Keep my colours**.
2. **Logo & colours.** Select **Upload a logo** (PNG, JPG, WEBP or SVG, up to 2 MB). Famlo then suggests up to three color sets marked **From your logo**. You can also pick one of the look's own palettes, or **Choose my own colours** (**Background**, **Text**, **Accent**, **Soft panels**, and **Second accent** on every look except Original). If your logo already spells out your name, tick **My logo already says our name (hide the name next to it)**. Choose a **Font pairing** last. Original has **Headings font** and **Body font** instead.
3. **Photos.** Choose **Your welcome photo**, the first thing families see. Pick one under **From your classes**, or **Upload** your own (PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 10 MB). Kids busy doing something, faces in good light, in landscape, work best.
4. **Details.** Under **How families reach you**, enter the **Phone**, **Email**, **Address** and **Hours** shown in your header, footer and Contact page. The first time, these are filled in from your Famlo listing, so remove anything you don't want public. Tick **We meet in different places (show the town only)** to hide your street address. Under **Social links** (optional), add Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
5. **Publish.** **Check and publish** shows your web address and what your site still needs. Select **Publish my site** (see the next section).

Select **Continue** at the bottom of each step to move on, or **Back** to return to the one before. On a phone, the step shows as "2 of 5" at the top, and **Preview my site** opens the preview.

The logo you add here is your website's logo. It doesn't change the logo on your Famlo listing.

## Check and publish

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-setup-wizard-publish.webp" alt="Step 5, Check and publish, showing Your web address, the Before you publish list and the Publish my site button" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/setup/publish" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

The last step has up to three parts.

- **Your web address.** Your site goes online at a web address you own, like yourcoop.org. There is no free famlo.co address for it. With no address yet, select **Connect a domain I own**. With one, **Check your domain** opens the **Domain** tab in a new tab. See [Connect your own domain](/docs/organizations/website/custom-domain).
- **Before you publish.** The readiness checklist (see [The Your website checklist](#the-your-website-checklist)), with a link beside each item. "You can publish now and finish the rest later."
- **Also going live.** If anyone on your team changed anything in the on-page editor that isn't published yet, it's listed here. Those changes publish with your setup.

Select **Publish my site**. The last screen depends on your address:

| Your web address | What you see |
|---|---|
| Connected | **Your website is live**, with your address, **Copy**, **Open my site**, **Share** and a QR code to "Print the QR code for your sign-in table or flyers." |
| Still connecting | **Your website is published**. "It goes online as soon as" your address connects, with **Check your domain**. |
| None yet | **Your website is published**. "It goes online when you connect your own web address", with **Connect your web address**. |

Under **What’s next**, **Edit your homepage** and **Add a page** open the [on-page editor](/docs/organizations/website/editor). **Back to Website** returns to the console.

## Save and exit, and running setup again

**Save and exit** in the top bar saves the step you're on and returns you to **Website**: "Your setup is saved. Pick up where you left off any time." Nothing is live yet.

To pick up again, select **Continue setup** on the **Set up your website** card. The welcome screen offers **Continue where you left off** with the step's name, or **Start again from the look**.

Once you've published through setup, the card is replaced by **Run website setup again** under the checklist on the **Pages** tab. It walks the same five steps, starting from your current look, logo, photo and details. Nothing changes for families until you select **Publish my site** again.

## The Your website checklist

The checklist shows what your site still needs, read from your real data. It's advice only and never stops you publishing. You'll find it on the **Pages** tab of **Website** and in the **Before you publish** part of the last step.

| Done | Still to do |
|---|---|
| **Your logo** | **Add your logo** |
| **Look:** and your look's name | **Pick a look** |
| **A welcome photo on your homepage** | **Add a welcome photo** |
| **Your homepage shows your classes** | **Show your classes on your homepage** |
| The number of classes families can book | **Add a class** |
| The number of teachers on your site | **Show a teacher on your site** |
| **How families reach you** | **Add a phone number or email** |
| **Online at** and your address | **Connect your own web address** |
| **Spanish written for every published page** (only if your site offers Spanish) | **Write the Spanish for** a page |
| **Your latest changes are live** | **You have changes that aren't live yet** |

On the **Pages** tab, the card changes as you go:

- Before setup, the **Set up your website** card holds the list under **What your site still needs**, with a count of how many are done.
- After setup, the card is headed **Your website** with the count, and lists only what's still open. A link such as **Show the 3 done** opens the rest.
- When everything is done, the card reads **Everything your site needs is in place.** Select **Show list** to see it all.

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Website in the console.**
**Website** shows only when your organization has the Website plan (or already has a site) and you have **Edit settings** on the **School settings** line. See [Famlo plans](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans).

**A banner says "Someone else changed your website just now. We’ve reloaded it."**
Someone else on your team saved a change to your website while you were working. The step reloads with the latest version. Check it, and make your last change again if it's missing.

**My class photos don't show under From your classes.**
"Your classes don’t have photos yet." Upload one instead, or add photos to a class and come back to this step.

**My photo is too big.**
Photos can be up to 10 MB. Pick a smaller photo, or one of your class photos. Logos can be up to 2 MB.

**Publishing says "Your site couldn’t be published yet:"**
The list under it names what needs fixing, with the page and section where it is. Fix it in the [on-page editor](/docs/organizations/website/editor), then come back to the **Publish** step.

**Publishing says "Another publish is running. Try again in a moment."**
Someone else on your team is publishing. Wait a moment and select **Publish my site** again.

**I published, but my site isn't online.**
Your site needs a web address you own. See [Connect your own domain](/docs/organizations/website/custom-domain) and [Domain troubleshooting](/docs/organizations/website/domain-troubleshooting).
