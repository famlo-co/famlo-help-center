# Sections you can add

Your homepage and the pages you add are built from sections: a welcome at the top, a heading and text, your classes, a photo gallery, a contact form, and more. This page lists every section, what you can fill in, and the limits on photos and files. You need the **Website** plan and **School settings** set to **Edit settings**.

## Add a section

Sections go on your homepage and on pages you add. Famlo fills your Classes and teachers pages by itself, so they don't take sections.

1. Open **Website**, then select **Edit your website**.
2. Select the section you want the new one to follow, on the page or in the list on the left.
3. Select the plus button that appears under it. On an empty page, select **Add a section** instead.
4. Choose a group on the left, or type in **Search sections**. The badge beside the title shows where the section will go, such as **After “Numbers”**.
5. Select a card, then **Add section**.
6. Fill in the section on its **Content** tab. Like every editor change, it's a draft until you publish.

The previews are drawn in your site's look. A card marked **One per page** can't be added because the page already has one.

## Content and style

Select any section, on the page or in the list on the left (1), and the panel on the right shows its **Content** tab (2) and its **Style** tab (3). **Content** holds the fields listed on this page. If the section is filled from elsewhere in Famlo, a note at the top says where to change it. **Style** holds the layout, background, spacing, **Hide on phones** and a **Link name**. See [Edit your website on the page](/docs/organizations/website/editor).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-sections-inspector.webp" alt="The website editor with the Numbers section selected in the page list as 1, and the Content tab as 2 and the Style tab as 3 in the right-hand panel, which shows the note that Famlo counts these numbers, a Heading field and four number rows" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/editor" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

Most sections have an optional **Heading** of up to 120 characters. Put *asterisks* around a word to make it stand out, in the style you pick under **Emphasized words** on the **Style** tab.

## Sections you write

You type the words in these sections yourself. Numbers in brackets are the most characters a field takes.

| Section | Group | What you fill in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Welcome (hero)** | Welcome | "The big welcome at the top of a page." **Small line above the headline** (80), **Headline** (120), **Welcome text** (400), a photo, and two buttons. Leave the first button blank and it says "See upcoming classes" and goes to your classes. One per page. |
| **Call to action** | Welcome | A heading, a line (300), **Button words** (40) and **Button goes to**. The button shows once both are filled in. Layouts: **Full-width band** or **Side by side**. |
| **Heading and text** | Info | A heading and a few paragraphs (up to 5,000 characters). |
| **Text and photo** | Info | A small line above the heading (60), a heading, paragraphs, a photo, **Photo side** and **Photo shape**. |
| **About us** | Trust | "Who you are. Uses your Famlo description until you write your own." Up to 10,000 characters. |
| **What we believe** | Trust | An intro (300) and up to 6 points, each with a small picture from a set list, a title (80) and a line (300). Layouts: **Grid** or **One row**. |
| **Quote** | People | The quote (400) and **Who said it** (80). It isn't drawn from your testimonials. Layouts: **Big** or **Plain**. |
| **Questions and answers** | Info | Up to 30 questions (200 each), each with an answer (2,000). |
| **Photo gallery** | Info | Up to 24 photos, with **Photo shape** set to **Square** for a tidy grid or **Original** to keep each photo whole. Visitors tap a photo to see it larger. |
| **Video** | Info | "A YouTube or Vimeo video. It loads only when a visitor presses play." **Video link**, a caption (200) and a cover photo. Layouts: **Full width** or **Inset**. |
| **Files to download** | Info | Up to 20 files, each with a title, a description (160) and the file. |
| **Button** | Info | "A single button linking anywhere." **Button text** (40) and **Link**. |
| **Price list** | Shop | Up to 12 price cards, each with a name, a price (30), **Per** (60), a note (240) and an optional button, plus **Note under the prices** (400). You type the prices as text, and nothing is charged from this section. |

## Sections Famlo fills for you

These sections show what's already in Famlo and update by themselves. You choose the heading, how many to show and how they look.

| Section | Group | Where it comes from, and what you set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classes** | Classes | Your upcoming classes. **Show**: **All**, or only some towns, categories or teachers. **How many**: 3, 4, 6, 9 or 12, or **Same as homepage**. **Card style**, and **When there are no classes**: **Hide the section** or **Say new classes are coming**. **On each card** applies to every class list on your homepage. |
| **Weekly timetable** | Classes | A **Classes** section that starts as a timetable: "Monday to Friday, every weekly class in its column." It shows every weekly class. Only offered in looks that draw a timetable. |
| **Next class** | Classes | "The next class that still has seats, in the spotlight." It changes by itself as classes fill and pass. |
| **Programs by age** | Classes | Up to 6 age groups, each with a **Name** (60), **From age** and **To age** (0 to 18) and **One line about it** (200). Each links to its classes with a live count. Layouts: **Soft cards**, **Color blocks**, **Bubbles** or **Columns**. |
| **Numbers** | Trust | Up to 4 numbers Famlo counts: **Classes this term**, **Teachers**, **Families booked recently** and **Year we started** (you type the year). **Words under it** is optional. A number that comes to zero isn't shown, and **Families booked recently** is hidden until it reaches 10. |
| **Instructors** | People | Cards linking to your teachers' profiles. "These come from your People: everyone shown on your website." **How many** (1 to 24, default 4), **Card style**, and switches for **Photo**, **Bio** and **Classes they teach**. Teachers' contact details are never shown. |
| **Testimonials** | People | Testimonials you published to your website. **Show**: featured, newest, or those about one class. **How many**: 1 to 12. See [Testimonials](/docs/organizations/communication/testimonials). |
| **Programs we accept** | Trust | "The scholarship programs families can pay with, from your settings." |
| **From the shop** | Shop | Downloads and videos from your **Store**, with their prices. **How many**: 1 to 12. Layouts: **Cards** or **One row**. See [Sell downloads, videos and bundles in your Store](/docs/organizations/running/store). |
| **Contact details** | Info | Your address, phone, email, hours and social links from **Website** › **Details**. **Show a map** works only when your site shows a street address. Layouts: **Side by side** or **One card**. |

A section Famlo fills shows nothing on your site while it has nothing to show, such as **Next class** when every class is full. Your page doesn't get an empty heading.

## Sign-up sections

| Section | Group | What it does, and what you fill in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contact form** | Sign-ups | "A short form that sends a message to your inbox." Families type their first name, email and a message, and it arrives in **Messages**. You set an intro (300) and **Starts the message with** (80), so you can tell at a glance what a message is about. See [Your team inbox and quick replies](/docs/organizations/communication/messages-inbox). |
| **Email signup** | Sign-ups | "A box where families join your mailing list." An intro (300). Layouts: **Full-width band** or **Card**. See [Your mailing list](/docs/organizations/communication/mailing-list). |

## The photo panel

Select a photo on the page, then **Crop** or **Replace**, to open the photo panel.

- **Replace with** (or **Add a photo from**): **Upload** a photo, or pick from **Class photos**, the photos already on your classes. A class photo is copied onto the page and the class keeps its own.
- **Focus point**: drag the dot to the part of the photo that must stay in view when your look crops it ("Drag the dot to keep faces in view"). Arrow keys move it too.
- **Shape on the page**: **Automatic**, **Original**, **4:5**, **Square** or **3:4**. A **Text and photo** section offers **Original**, **Wide** or **Square**.
- **Frame**: **Automatic**, **Tile**, **Arch**, **Circle**, **Blob** or **None**. **Cut-out (needs a transparent PNG)** lets a photo with a see-through background stand on the page with no tile.
- **Describe the photo**: up to 160 characters, read aloud by screen readers. "Leave blank if the photo is only decoration."

Some looks set the shape or frame themselves. The panel then says, for example, "This look sets the shape and frame of this photo."

In a **Photo gallery**, each photo gets a focus point and a description but no shape or frame of its own. **Add photos** puts new ones at the end of the gallery, and **Remove from gallery** takes one out.

## Upload limits

| What | Types | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | JPEG, PNG or WebP | Up to 10 MB |
| Files in **Files to download** | PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, CSV or a photo | Up to 10 MB |

Famlo removes hidden details such as the location from photos you upload. Photos and files stay out of sight until you publish. On a members-only page, they're sent only to people who can open that page. See [Pages, your menu, and who can see each page](/docs/organizations/website/pages-and-menu).

There's no separate photo library. Each photo belongs to the section you added it to.

## What isn't offered

Sections keep to plain fields, and your look does the design. On purpose, there's no:

- custom code, HTML or CSS;
- choosing fonts or colors for one section (they come from your look; see [Your site's look](/docs/organizations/website/look-and-brand));
- stock photos or background removal;
- links set to open in a new tab;
- video from anywhere other than YouTube or Vimeo.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't add a second Welcome (hero).** Each page takes one. The card shows "One per page — this page already has one." Use **Call to action** or **Text and photo** for a second big moment.

**A section I added doesn't show on my site.** Check two things. Editor changes appear only after you publish. And a section Famlo fills shows nothing while it has nothing to show, for example **Testimonials** before you've published any to your website.

**My video link is refused with "Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link."** The section takes YouTube and Vimeo links only. Copy the address from the video's page, like https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…

**My photo won't upload.** "That photo is bigger than 10 MB." means you need a smaller copy. "That isn't a photo we can use. Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo." means the format isn't accepted. Save the photo as a JPEG and try again.
