# Edit your website on the page

The website editor shows your real site and lets you change it where you see it: click a heading to retype it, click a photo to replace it, click a button to change where it goes. Everything you do saves to a draft as you go, and families keep seeing your published site until you publish. You need the [Website plan](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans) and **School settings** set to **Edit settings**, which Owners and Co-leaders have.

## Open the editor

There are three ways in:

- In your console, open **Website** and select **Edit your website**. It's on every **Website** tab.
- On **Website** › **Pages**, select a page's name to open the editor on that page.
- On your live site, select **Edit this page** (see [Edit from your live site](#edit-from-your-live-site)).

To leave, select the red Famlo mark at the top left (**Done** on a phone). You go back to **Website** › **Pages**. Your draft is kept.

## Find your way around

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-editor-layout.webp" alt="The website editor with the page switcher in the top bar as 1, the Pages list on the left as 2, the homepage draft in the middle as 3, and the panel for the selected section on the right as 4" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/editor" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

The editor has four parts:

- **The top bar.** The page switcher (1) shows the page you're on. Open it to jump to another page, or for **Page settings…** and **Add page**. The rest of the bar holds the preview sizes, undo and redo, the save status, **Preview draft**, **View live page**, **History** and **Publish**.
- **The left side (2).** Under **Pages** are all your pages, with the sections of the page you're on listed beneath it, top to bottom. Select a section's name to jump to it. Under **Site** are **Theme** and **Site settings** (**Website** › **Details**), which open those console tabs, and at the foot **Help & shortcuts**.
- **The page (3).** Your site as families will see it once you publish, drawn from your draft. The strip above it says "Editing a draft · families see the published site".
- **The panel (4).** It opens on the right when you select something, and shows that thing's settings. Close it with the **×**.

On a phone, the editor has no left side: use the page switcher to move between pages. Tapping something opens its panel from the bottom of the screen, and you type there rather than on the page.

### Preview sizes

The three buttons in the middle of the top bar, **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone**, redraw the page at that width so you can check how it looks on each. They don't change your site.

### Undo and redo

Use the arrows in the top bar, or press ⌘Z to undo and ⌘⇧Z to redo (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z on Windows). While you're typing in a box, those keys undo your typing instead. Undo only covers what you did in this browser tab since you opened it; reloading the page clears it. To go back further, restore an older version from **History** (see [Publish, schedule and restore versions](/docs/organizations/website/publishing-and-history)).

## Change words, photos and buttons

Click any text, photo or button on the page to change it.

- **A line of text**, such as a heading or a button's words: type straight on the page. A counter shows how many characters you've used. Press Enter (or click elsewhere) when you're done, or Esc to put the line back as it was.
- **A paragraph**: clicking it opens the section's panel, where you type in the box.
- **Emphasized words**: in many headings, putting \*asterisks\* around a word makes it stand out, for example in your accent color. While you edit such a heading, **Emphasize word** does the same for the words you've selected.
- **A photo**: select **Replace** or **Crop** to open the photo panel. See [Sections you can add](/docs/organizations/website/sections) for photo options and upload limits.
- **A button**: opens the button panel. See [Buttons and links](#buttons-and-links).

The header, footer and announcement bar are on every page, so they aren't changed here. Clicking them tells you where they are changed: your logo and colors on **Website** › **Theme** and **Logo & icon**, the announcement bar, contact details and footer in **Site settings** (**Website** › **Details**). Those tabs save straight to your live site, not to the draft. See [Your site's look](/docs/organizations/website/look-and-brand). Your menu is made from your pages; see [Pages, your menu, and who can see each page](/docs/organizations/website/pages-and-menu).

Your **Classes** page and your teachers page are filled in by Famlo, from your classes and from **People**. Change what's on them there. In the editor you can rename them or hide them from the menu, and on the **Classes** page choose how class cards and filters look. See [How classes show on your site](/docs/organizations/website/classes-on-your-site).

## Work with sections

Each page is a stack of sections: a welcome, a heading and text, a photo gallery, your classes, questions and answers, and so on. For the full list, see [Sections you can add](/docs/organizations/website/sections).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-editor-section.webp" alt="A selected section in the website editor, with its toolbar of move, duplicate, background, hide on phones and delete buttons as 1, and the panel's Content and Style tabs as 2, showing the Style tab" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/editor" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

When you select a section, a toolbar (1) appears on it and its panel opens with two tabs (2):

- **Content**: the section's words, photos, list items and choices.
- **Style**: **Layout** (only the layouts your look draws), **Background** (**Automatic**, **Page**, **Soft**, **Dark** or **Accent**, all from your look's colors), **Space around**, **Hide on phones**, **Link name** and, where it applies, **Emphasized words**.

### Add a section

1. Point between two sections and select the plus button that appears, or the plus button under the section you have selected. On an empty page, select **Add a section**.
2. Find the section you want. Browse by group, or type in **Search sections**. The previews use your look.
3. Select it, then select **Add section**.

The new section goes in where you chose and is selected, ready to fill in. A section that a page can only have one of, such as the welcome, shows "One per page" once the page has it.

### Move, copy or delete a section

1. Select the section.
2. On its toolbar, select **Move up** or **Move down** (the arrows), **Duplicate**, or **Delete** (the bin).
3. If you chose **Delete**, confirm with **Delete section**, or select **Keep it**.

You can also drag a section by its handle in the list on the left, or select the handle and use the arrow keys. The **⋯** menu at the top of the section's panel has the same actions.

A deleted section comes off the page when you publish. Before then, undo brings it back. After you publish, you can bring it back by restoring an older version from **History**.

## Buttons and links

Select a button on the page to open its panel. **Button text** is its words, and **Goes to** shows where it leads. Select **Change** (or **Choose** if it doesn't go anywhere yet) to pick a destination.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/website-editor-link-picker.webp" alt="The Link to box, with the kinds of destination listed on the left as 1 (Page on your site, A class, A teacher, A resource, Phone number, Email, Outside link) and the Done button as 2" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/website/editor" width={1280} height={800} dark={false} />

1. Select the button on the page.
2. In its panel, select **Change** or **Choose**.
3. Pick the kind of destination (1): **Page on your site**, **A class**, **A teacher**, **A resource**, **Phone number**, **Email** or **Outside link**. You can also type in **Search pages, classes, teachers…**.
4. Select the destination, or type the phone number, email address or web address.
5. Select **Done** (2).

Some things to know:

- **Link to things, not addresses.** When you choose a page, class or teacher from the list, the button keeps working if that page is renamed or the class moves. Use **Outside link** only for other websites. It needs the full address, starting https://.
- **Link to a section.** Give a section a **Link name** on its **Style** tab. It then shows in the list under its page, so a button can jump straight to it.
- **Phone number** lets families tap to call you. **Email** opens their email, addressed to you.
- **Remove link** clears the destination. A button with nowhere to go is hidden from your site.
- On the welcome section, a button left blank says your look's own words (such as "See upcoming classes") and goes to your classes.
- Your look decides how buttons look.

## Saving, and editing with others

There's no Save button. Changes save a moment after you stop typing. The status beside the undo arrows tells you where your draft stands. On narrower screens it's a colored dot; point at it to see the words.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Saving…** | Your latest change is on its way. |
| **Saved · draft** | Saved in your draft. Families don't see it until you publish. |
| **Up to date** | Your draft matches your live site. **Publish** is grayed out. |
| **Not saved** · **Retry** | The editor couldn't reach Famlo. It tries again by itself; select **Retry** to try now. |
| **Scheduled · Mon, Oct 5, 7:00 AM** | Your changes are set to publish at that time. |

Your organization has one shared draft, so everyone who edits the site works on the same one. When someone else has the editor open, the status adds "Sam is editing too" (with their name). If you both change the same thing, the later change wins and you see "Updated with Sam's changes." If someone changes a page you're rearranging, the editor shows "Someone else just changed this page. Showing their changes." Make your change again.

## Preview your draft

Select **Preview draft** to open the page you're on in a new tab, just as families will see it once you publish, with the note "Preview of your draft · families still see the published site". Links in the preview don't go anywhere. Families can't see the preview.

**View live page** opens the published version of the page, as families see it now. It shows only when the page is published and your site has a web address.

When you're happy with the draft, select **Publish**. See [Publish, schedule and restore versions](/docs/organizations/website/publishing-and-history).

## Edit from your live site

When you're signed in on your own website, a round **Edit this page** button appears in the bottom right corner of its pages. Only people who can edit your site see it; families never do.

| On this page of your site | **Edit this page** opens |
| --- | --- |
| Your homepage or a page you made | The editor, on that page |
| **Classes** | The editor's **Classes** page, with your card and filter settings |
| A single class | That class in **Classes**, in your console |
| Your teachers page | **People**, in your console |
| Any other page | **Website** › **Pages**, in your console |

Your **Parent portal** on the site also shows a bar for you and your team: "You run this website. Only you and your team see this bar." with **Edit your website** and a link to your console.

## Troubleshooting

**The editor says "You were signed out".** Your Famlo session ended. Sign in again in a new tab, then come back and select **Try again**. If a change didn't save, the box shows it so you can copy it.

**The editor says "Your site was just published".** Someone else on your team published while you were editing. Select **Reload** to carry on from the published version. Any change that didn't save is shown so you can copy it.

**A note says some things "changed outside the editor since your draft started".** Someone changed a setting such as your colors or contact details on a **Website** tab while a draft was open. Your draft keeps its own version of those things. Check them before you publish.

**Publish is grayed out.** There's nothing new to publish: your draft matches your live site.

**I can't find View live page.** The page hasn't been published yet, or your site doesn't have a web address yet. See [Connect your own domain](/docs/organizations/website/custom-domain).

**I don't see Edit this page on my site.** Sign in on your website (not only on famlo.co) with the account you use to run your organization. Your role needs **School settings** set to **Edit settings**.
