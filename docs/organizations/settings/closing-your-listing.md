# Closing your listing

Closing your listing schedules your organization for permanent deletion after a **30-day grace period**, or a **90-day grace period** if your organization holds any signed agreements. During the grace period nothing is lost and you can call it off. When it ends, everything is removed and can't be recovered.

Only the person who created or claimed the listing (or who accepted a handover of it) can do this. Ticking **Owner** for someone else doesn't give them this power.

## Before you start: save what you need

Once the organization is deleted, its records go with it. Download anything you want to keep while the listing still exists. You can export your parents list, your money ledger, registrations, class rosters, attendance and your mailing list as CSV files. [Export your data (CSV)](/docs/organizations/settings/exports) shows where each one is.

**If you hold signed agreements.** The **Danger zone** tells you how many signed agreements you hold, and that deleting the organization deletes them too, 90 days after you schedule it. That's why your grace period is 90 days instead of 30: it gives you time to select **Download all signed records** and keep a copy. You can download them as many times as you like during those 90 days. Families keep the copies Famlo emailed them. If some of your signed records are on hold, the **Danger zone** says so, and the organization can't be deleted until the hold is released.

You don't need to cancel classes, memberships or your Famlo plan by hand first. On the deletion date, Famlo:

- **Cancels and refunds every class with dates still to come.** Each family gets back what they paid for it by card, and is told. A class that has already finished isn't refunded. If a family is paying for a finished class in installments, the remaining installments are canceled and what they've paid stays with you.
- **Ends dues and program payments**, refunding the unused part of what each family paid by card. Dues a family paid you directly aren't refunded through Famlo.
- **Cancels your paid Famlo plan.** The rest of the current billing period isn't refunded.

If you'd rather tell families yourself, or cancel a class sooner, open the class, then **•••** › **Cancel activity**. See [Cancel, unpublish or delete a class](/docs/organizations/running/cancel-or-delete).

## Schedule the deletion

1. Open **School settings** (1) from the console, then **Danger zone** (2) at the bottom of the settings list.
2. Under **Delete this organization**, select **Schedule deletion** (3).
3. Confirm. The dialog reads "Schedule Sunnyside Learning Co. for deletion in 30 days? You can cancel any time before then.", with your organization's name and, if you hold signed agreements, 90 days.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/closing-your-listing-danger-zone.webp" alt="School settings with Danger zone open, showing Delete this organization and the Schedule deletion button" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/settings/danger" width={1280} height={800} />

That's the whole confirmation. You aren't asked for your password or to type the organization's name. A notice confirms the date, for example "Sunnyside Learning Co. is scheduled for deletion on November 9, 2026." You also get an email.

## What happens straight away

As soon as you schedule it:

- **It's hidden from search.** Your organization and its events no longer show in search or the directory.
- **New joins close.** Nobody new can join or apply. Your page shows a red bar: "**This listing is leaving Famlo.**" It gives the date and says your group "may continue elsewhere — contact the organizers to stay in touch."
- **Families are emailed.** Every member, every family enrolled in one of your programs, and every family registered for a class that still has dates after the deletion date gets an email titled "Sunnyside Learning Co. is leaving Famlo" (with your organization's name). It says the listing is leaving Famlo, not that your group is closing. It tells them:
  - their membership stays active until the date, and their events and programs still go ahead until then;
  - paid memberships and program enrollments end on that date, and the unused part is refunded automatically;
  - registrations for classes that still have dates to come are canceled on that date, and what they paid is refunded automatically;
  - the organizers can still call it off, and Famlo will email them if you do.
- **The Danger zone changes.** It now says "Scheduled for deletion on" with the date, and offers **Cancel scheduled deletion**. It also spells out what happens to your Famlo plan and to families' payments on the date.

## During the grace period

Families who already belong carry on as before, and you keep full control of the console. Reminders go out so the date doesn't arrive unannounced.

| When | Who is reminded |
| --- | --- |
| 7 days before | You, every member, every family enrolled in a program, and every family registered for a class that will be canceled |
| 1 day before | You |

## What happens on the date

- **Paid memberships end.** Every paid membership is cancelled, and the unused part of the period the family already paid for is refunded to them. They don't have to do anything.
- **Program enrollments end.** Every enrollment is cancelled the same way, with the unused part refunded.
- **Classes with dates still to come are canceled.** Every registration is refunded what the family paid by card, and each family is told, the same as when you cancel a class yourself.
- **Your Famlo plan is canceled.** The rest of the current period isn't refunded.
- **The listing is permanently removed**, with everything attached to it: activities, memberships, followers, promo codes, payout settings and any signed agreements. This can happen a day after the date, so families' cancellation notices go out first.
- **You're emailed** to confirm the deletion is complete.

If a registration can't be canceled and refunded automatically, the deletion waits. You get an email titled "Sunnyside Learning Co.'s deletion is waiting on refunds for 1 registration" (with your organization's name and the count). Famlo staff settle those refunds by hand, any payment plan involved is paused so nobody is charged, and the deletion goes ahead once every one is settled. You don't need to do anything, and you can still cancel the deletion in the meantime.

:::danger This can't be undone
Once the grace period ends, there's no way to get the organization or anything in it back. Starting again means creating a new listing and asking everyone to join again.
:::

## Cancel a scheduled deletion

You can stop it at any time during the grace period.

1. Open **School settings**, then **Danger zone**.
2. Select **Cancel scheduled deletion**.

You'll see "Deletion of Sunnyside Learning Co. has been cancelled." Everyone who was emailed that the listing was leaving gets an email that it's staying, and that their membership or program enrollment carries on as before. Your listing goes back to normal. If you ever schedule deletion again, the grace period and the reminders start fresh.

## Things to try instead

Closing is rarely the only option.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/closing-your-listing-visibility.webp" alt="School settings, Joining, with the Visibility editor open showing the Private option and the Published checkbox" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/settings/joining/visibility/edit" width={1280} height={800} />

**Hide it but keep your members.** In **School settings** › **Joining**, edit **Visibility** and choose **Private** (1): "Hidden from search and directories — reachable only by a direct link you share." Select **Save**. Your members keep their access, and you can switch back whenever you like.

**Hide the page from everyone but your members.** In the same editor, untick **Published (page is public)** (2) and select **Save**. Your page is then hidden from the public and from search, and anyone else who opens it gets "not found". Your members and staff can still open it. Tick it again to bring the page back for everyone.

**Stop charging dues.** In **School settings** › **Dues & pricing**, edit **Membership dues**, untick **Enable membership dues** and select **Save**. This turns dues off for anyone joining from now on. It doesn't cancel memberships that are already running. See [Membership dues](/docs/organizations/membership-dues).

**Hand it to someone else.** In **People** › **Staff & teachers**, open a trusted person, select **Change what** *(their name)* **can do**, and tick **Owner**. They can then run every area of the console. Payouts (**Getting paid**) and the **Danger zone** stay with you, the person who created or claimed the listing, until you hand them over too: in **Danger zone**, under **Hand over this organization**, select **Hand it over** and choose an Owner or Co-leader. Once they accept, they're the creator and you stay an Owner. See [What staff can do](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles) and [How do I transfer group leadership?](/docs/how-to/transfer-leadership).

## Troubleshooting

**I can't see Danger zone.** Only the creator sees it: the person who created or claimed the listing, or who accepted a handover of it. Co-leaders don't, and neither does anyone who was given **Owner** later.

**It says the organization is already scheduled for deletion.** Open **School settings** › **Danger zone** and look for "Scheduled for deletion on". Select **Cancel scheduled deletion** if you didn't mean to schedule it.

**My families say they were never told.** Members, families enrolled in a program and families registered for a class that will be canceled were emailed when you scheduled it, and again a week before the date. Ask them to check their spam folder. You can also message them yourself from **Messages**.

**I need the roster before it goes.** In **People** › **Parents**, select **Export**. See [Export your data (CSV)](/docs/organizations/settings/exports) for the other exports.

**I want to delete my Famlo account too.** While you own an organization that has other members, or paid bookings for activities that haven't happened yet, your account can't be deleted. For members you'll see "Transfer ownership or delete the organization first." Once your organization is scheduled for deletion, it no longer blocks your account. See [Download your data or delete your account](/docs/account/closing-your-account).

**The grace period ended and I want the organization back.** It's gone and can't be restored. Create a new listing and invite your families to join it.

**A family says they were charged after we closed.** Paid memberships and program enrollments are cancelled on the deletion date, with the unused part refunded, and registrations for classes with dates still to come are canceled and refunded. If a charge appears after that, ask the family to [open a support ticket](/docs/account/support) with the date and amount.
