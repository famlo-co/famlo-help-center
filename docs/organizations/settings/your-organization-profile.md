# Your organization profile

Your profile is what families see of your organization on Famlo: your logo, photos, name, description, where you meet and how to reach you. You edit it in **School settings** › **Identity**, and you can change your cover photo and pin things straight from your public page.

To make these changes, you need School settings set to **Edit settings**. Owners and co-leaders have this.

## Edit your profile

1. In your console, select **School settings** (1).
2. Select **Identity** (2).
3. On the row you want to change, select **Edit** (or **Add** if it's empty).
4. Make your change, then select **Save**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/your-organization-profile-identity.webp" alt="School settings with the Identity section open, showing the Logo, Photos, Name, Short link, Type, About and Location rows, with School settings and Identity highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/settings/identity" width={1280} height={900} />

Each row saves on its own, so you can change one thing without touching the others. An amber dot next to **Identity** means you haven't added a logo or a short link yet.

Under the rows, the **Your listing** card shows how complete your listing is as a percentage, and suggests the most useful thing to add next.

## What each row does

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | Your mark, shown on your page and next to your name across Famlo. Select **Upload** (or **Change**), then crop it to the rounded square. A JPG or PNG up to 10 MB works. **Remove** takes it off. Without a logo, families see your first initial. |
| **Photos** | Up to 15 photos for your page. The first one is your cover. See [Photos and your cover](#photos-and-your-cover). |
| **Name** | **Organization name** is "what families see everywhere on Famlo — in search, on your page, and on every invoice." **Tagline** is one line shown under your name in search results. |
| **Short link** | A short address like famlo.co/@sunnyside that's easier to text or print. See [Your short link](#your-short-link). |
| **Type** | What kind of organization you are, such as Co-op or Micro-school. Read [Changing your type](#changing-your-type) first. |
| **About** | **Description** is the first thing a family reads about you. **Meeting since** is optional: the year your group started meeting, not the year you joined Famlo. |
| **Location** | Your address and **Time zone**. Without a location you can't appear in searches near anywhere. A city is enough: your listing then shows an approximate area rather than a pin. The time zone is the default for activities you schedule; **Platform default** uses Eastern time. |
| **Contact** | **Contact email**, **Contact phone** and **Website**, shown on your public page for questions that come before joining. **Website** is just a link to a site you already have. |

## Photos and your cover

Your cover is simply the first of your photos. It's the picture at the top of your page and on your listing everywhere it appears.

1. In **School settings** › **Identity**, on the **Photos** row, select **Add** (or **Manage**).
2. Drag photos onto the page, or click to choose them. You can have up to 15. Photos are resized automatically.
3. To make a different photo the cover, select **Cover** on it, or move it to the front with the arrows.
4. To remove a photo, select the **×** on it (**Remove this photo**).
5. Select **Save photos**.

To write a caption for each photo, tick **Edit photo descriptions**. A caption shows under the photo and also describes it for people using screen readers.

You can also change the cover from your public page. On a computer, select **Change cover** (or **Add a cover photo**) at the top left of the cover. Choose a photo, then frame it: covers are 2:1 everywhere they show, and look best at 2400 × 1200 or larger. You'll see your page, a card and a link preview before it saves. The new photo becomes the first in your gallery. **Reframe this photo** changes the framing of the cover you already have.

## See your page as a family does

When you open your organization's page on famlo.co while signed in, a bar at the top says **This is your page.** Families don't see this bar or your editing buttons.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/your-organization-profile-owner-bar.webp" alt="An organization's public page with the bar This is your page, the View as a family button highlighted, and the Add a cover photo button highlighted on the cover" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning" width={1280} height={760} />

- **View as a family** (1) shows the page exactly as families see it. Select **Back to your view** to return.
- **Add a cover photo** or **Change cover** (2) opens the cover framer described above.
- **Edit page** opens **School settings**.

## Your short link

Your short link is famlo.co/@ followed by a name you choose. Anyone who types it, in any mix of capital and small letters, lands on your page.

The rules:

- 3 to 30 characters: lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores.
- It must start and end with a letter or a number.
- No other organization can already have it.
- Some words are reserved and can't be used, such as famlo, admin, support, help, team, staff, official, contact, billing and about.

If you type an @ at the start, it's removed for you.

## Changing your type

Your type is part of your page's web address. For example, a co-op's page is at famlo.co/co-op/your-name. When you open the **Type** row, it shows your page's address today.

:::warning
Changing your type changes your public web address. Your main page's old address redirects to the new one, so links to it that you've already shared (flyers, texts, other sites) keep working. Links to your other pages at the old address, such as your schedule, staff or photos page, open your main page instead of that page. Change your type only if your current one is wrong, and update any links to those other pages you've shared.
:::

## Pin things to the top of your page

You can pin up to 3 things to the top of your famlo.co page, right under your tabs, in a row headed **Pinned by** your organization's name.

You can pin:

- A class that anyone can already see: published, open to everyone, and still to come. Members-only, unlisted, draft and finished classes can't be pinned.
- A service that's listed on your page.
- An active item from your **Store**, shown as a resource.
- An organization-wide announcement you sent to **Both** (members and everyone registered). See [Announcements](/docs/organizations/communication/announcements).

To pin something:

1. Open your organization's page on famlo.co while signed in.
2. In the pinned row, select **Pin something**. Or, on a class, open its menu (the three dots) and select **Pin to top of page**.
3. In **Pin to the top of your page**, choose a tab, then the item.
4. Select **Pin it**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/your-organization-profile-pin-picker.webp" alt="The Pin to the top of your page dialog with Classes, Services and Resources tabs, two classes to choose from, 0 of 3 pinned, and Cancel and Pin it buttons" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning" width={632} height={432} />

A message confirms it, with **Undo** if you change your mind. To change the order, drag a pinned card by its handle. To remove one, select **Unpin** on the card, or **Unpin from top of page** in the class's menu (the three dots).

When all 3 are used, you'll see "All 3 used. Unpin one first." If a pinned item stops qualifying (a class finishes, or you make it members-only), it drops out of the row and frees its slot. Deleting the item removes its pin.

## The Verified badge

Verified organizations show **✓ Verified** next to their name on their page, and **Verified organizer** on their activity pages. It tells families your organization is real and run by who it says it is. It isn't a safety inspection or a background check on staff or volunteers.

There's nothing to apply for. Your organization is Verified once Stripe setup is finished or while a paid Famlo plan is active. The owner gets an email when the badge is added.

To finish Stripe setup, see [Getting paid](/docs/organizations/getting-paid). For plans, see [Famlo plans](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Identity in School settings.**
You need School settings set to **Edit settings**. Ask an owner of your organization to change what you can do in **People** › **Staff & teachers**.

**My short link won't save.**
Check that it's 3 to 30 characters, uses only lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores, and starts and ends with a letter or number. If it's already taken or reserved, try another.

**A photo wasn't added.**
Your organization can have 15 photos. Remove one in **Photos**, then add the new one.

**I don't see Change cover on my phone.**
The cover button on your public page shows on a computer. On a phone, go to **School settings** › **Identity** › **Photos** and move the photo you want to the front.

**I don't see Pin something.**
Pinning is for people with School settings set to **Edit settings**, and only on your famlo.co page while you're signed in.
