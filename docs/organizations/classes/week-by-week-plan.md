# Week-by-week plan

A week-by-week plan tells families what happens at each meeting of a class and what to bring. Your team sees it on the day, too. You write the words, and Famlo fills in the dates and week numbers from the class's meetings. A plan is optional: a class can be published without one.

## Open the plan

You can reach the plan from the class page or from the class editor.

1. Open **Classes**, then open the class.
2. Select the **•••** menu (1).
3. Choose **What you'll do** (2). If the class has no plan yet, it says **Add what you'll do**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/week-by-week-plan-class-page.webp" alt="A class page with the More actions menu open and What you'll do highlighted, and the plan card for the next meeting below" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/tide-pool-explorers-cape-coral-fl-89nf9umdyn" width={1280} height={1000} />

Other ways in:

- In the class editor, the **Everything else** card has a **Week-by-week plan** row that shows how many weeks are planned. Select **Open**. On a class you haven't saved yet, it says "Opens once the class is saved", because there are no meetings to plan until then.
- The class's **Details & questions** tab has a **What you'll do** card with the same choices and the first few weeks. Select **Full page** to see every week.

## Write the plan week by week

The plan page is titled **Week-by-week plan** and has one row per meeting, from your dates.

1. Under **How do you want to lay it out?**, choose **Week by week** (1): "One line per meeting, dated. Only this shape can say what happens next Thursday."
2. In each meeting's row (2), type what happens that week under **What you'll do**, and anything families should bring under **Bring**. Separate the things to bring with commas, such as "Water bottle, notebook".
3. Leave **Show the outline to families** (3) ticked if families should see the plan, or untick it to keep the plan for your team.
4. Select **Save** (4).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/week-by-week-plan-editor.webp" alt="The Week-by-week plan page with Week by week chosen, ten dated meeting rows, Show the outline to families, Copy from last term and the Save button" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/tide-pool-explorers-cape-coral-fl-89nf9umdyn/plan" width={1280} height={1496} />

You never type a date or a week number. The count above the rows, such as "7 of 10 planned", shows how far you've got. You don't have to fill every week: a blank week doesn't show to families.

If a meeting is canceled, its row stays in the plan as "No class — this week is cancelled", and the weeks are numbered around it. Families see that date marked "no class this week", so the gap reads as planned.

To check what families will see, select **Preview as a family** at the foot of the page.

## Choose a shape: week by week, stages, or no plan

| Choice | What it means | What families see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Week by week** | "One line per meeting, dated." What you'll do and what to bring, for each meeting. | Each planned week with its number, its date, what you'll do and a **Bring:** line. |
| **A few stages** | "Three to five arcs across the term. Quicker, and it gives up the dates." | Each stage with its weeks (for example "Weeks 1–3"), its title and description. No dates. |
| **Don't show a plan** | "Nothing appears on the listing." | No plan section. |

To write stages:

1. Choose **A few stages**.
2. Select **Add a stage**.
3. Pick the first and last week with the two **Weeks** menus. They only offer weeks the class actually has.
4. Type a title ("What this stretch is about") and, if you like, a sentence about it.
5. Repeat for each stage, then select **Save**.

"Three to five is usually right. A stage with no title is dropped." To remove one, select the bin icon on its row.

:::tip
Switching shapes doesn't throw away what you wrote. The other shape's words are kept, and the shape you choose decides what families see.
:::

## Show or hide the outline for families

**Show the outline to families** is ticked by default. With it ticked, the plan appears in a **What you'll do** section on the class's page on famlo.co, with each week's date and what to bring.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/week-by-week-plan-family-view.webp" alt="The What you'll do section on a class page, listing seven dated weeks, each with a title and what to bring" url="famlo.co/activities/tide-pool-explorers-cape-coral-fl-89nf9umdyn" width={848} height={716} />

Untick it to keep the plan for your team only. Your team still sees it on the plan card (below), and families don't. When the outline is hidden, the class's sign-in QR page shows the meeting's date where it would otherwise show that week's title.

In the class editor, the **Week-by-week plan** row adds "Families don't see it" when the outline is hidden.

## Copy a plan from last term

If you've run the same class before with a week-by-week plan, you don't have to type it again.

1. Open the plan page for this term's class.
2. Select **Copy from last term**. Point at it to see which class it copies from.

Famlo first saves anything you've just typed, then copies the earlier plan in meeting order: last term's week 1 goes onto this term's week 1, and so on. It only fills weeks that are still blank, so it never overwrites what you've written. You see how many weeks were copied, or "Nothing to copy — every week already has a plan."

**Copy from last term** appears only when your organization has an earlier class with the same name that has a week-by-week plan. It's on the full plan page, not on the **Details & questions** card.

You often won't need it: when you **Duplicate** a class or copy your classes to the next semester, the copy brings its plan along, so next term starts already planned.

## When dates change, the plan follows

The plan belongs to the meetings, not to fixed dates. As the page says: "Change the dates and the plan follows, week by week."

| What you change | What happens to the plan |
| --- | --- |
| Move one meeting (**•••** › **Add or move a meeting**) | That week's plan moves with the meeting. |
| Change the schedule's pattern, such as the first date, the time or how often it meets | The weeks still to come are laid onto the new meetings in order, so week 1 stays week 1. |
| Add or remove individual dates, or move the end date | Weeks whose meeting still exists stay where they are. A removed date's plan moves to a date you added. |
| Meetings that have already happened | Their plan stays as it was. |

If a planned week is left with nowhere to go, for example because you removed a date and didn't add one, you see "1 week of the plan no longer has a meeting." That week's words aren't carried over, so open the plan and add them where they belong.

For changing dates, see [Dates, repeats and moving meetings](/docs/organizations/classes/schedules-and-recurring).

## Today's plan

On the class page, under the meeting card, a plan card shows that meeting's plan:

- On the day it's titled **Today's plan**. On other days it says "Plan for" and the date, such as "Plan for Tue, Oct 13". A class with more than one meeting also shows which week it is, such as "week 1 of 10".
- **What we'll do** and **What to bring**, with each item to bring as its own chip, or "Nothing to bring."
- A line on who else sees it: "Families see the outline and what to bring on their class page." or "Only your team sees this plan. Families don't."
- Below it, the next meeting in one line, such as "Next week · Oct 20" and what's planned.

If you can edit the plan, the card has an **Edit** link, or **Add the plan** when nothing is written for that meeting yet. Teachers also see the card on their **Today** page, under the class they're teaching.

The card reads the week-by-week rows. A plan written as stages doesn't fill it.

## Troubleshooting

**The plan page says "No meetings yet."**
The class has no dates yet. "Set a date on the activity and the weeks appear here." See [Dates, repeats and moving meetings](/docs/organizations/classes/schedules-and-recurring).

**I don't see Copy from last term.**
It shows only when an earlier class of yours has the same name and a week-by-week plan. If last term's class had a different name, type the plan, or rename the class to match before you copy.

**Families can't see the plan.**
Check that **Show the outline to families** is ticked, that the shape isn't **Don't show a plan**, and that at least one week (or one stage with a title) is written. Blank weeks don't show.

**A week's plan disappeared after I changed the dates.**
If you saw "… no longer has a meeting", that week had no meeting left to sit on. Open the plan and type it into the right week.
