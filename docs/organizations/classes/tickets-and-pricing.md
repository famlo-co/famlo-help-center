# Prices, spots and ways to join

Every class has one main price and a number of spots, set in the class editor's **Price & spots** card. From there you can add other ways to join, such as single dates, a pack of visits or a payment plan, plus extras and family rates.

To take money for a class, your organization has to be able to accept payments first. You can save a priced class as a draft without that, but you can't publish it until it's done. See [Getting paid](/docs/organizations/getting-paid).

## Set the main price

1. Open **Classes**, choose the class, and open it for editing. For a new class, use **New class**.
2. Scroll to **Price & spots**.
3. Type the amount in **Price** (1). Leave it blank or enter 0 for a free class.
4. Choose what the price covers in **Price is** (2).
5. Set **Spots** (3), or leave it blank for no limit.
6. Save the class.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/tickets-and-pricing-price-card.webp" alt="The Price & spots card with the price, Price is, Spots and the open Add another way to join list highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/watercolor-studio-cape-coral-fl-cf0wuo2d4q/edit" width={648} height={1195} />

The left side of the price row says what one payment buys, read from the class's dates: **Whole term** ("All 10 meetings"), **Whole camp** for days in a row, or **The class** ("One meeting").

**Price is** offers **Per child**, **Per family**, **Per person**, **Per student**, **Per group**, **Per hour**, **Per class**, **Per session**, **Per semester** and **Something else…**. **Per family** and **Per group** charge once per order. The others charge for each child booked. **Something else…** asks for **Your own wording** and has a **Charge once per order** checkbox.

Under the price, a line shows what the price means for you. When families pay the booking fee (the default), it reads like "Families pay $108.95 with the booking fee. You receive $100.00." Families pay a booking fee on paid bookings (3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus Stripe's processing), so you receive the full price you set. You can choose to pay it yourself instead. See [The booking fee](/docs/organizations/money/booking-fee).

### The price's other settings

When a saved price has its own settings, a line under it sums them up, for example "Full term · 10 seats". Select **Change** to see and edit them:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name families see** | The price's name at checkout. |
| **Seats on this price** | Stops this price at a number, even if the class has more spots. |
| **On sale from** | Families can't buy this price before this date and time. |
| **Sales close** | This price stops selling at this date and time. |
| **Fewest per order** / **Most per order** | The fewest and most a family can book in one order. |
| **Note for families** | A short note shown with the price. |

## Spots and seats

**Spots** is the class's limit. Add a number and Famlo stops at it. Leave it blank for no limit.

Once a class has more than one way to join, every way shares the same spots. On a class with several dates the field becomes **Spots per meeting**, and whole-term children and single-date children are added together for each date.

**Seats on this price** is a second, smaller limit on one price. Extras have their own counts.

When a class is full, families see **Join the waitlist**, which saves the class for them. Nobody holds a place in line. When a spot opens, everyone who saved it hears at the same time and the first to register gets it.

## Add another way to join

Select **+ Add another way to join** (4) under Spots to open the **Add a way to join** list. Each choice adds one row under the main price, marked **NEW** until you save. Open a row to change it, or select **Remove** inside it. The list only offers what fits the class.

| Way to join | What families get | When it's offered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Single dates** | Book only the meetings they come to | Classes with more than one date |
| **Pack of visits** | Prepay a set number of visits, use them any week | Repeating classes, programs and classes split into sections |
| **Payment plan** | Pay the main price in 2 to 6 monthly payments | When the main price is one payment |
| **Monthly** | Pay each month until the class ends | Any class |
| **Members price** | A lower price only your members see | Any class |
| **Free (RSVP)** | Book a spot with no payment | Any class |

### Single dates

Single dates let a family book one meeting at a time. They're also how a family joins late and books only the dates that are left.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/tickets-and-pricing-single-dates.webp" alt="A Single dates row with a price per date, and When a date fills set to Protect the term" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/watercolor-studio-cape-coral-fl-cf0wuo2d4q/edit" width={648} height={1081} />

1. Choose **Single dates** from the list. The **Single dates** row (1) opens.
2. Set **Price per date**. It's always per child. Select **Same as the term rate · $X (no markup)** or the suggested price (**$X suggested (+25%)**, the term rate per date plus 25%, rounded up to the next $5), or type your own.
3. Leave the switch **On**.
4. Under **When a date fills** (2), choose a rule:
   - **First come**: "Drop-ins can fill a date. Once it's full, the term is offered for the dates with room."
   - **Protect the term**: **Hold** a number of spots on every date for whole-term families. "Blank holds half the class. Drop-ins can't take the last of them."
5. Save the class.

Turn the switch **Off** to pause single dates. Families then see only the whole term. Your price is kept for when you turn it back on, and families who already bought a date keep it.

Whether a family can join the whole term after the first meeting is a separate setting, **Sign-ups after the class starts**. When it's on, a late family pays for the dates that are left.

### Pack of visits

A pack is a set of visits a family pays for up front and uses on any meetings of the class.

1. Choose **Pack of visits** from the list.
2. Set the pack's **Price** and the number of **Visits**.
3. Save the class.

The row also tells you the rules, which you can't change:

- **Use by**: "End of this class". Visits end when the class does.
- "Unused visits: refunded under your refund terms".
- "One pack per family, for any of its children. A family who cancels a visit in time gets it back."

A class has one pack row.

**See who has a pack.** On the class page, the **Packs** list sits above the roster, for example "3 families with visits to use · 5 used". Select a family to open their pack. It shows the visits left, the next visit booked, and the pack visit by visit. "A visit is used when it's booked. A family who cancels before the date starts gets it back."

**Give a visit back.** In a family's pack, select **Give a visit back**. The most recent visit they used goes back into the pack. If that visit's date hasn't started yet, the family's booking for that date is canceled. No money changes hands.

### Payment plan

A payment plan lets families split the main price into equal monthly payments by card.

1. Choose **Payment plan** from the list, or select **Add a payment plan** when Famlo suggests it for a large price.
2. In **Families can pay**, choose **In full only** or **In full, or N monthly payments** (2 to 6).
3. Save the class.

The first payment is taken when the family signs up. Only plans that finish before the last class are offered. A longer one is grayed out with "the class ends before payment N". Step Up families pay the whole term. Each monthly payment carries its own booking fee unless you pay it.

### Monthly

**Monthly** bills a family each month for as long as the class runs.

1. Choose **Monthly** from the list.
2. Set **Price each month** and **Price is**.
3. Save the class.

The family is billed by card every month from the class's first day to its last meeting. A family who leaves stops paying. It's card only, so Step Up families choose the whole term. A **Monthly** price is billed over the class's own dates, so its refund terms are chosen like a term's: **Cancel any time, with notice** isn't offered for it. See [Refund terms](/docs/organizations/classes/refund-terms).

### Members price

A **Members price** is a lower price that only members of your organization can buy. Set its **Price** and **Price is**. "Families who aren't members never see it, and checkout refuses it to them."

### Free (RSVP)

**Free (RSVP)** adds a way to join with no payment. You can change **Name families see** (it starts as "Free (RSVP)"). It still counts toward spots and attendance. Use it for a free trial day, a helper parent or a sibling who comes along.

## Extras

Extras are things a family buys alongside a way to join, like lunch, materials or a T-shirt in sizes. They live in the editor's **Everything else** card.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/tickets-and-pricing-extras.webp" alt="An extra called T-shirt in two sizes, with Charged, Add a size or option, Refundable and Only offer with highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/watercolor-studio-cape-coral-fl-cf0wuo2d4q/edit" width={630} height={661} />

1. In **Everything else**, open **Extras** and select **Add an extra**.
2. Type **What it is**, then its price and how many you have. Leave the count blank for no limit.
3. Choose how it's **Charged** (1): **Once per child** or **Once per order**.
4. For sizes or other choices, select **Add a size or option** (2) and fill in one row per option, each with its own price and count. Each child picks one. An extra with options is always charged once per child.
5. Tick **Refundable** (3) if the extra follows your refund terms. When it's unticked, families are told it isn't refundable.
6. Under **Only offer with** (4), tick the prices it goes with. Leave them all unticked to offer it with every way to join.
7. Save the class.

To delete one, select **Remove this extra**.

## Family rates

Family rates are sibling discounts. They're "applied automatically when a family signs up more than one child in the same order. Families never choose it."

1. In **Everything else**, open **Family rates**.
2. Fill in a rung:
   - **What happens**: **They pay a set amount**, **Take a percentage off** or **Take an amount off**.
   - **From which child**: for example 2 for the second child onward.
   - **Amount** for a set amount or an amount off, or **Percent** for a percentage.
   - **Family max** (optional): the most a family pays for this price in one order.
3. Select **Add another rung** for a deeper step, for example $90 from the third child. Each price can have up to four rungs.
4. Save the class.

A family rate only ever lowers a price. When two set-amount rungs both reach a child, the one for the later child wins.

## Changing prices after families book

Once anyone has booked, a banner at the top of the editor reads "Live · N families booked. We check each change before it saves, and show you what families will hear." Nobody loses what they already have:

| You change | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A price's amount | "The N families who booked keep $X. A new price is only for new families." |
| **Spots** to fewer than are already coming | Everyone keeps their spot. New bookings stop until fewer than that number are booked. **Set N instead** puts it back. |
| Removing a way to join someone bought | It stops selling instead of being deleted: "… stopped selling. Families who bought it keep theirs." |
| How a bought price is charged (for example one payment to monthly) | Refused. Add another way to join to sell it differently. |

## Troubleshooting

**I can't publish a class with a price.**
The publish checklist needs "A way for families to pay". Finish [Getting paid](/docs/organizations/getting-paid), then publish again.

**Single dates or Pack of visits is missing from the list.**
Single dates need a class with more than one date. A pack needs a repeating class, a program, or a class split into sections. See [Dates, repeats and moving meetings](/docs/organizations/classes/schedules-and-recurring).

**A payment plan option is grayed out.**
That plan would still be charging after the last class. Choose fewer payments.

**Saving says a price "can't change" because families bought it.**
You can't change how families who already bought a price are charged. Add another way to join with the new terms instead.

**I don't see Price & spots on my class.**
A program uses **Days & monthly tuition** instead. See [Programs](/docs/organizations/classes/programs).
