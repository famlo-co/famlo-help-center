# Refund terms (If a family cancels)

Every paid class has refund terms: what a family gets back if they cancel. You choose them, and a paid class can't be published until it has them. Set a default once in **School settings** › **If a family cancels**, then change it on any class that needs something different.

Families read your terms as one sentence on the class page, and they agree to them when they book.

## The refund policies

There are five policies. Each one shows families a sentence written from your choice and your dates.

| In the class editor | What families read |
|---|---|
| **Full refund any time before the first class** | "Full refund any time before the first class." |
| **Full refund until a date, then half** | "Full refund until [date]. Half until the first class. None after." |
| **No refund after enrolling — commits to the whole term** | "Enrolling commits you to the full term. Tuition is not refunded." |
| **Cancel any time, with notice** | "Cancel any time. Billing stops [N] days after you tell us." |
| **No refunds for this class** | "No refunds if you cancel." |

**Full refund until a date, then half** asks for a **Full refund until** date. **Cancel any time, with notice** asks for **Notice (days)**, and 30 days is used if you leave it blank.

The class editor lists only the policies that fit how each price is charged:

| How the price is charged | Policies offered |
|---|---|
| Paid once, in full. This is usually a class's main price, even when it covers a whole term, and also when you add a **Payment plan** to it. | Full refund any time before the first class, No refund after enrolling, No refunds for this class |
| **Pack of visits** | The same three |
| **Single dates** | The same three, plus Full refund until a date, then half. Each is counted to that one date, so families read, for example, "Full refund any time before the date." |
| **Monthly** (billed each month until the class ends), or a term paid in full or in installments | The same three, plus Full refund until a date, then half |
| Billed monthly with no end date (**Monthly, ongoing**) | Cancel any time, with notice, or No refunds for this class |

A single date that has no terms of its own follows the class's term price, applied to that date. A pack's unused visits are refunded under the class's terms.

A **Program** works differently. Its families leave by notice, set on the program's own card, so the **If a family cancels** row shows that notice rule instead. See [Programs](/docs/organizations/classes/programs).

## Set your organization's default

Your default is what every new paid class starts with. To change it, you need Money set to **Take payments & refund**. By default that's the **Owner** and the **Bookkeeper**.

1. Open **School settings**, then **If a family cancels** (1).
2. Under **Your standard terms** (2), choose one:
   - **Full refund any time before it starts**: "The simplest promise to keep, and the one most families expect."
   - **Enrolling commits them to the full term**: "For terms where you commit the cost — a room, a kiln, a teacher — before anyone turns up."
   - **Ask me each time**: "No default. Every paid activity picks its own before it can go live."
3. Select **Save** (3).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/refund-terms-settings.webp" alt="School settings with If a family cancels selected, the three standard terms and the Save button highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/settings/enrollment/enrollment_terms/edit" width={1280} height={900} />

Only those two policies can be a default. A policy that needs a date or a notice period is set on the class itself.

Until you choose, the row reads "Not set — every paid activity is asked before it can go live".

## Change the terms for one class

Anyone who can edit a class can set its terms.

1. Open **Classes**, choose the class, and open it for editing.
2. In **Everything else**, open **If a family cancels** (1).
3. Pick a policy (2). The first choice keeps what the class already has:
   - **Use your default** shows your organization's sentence.
   - **Keep this class's terms** appears when the class already has its own.
   - **Not set yet** means there's no default and nothing chosen: "A paid class needs refund terms before it can publish."
4. If you chose a date or a notice period, fill it in.
5. Save the class.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/refund-terms-editor.webp" alt="The class editor's If a family cancels row, open, with its policy choices highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/watercolor-studio-cape-coral-fl-cf0wuo2d4q/edit" width={670} height={472} />

When a class has its own terms, the row shows **Changed for this class**. If the class has more than one price, you'll see a set of choices for each price, under the price's name, and each can have different terms.

Extras have their own **Refundable** tick box. When it's unticked, families are told the extra isn't refundable. See [Prices, spots and ways to join](/docs/organizations/tickets-and-pricing).

## Refund terms are needed to publish

A paid class shows **Refund terms** in its checklist of things to finish before families can book. If you select **Publish** without terms, the editor tells you: "Choose the refund policy families agree to when they enrol. Famlo will not publish terms on your behalf."

If you picked **Full refund until a date, then half** but left the date empty, you'll see: "Choose the date the full-refund window closes, or pick a different refund policy."

Free classes don't need refund terms.

## Each booking keeps its terms

A family's booking keeps the terms they agreed to when they booked. If you change a class's terms or your default later, families who already booked aren't affected. Only new bookings get the new terms.

If you want a class to keep particular terms whatever your default says, choose them on the class itself instead of using your default.

## What refund terms cover

Refund terms decide what comes back when a family cancels a booking themselves before it starts. They apply to the class price. At checkout, the booking fee line tells families it's "Not refundable unless the organizer cancels."

After a booking has started, families can use **Request a refund** to ask you, and you decide. When you cancel a booking or a whole class yourself, or send a refund, that's covered in [Refunds and cancellations](/docs/organizations/money/refunds).

## Older classes that say No refunds

Some classes made before refund terms existed carry an older No refunds setting. In the editor, those classes show **No refunds for this class** already chosen, with this note under the prices: "This class says No refunds. A price you add follows your standard refund terms, and the prices already here keep No refunds."

To change it, open **If a family cancels** on the class and pick a different policy.

## Troubleshooting

**The policy I want isn't in the list.**
The list depends on how the price is charged. For example, **Full refund until a date, then half** isn't offered for a price paid once, in full, and **Cancel any time, with notice** is only for a price billed monthly with no end date. Check how the price is charged in **Price & spots**, or add another way to join, such as **Single dates**, that allows the policy you want.

**I can't see If a family cancels in School settings.**
Your default terms need Money set to **Take payments & refund**. Ask your organization's owner. You can still set terms on any class you can edit.

**My class won't publish.**
Look for **Refund terms** in the checklist. Open **If a family cancels** on the class and choose a policy, or set a default in **School settings**.
