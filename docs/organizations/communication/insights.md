# Local insights

**Local insights** shows what families near you look for and can't find, so you can decide what to run next. It also shows how your own listings are doing and who else runs activities nearby. Owners, co-leaders and class organizers can open it on any Famlo plan. It needs your organization's address.

## Open Local insights

1. Select **Local insights** (1) at the foot of the console rail. On a phone, it's under **More**.
2. Read the line above the title, for example "Within 35 miles of Cape Coral, FL · last 30 days". Every number on the page uses that area and window.
3. To use a different address, select **Change area** (2). It opens your address in **School settings** › **Identity**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/communication-insights-where.webp" alt="The Local insights page with Local insights in the console rail and the Change area button highlighted, and The opportunity card below the title" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/insights" width={1280} height={800} />

"Near you" means within 35 miles of your organization's address. The window is the last 30 days and moves forward every day. It doesn't follow calendar months.

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owners and co-leaders | Open the page and use **Change area** |
| Class organizers, and anyone with **Classes** set to **All classes** | Open the page, but not **Change area** |
| Teachers, Front desk, Bookkeepers | Can't open it |

See [What staff can do: roles and access](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

## What the page shows before there's data

The page has three states.

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We need your address first" | Your organization has no address yet, so there's no "near you" to report. **Your listings** still shows. | Select **Add your location** to add your address in **School settings**. See [Your organization profile](/docs/organizations/settings/your-organization-profile). |
| "We're listening for demand near [your town]." | Not enough families have searched near you or set alerts yet. "Nothing to report yet." | Select **Publish an activity**. If your organization is listed, **Your listings** also offers your listing link with **Copy**, to share where your families already talk. |
| The full report | Families near you have searched or set alerts. | Read **The opportunity** first, then the evidence under it. |

In the full report, demand is on the left: **The opportunity**, **What families searched for**, **What families are watching for** and **When they look**. On the right are **Your listings** and **Who else runs things near you**. On a phone they stack in that order.

## The opportunity

When families keep looking for something they can't find nearby, the page opens with one sentence about it and a button to act on it.

- From searches: "N searches for [category] near you in the last 30 days, and M found nothing." It ends with "Nobody within 35 miles offers it." or "N nearby activities aren't meeting it."
- From alerts: "N families near you are waiting for [category]. Nobody nearby offers it." Under it: "They have each set an alert and nothing has matched yet", sometimes with an age range such as "Mostly ages 5–11".

If both kinds exist, the search gap wins. A search only becomes the opportunity when it has been made often enough and at least half of those searches found nothing.

To act on it:

1. Select **Add a [category] class** (1), for example **Add a Drama & Theater class**.
2. The new class form opens. When the opportunity comes from alerts, the category is already filled in.
3. Finish and publish the class as usual. Nothing is saved until you save the class. See [Create and publish a class](/docs/organizations/activities).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/communication-insights-demand.webp" alt="The opportunity card saying 4 families near you are waiting for Drama & Theater, with the Add a Drama & Theater class button highlighted, above What families searched for and What families are watching for" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/insights" width={624} height={822} />

If every category families are watching already has someone offering it, the page says so instead: "Every category families are watching near you already has someone offering it." That means the area is well served. It doesn't mean data is missing.

:::note Your own classes aren't counted as "nearby"
"Nobody nearby offers it" and "N nearby activities" count only other organizations' published, upcoming activities. Your own classes are left out, so the page can suggest a category you already run.
:::

## What families searched for

This section shows searches for activities on famlo.co made within 35 miles of you.

- The header gives the totals: "N searches in 30 days · M came back with nothing".
- Each row is a category, with its number of searches. Then either "M found nothing" (in amber) or "N nearby activities". Categories are ranked by how often their searches found nothing, not by how many there were.
- **Add one** appears on a row when nobody nearby offers that category. It opens a blank new class form.
- **What they typed** lists up to 8 common search phrases with counts.
- **Ages searched for:** lists the ages families searched for, with counts.

These figures count searches, not families. One family searching three times counts as three searches.

Until enough separate households have searched near you, this section says "We have started counting searches near you." It fills in once there's enough to report safely.

## What families are watching for

This section shows families who set a [location alert](/docs/families/discover/location-alerts) for activities in your area. Each one asked to be told when something new matches.

- The header says "N families watching this area · M have never had a match".
- Each row shows a category, an age range ("Mostly ages 5–11", or "All ages"), and "N families watching". Then it shows either "nobody nearby offers this" with **Add one**, or "N nearby activities". **Add one** opens the new class form with the category filled in.
- Up to four categories show, with the ones nobody nearby offers listed first.

If no single category has enough families yet, you see "Too few families in any one category to report safely." If nobody has set an alert for your area, you see "No families are watching this area yet."

## When they look

Once the search section has filled in, this section shows a bar for each day of the week, starting on Monday. A sentence underneath names the busiest day and time of day, for example: "Searches near you peak on Saturdays, and the busiest time of day is the evening."

Days and times are the searching family's own local time. Use the busiest day and time to send an [announcement](/docs/organizations/communication/announcements) or post your class link.

## Your listings

This section shows how your own published listings are doing. It works from your first day, before anyone has searched.

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **published** | Your published listings |
| **RSVPs, all time** | Confirmed bookings on your published listings |
| **nobody opened in 30 days** | Published listings that nobody has opened in the last 30 days (shown in amber when it's more than zero) |

- **See the N listings nobody opened** names up to five of them. Each name opens that class. If there are more, a link to **Classes** follows.
- **Most opened in the last 30 days** lists up to five of your listings with "N viewers".

A listing nobody opens has a different problem from one people open but don't book. Try a clearer title, a photo or a better category, then share it. See [Share a class and add a Reserve on Famlo button](/docs/organizations/classes/share-a-class).

## Who else runs things near you

This section lists other organizations within 35 miles that have published, upcoming activities, busiest first. The heading shows their total, for example "Who else runs things near you · 130 activities".

- Each row shows the organization's name and how many activities it has. Select a row to open its public Famlo page.
- The first eight show, then "and N more organizations".
- Only listed organizations appear. Hidden ones never show.

If nobody else nearby has anything coming up, it says "No other organization within 35 miles has anything published and upcoming."

## How families' privacy is protected

Local insights never names a family, a child or a person who searched. Small numbers are held back so a row can't point to one household:

- **What families searched for** shows nothing until at least five separate households have searched near you. A category needs at least five searches before it gets its own row.
- **What families are watching for** shows a category only once three or more families are watching it ("A category appears once 3 or more families are watching it.").
- **What they typed** shows only phrases that many separate households in several areas have typed and that a person at Famlo has approved. Everything else is counted in the totals but never shown.
- Ages are shown as broad ranges such as "Mostly ages 5–11", never as a child's actual age.

**Your listings** and **Who else runs things near you** have no minimum. One is your own data, and the other is public information that anyone can see on Famlo.

## What Local insights doesn't do

- There are no charts to filter, no date range to choose, and no comparison with past months. The area and the 30-day window are fixed.
- Nothing is emailed or scheduled. Open the page when you want to look.
- Local insights can't be exported. For spreadsheets of families, money, registrations, rosters and attendance, see [Export your data (CSV)](/docs/organizations/settings/exports).

## Troubleshooting

**I can't see Local insights in the rail.**
It's for owners, co-leaders and class organizers, and anyone whose **Classes** access is **All classes**. Ask an owner to change your access. See [What staff can do: roles and access](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

**There's no Change area button.**
Only people who can edit **School settings** (owners and co-leaders) can change the address. It also doesn't appear until your organization has an address.

**It still says "We're listening for demand".**
Not enough families have searched near you or set alerts yet. Publish your classes and share your listing link. The page fills in by itself as families search.

**It says nobody nearby offers something I already run.**
The "nearby" counts leave out your own classes. They only count other organizations' published, upcoming activities.

**I expected more categories or search terms.**
Rows below the privacy minimums are held back on purpose, and only approved search phrases are shown. See [How families' privacy is protected](#how-families-privacy-is-protected).

**The area is the wrong town.**
The area is your organization's address. Select **Change area**, or open **School settings** › **Identity**, and correct the location.
