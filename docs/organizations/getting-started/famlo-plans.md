# Famlo plans: Free, Scholarship and Website

Every organization on Famlo starts on **Free**, and many stay there. Two paid plans add more: **Scholarship** for approved Step Up providers, and **Website** for your own website on your own web address. You buy, switch and cancel a plan in **School settings** › **Plan & billing**.

## The three plans

| Plan | Price | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 / month, with no time limit | Everything you need to list, sign up and run your group (see below). |
| **Scholarship** | $49 / month (monthly only) | Everything in Free, plus Step Up and ESA payments: families pay with their Step Up scholarship instead of a card, and Famlo writes the invoice and tracks it until you're paid. |
| **Website** | $99 / month or $990 a year | Everything in Scholarship, plus your own website, built and edited in the console, on a web address you already own. |

**Free** is not a trial. Nothing switches off after 30 days. It includes:

- a listing on the map families already search;
- unlimited events, registrations and rosters;
- card checkout straight into your own Stripe account;
- allergies, needs and pickup details on rosters;
- attendance, with QR self check-in;
- announcements and messages;
- staff roles you can adjust area by area;
- a ledger export (CSV);
- Services, Store, Testimonials and Integrations.

**Scholarship** adds:

- running without connecting Stripe at all;
- invoices in the child's name, period by period, with a status on every invoice;
- what you're floating this month;
- **Money** › **Scholarships & ESA** and **School settings** › **Scholarship billing**, including your own scholarship funds and awards.

[Accept Step Up and ESA payments](/docs/organizations/money/step-up-and-esa) walks through it.

**Website** adds:

- the **Website** place in your console, where you build your site;
- your own web address. Bring a domain you already own; Famlo doesn't sell or register one;
- booking emails to families in your site's look, under your name;
- recording cash, check and transfer payments you receive outside Famlo, such as membership dues.

[Your Famlo website: what you get](/docs/organizations/website/overview) covers the site itself.

### The booking fee is the same on every plan

Families pay a booking fee on paid bookings (3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus Stripe's processing), so you receive the full price you set. You can choose to pay it yourself instead. No plan waives or lowers it, and there's no booking fee on scholarship money or on money you collect outside Famlo. [The booking fee, and paying it yourself](/docs/organizations/money/booking-fee) has the details.

## Who can buy or change a plan

Anyone whose **Money** access is set to **Take payments & refund** can open **Plan & billing**. With the standard roles, that's the **Owner** and the **Bookkeeper**. Co-leaders don't see it. See [What staff can do](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles) to change someone's access.

Each organization has its own plan. If you run more than one, each is billed separately: one can stay on Free while another adds Scholarship.

## Buy a paid plan

1. Open **School settings** (1), then **Plan & billing** (2).
2. On the **Scholarship** or **Website** card, choose how to pay: **Switch to Scholarship**, or for Website **Pay monthly, $99** or **Pay yearly, $990**.
3. The first time you buy, tick the box to agree to Famlo's Terms of Service and Refund Policy. After that, the page reminds you that buying or changing a plan agrees to them.
4. Enter your card on the secure Stripe checkout page and pay.

When you come back, the page says "Your plan is active. Thanks for backing Famlo." If it says Famlo is still confirming the payment with Stripe, wait a moment and the page catches up.

Paid plans have no free trial, no setup fee and no cancellation fee. A yearly plan is paid for the year in advance and starts the day you pay for it.

### Coming from the pricing page

The buttons on famlo.co/pricing (**Add Scholarship** and **Get Website**) take you to **Plan & billing** with that plan highlighted as "The one you picked". If you handle money for more than one organization, Famlo first asks **Which organization is this for?** Pick one; the others stay exactly as they are. If you don't run an organization yet, you start by creating a listing. **Start your community** is the Free plan's button: it starts a new listing, or opens your organizations if you already run one.

## Switch plans or billing

Once you pay for a plan, the other card's buttons change it in place, for example **Switch to Scholarship** or **Pay monthly, $99**. Each button also shows the amount you'll pay today. Changes are prorated both ways:

- Moving up from Scholarship to Website, billed monthly, charges the difference for the rest of your current period now.
- Moving down from Website to Scholarship, billed monthly, charges nothing now. What's left of your current period is credited against your next invoice.
- Changing how often you're billed (monthly to yearly, or yearly to monthly) starts a new period today, and what's left of the current one is credited against it. On your current Website card, the button reads **Switch to yearly billing** or **Switch to monthly billing**, followed by the amount due today.

:::warning
If your website is live on your own web address, switching from Website to Scholarship takes it offline as soon as you switch. The Scholarship card says so before you choose.
:::

## Cancel a plan

1. Open **School settings**, then **Plan & billing**.
2. On your current plan's card, select **Cancel this plan**, then confirm.

The plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and that period isn't refunded. Until then nothing changes, and **Plan & billing** shows the date it ends. Changed your mind? Select **Keep it running** before that date and the plan keeps renewing. After it ends, your organization carries on with everything in Free. Nothing you've created is deleted.

If you're on Website, your website goes offline on the day the plan ends. It shows a "taking a short break" page, nothing on it is deleted, and buying the plan again brings it back. [Your Famlo website: what you get](/docs/organizations/website/overview) explains what visitors see.

## Invoices, your card and tax

Once you've paid for a plan, **Plan & billing** has an **Invoices & receipts** card. Select **View billing history** to see every Famlo invoice for your organization with PDFs, and to change the card Famlo bills.

Plan prices don't include tax. Where tax applies, it's worked out from your organization's billing address and shown separately on the invoice. If your organization is exempt, for example a recognized nonprofit, you need to give Famlo a valid exemption certificate for your state. Tax stops from then on, but tax already paid isn't refunded.

## Renewal reminders and failed payments

Paid plans renew automatically. Famlo emails the person who created or claimed the listing before each renewal: 3 to 7 days ahead for a monthly plan, and 30 to 60 days ahead for a yearly one.

If a payment doesn't go through, the same person gets an email, and the plan shows as past due on **Plan & billing**. While it's unpaid:

- the features your plan adds stop working, but nothing you've created is deleted;
- on Website, your website goes offline until the plan is paid;
- Stripe keeps retrying the card for a few days.

To settle it straight away, open **Plan & billing**, select **View billing history** and update your card.

## If your plan shows On us

If your plan shows **On us**, there's nothing to pay and no card on file. You have everything the plan includes.

## The Verified badge

Your organization is Verified once Stripe setup is finished or while a paid Famlo plan is active.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't find Plan & billing.**
Your **Money** access needs to be **Take payments & refund**. Ask your organization's Owner to change it in **People** › **Staff & teachers**.

**I bought the plan for the wrong organization.**
Plans belong to one organization. Cancel it in that organization's **Plan & billing**, then buy it in the right one. If you need help with a charge, [open a support ticket](/docs/account/support).

**The page says "Please agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy to change your plan."**
Tick the agreement box above the plan cards, then select the button again.
