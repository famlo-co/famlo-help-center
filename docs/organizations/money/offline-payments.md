# Record cash, check and transfer payments

When a family pays their membership dues to you directly, record it in Famlo so their membership counts as paid and Famlo stops asking them to pay. You need the **Website** plan and your **Money** access set to **Take payments & refund**. Recording a payment doesn't move any money. It notes a payment that already happened.

## Record a dues payment

You can do this only if your organization charges membership dues. Start from **Money** › **Dues**, or from **People** if you can open it.

**From Money › Dues:**

1. Open **Money**, then **Dues**.
2. Under **Paid outside Famlo**, choose the family, then select **Record a payment**. Every member is in the list, including a family paying you for the first time. A member who already appears on the Dues list also has a **Record a payment** link on their row.
3. Fill in the panel as in steps 4 to 9 below.

**From People:**

1. Open **People** (1). It opens on the **Parents** tab.
2. On the family's row, select the **•••** menu (2).
3. Choose **Dues & payments** (3). The family's dues panel opens on the right.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/offline-payments-row-menu.webp" alt="The People list on the Parents tab, with People in the rail, a family's ••• menu and Dues & payments highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/people" width={1280} height={800} />

4. In **Amount paid** (1), type what the family gave you, for example 45.00.
5. Under **How they paid** (2), pick **Cash**, **Check**, **Bank transfer**, **Venmo**, **Cash App**, **Zelle** or **Other**.
6. Check **Date received**. It starts on today's date, so change it if the money came in earlier.
7. In **Membership paid up until** (3), set the last day this payment covers. If the family should never be asked to pay, leave it blank. The next section explains how this works.
8. If you like, add a **Note (optional)**, such as a check number. It's for your own records, and the family never sees it.
9. Select **Record payment** (4).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/offline-payments-record-form.webp" alt="A member's dues panel with Amount paid, How they paid, Membership paid up until and Record payment highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/people" width={1280} height={800} />

You'll see "Recorded $45.00 from" and the member's name. You can open the same panel from the member's own panel too. In the **•••** menu, choose **Open** followed by their first name, then select **Record a payment** under **Dues**.

## What "Membership paid up until" does

The date decides how long the family counts as a paid-up member.

| What you enter | What happens | What the member's panel shows |
|---|---|---|
| A date | Until that date, the family can book members-only activities without paying through Famlo. The product says: "A week before it, they're reminded; after it, Famlo asks them to pay." | "Comped through" and the date |
| Nothing | The family is never asked to pay. This suits a founding family, a volunteer or a scholarship place. | "Comped indefinitely" |

In **Money** › **Dues**, these families show "(comped)" after their name. When a date passes and the family hasn't paid, their panel shows "Complimentary period ended — not paid".

**Amount paid** has to be more than zero. To give a family a free membership when no money changed hands, comp them when you add them instead.

:::tip Comp a family when you add them
When you add a family with **People** › **Add a parent**, you can choose **No charge — comp them.** That works on every Famlo plan and doesn't need a recorded payment.
:::

## Families who pay by card

You can't record a cash payment for a member who is already paying their dues by card through Famlo. If you try, Famlo stops you with this message: "[Name] is paying by card through Famlo. Cancel that subscription first, otherwise recording this would leave them charged twice."

This protects the family from paying twice. Their card membership has to end before you can record payments for them. [Membership dues](/docs/organizations/membership-dues) explains how a membership ends.

## No fee on money you record

Famlo takes no fee on money you record. It never passed through Famlo, so there's no booking fee and nothing comes out of your payouts. Dues that families pay by card carry the booking fee as usual. See [The booking fee](/docs/organizations/money/booking-fee).

## Your past records

Under **Recorded off-platform**, the family's dues panel lists the most recent payments you've recorded. Each one shows the amount, how they paid, the date, who recorded it and any note.

## Without the Website plan

On the **Free** or **Scholarship** plan, the dues panel shows "Recording cash, checks and transfers is part of the Website plan." in place of the form, with a **See plans** link for the Owner and Co-leaders. Anyone else sees "Ask the owner or a co-leader to add it." Anything you recorded earlier stays listed under **Recorded off-platform**. To compare plans, see [Famlo plans](/docs/organizations/getting-started/famlo-plans).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Dues & payments in the family's menu, or Record a payment on Dues.**
Both need your organization to charge dues and your **Money** access set to **Take payments & refund**. **Record a payment** on **Dues** also needs the **Website** plan. If you can't open **People**, use **Money** › **Dues** instead. To set up dues, see [Membership dues](/docs/organizations/membership-dues).

**"Enter the amount that was paid, for example 25.00."**
**Amount paid** is empty, zero or not a number. Type the amount with no currency sign.

**"Choose how the payment was made."**
Pick an option under **How they paid**, then select **Record payment** again.

**The family keeps getting asked to pay.**
Check the date in **Membership paid up until**. After that date, Famlo asks them to pay again. Record their next payment with a later date, or leave the date blank if they should never be asked.
