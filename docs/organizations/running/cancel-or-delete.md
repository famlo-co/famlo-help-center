# Cancel, unpublish or delete a class

There are three ways to stop a class: **Cancel activity**, **Unpublish** and **Delete**. The right one depends on whether families have booked it. To cancel or delete a class, your **Classes** access must be set to **All classes**, or you need **School settings** access. Owners, co-leaders and class organizers have this.

## Which one to use

| You want to | Use | What happens to families who booked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call off a class families have booked | **Cancel activity** | Their bookings are canceled. Card payments are refunded and every family is told. |
| Take a class off Famlo for now, and maybe bring it back | **Unpublish** | Nothing. They keep their places and aren't refunded. |
| Get rid of a class nobody has booked, such as a test or a duplicate | **Delete** | Not allowed: Delete is refused once families have booked. |
| Give back part of a payment and keep the class running | **Send refund** | The booking stays. See [Refund part of a payment instead](#refund-part-of-a-payment-instead). |

## Cancel a class families have booked

**Cancel activity** cancels every booking on the class, refunds card payments and tells each family. It only shows in the menu while families hold bookings on the class.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/cancel-or-delete-menu.webp" alt="The ••• menu on a class page with Cancel activity highlighted, above the Can't delete row" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/great-books-club-fl1a46fybk" width={344} height={559} />

1. Open **Classes**, then select the class.
2. Select **•••** (**More actions**) at the top of the class page. On a phone it's in the bar at the bottom.
3. Select **Cancel activity** (1).
4. Confirm. The message reads "Cancel this activity? Everyone registered will be refunded and notified."

The page then says "Activity cancelled." and how many registrations were notified and refunded. The class is unpublished, its status changes to **Cancelled**, and it moves to **Finished** on your Classes list.

There's no undo. To run the class again later, select **Duplicate** from the **•••** menu to start a new draft from it.

:::note
Card refunds come out of your organization's Stripe balance.
:::

## What families get when you cancel

| What they paid with | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A card (or Apple Pay, Google Pay and other card methods) | Refunded to the card they used. It usually shows up in 5–10 business days. |
| Famlo account credit | Given back as credit. |
| Your organization's own scholarship award | Goes back on the award it came from. |
| A pack of visits | Every visit they haven't used yet is refunded. |
| Step Up or an ESA | If the program hasn't paid you for the seat yet, it comes off the invoice. If the program has already paid you, Famlo can't return that money. Settle it with the program yourself. |
| Nothing (a free class or RSVP) | The booking is canceled. |

Families get one email and a notification for each order. They don't get one for each child.

**For a class that meets more than once,** canceling also cancels and refunds any single dates families booked on meetings that haven't happened yet. Meetings that already took place keep their bookings.

**For a class split into sections,** canceling the class cancels every section and refunds every section's families.

## Cancel one section or one date

You don't have to cancel the whole class.

- **One section:** open the class editor, find the section under **Sections**, and select **Remove this section…**. If families booked it, the panel offers **Cancel [section] and refund**. That cancels the section and refunds and tells its families. The class's other sections stay as they are. See [Split a class into sections](/docs/organizations/classes/sections).
- **One date of a weekly class:** cancel or move that meeting from the class's dates. Families then choose between a credit and a refund. See [Dates, repeats and moving meetings](/docs/organizations/classes/schedules-and-recurring).

## Unpublish a class

Unpublish takes a class off Famlo without touching anyone's booking. Use it when you want to pause a class, fix something, or bring it back later.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/cancel-or-delete-unpublish.webp" alt="The class editor's buttons with Unpublish highlighted below Save" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/activities/great-books-club-fl1a46fybk/edit" width={342} height={168} />

1. Open **Classes**, then select the class.
2. Select **•••**, then **Edit details**.
3. Select **Unpublish** (1), under the save button.
4. Confirm. The message reads "Unpublish [class name]? The class page stops showing to families, including anyone with the link. Your other edits on this page are saved too."

What happens:

- The class page stops showing to families, both in search and from a shared link.
- Families who already booked keep their places. Nothing is canceled or refunded. If they need to know, send them a message with **Message families** on the class page.
- The class becomes a draft again. To bring it back, open it and select **Publish**.

:::tip
If you only want the class out of search, select **Switch to link only** from the **•••** menu instead. The class stays bookable for anyone you send the link to.
:::

## Delete a class

Delete removes a class for good. It's meant for a class nobody has booked, such as a test or a duplicate.

1. Open **Classes**, then select the class.
2. Select **•••**, then **Delete**.
3. Confirm "Delete this activity?".

You're taken back to your Classes list, and it says "Activity deleted."

### When Delete is refused

When a class can't be deleted, the **•••** menu shows **Can't delete** in place of **Delete**, with a short reason underneath. The full reason tells you what to do instead.

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Families have booked it. This counts even if they were refunded later ("Kept as the families' record"). | If they still hold places, use **Cancel activity**. If it's still published and nobody holds a place, use **Unpublish**. |
| Families have enrolled in it (a program). | Cancel any enrollments that are still running, which stops their payments. The enrollments stay as the record of what families paid, so unpublish the program instead of deleting it. See [Programs](/docs/organizations/classes/programs). |
| You sent a payment request through it. | Unpublish it instead. |
| You've taken attendance for it. | Unpublish it instead. |
| You canceled it less than an hour ago ("You can delete this in an hour"). | Wait an hour. Families are still being told about the cancellation. |

Bookings, payments and attendance are your records and the families' records of what they paid and when their children attended, so Famlo keeps them. A canceled or unpublished class is already hidden from families, so there's nothing more to do.

## Refund part of a payment instead

If a class is going ahead but you owe a family something back, for example for a missed week, send a refund without canceling. Select **Send refund** on the family's booking in **Money** › **Class payments**, or from the family in **People**. You choose the amount, and the booking stays as it is.

Sending refunds needs **Money** set to **Take payments & refund** (by default, the Owner and the Bookkeeper). To cancel one family's booking rather than the whole class, see [Refunds: sending one, answering a request, cancellations](/docs/organizations/money/refunds).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Cancel activity in the menu.**
It only shows while families hold bookings on the class. If nobody has booked it, use **Unpublish** or **Delete**. A class that's already canceled doesn't show it either.

**Cancel activity or Delete doesn't work for me.**
Canceling and deleting need **Classes** set to **All classes** (or **School settings** access). Teachers can't cancel or delete classes. Ask an owner to check your access in **People** › **Staff & teachers**.

**I cancelled, but a family says they weren't refunded.**
Card refunds usually take 5–10 business days to show up. If the family paid with Step Up or an ESA and the program had already paid you, Famlo doesn't return that money. Settle it with the program.

**I cancelled a free class and still can't delete it.**
Wait an hour after canceling. If the menu still says **Can't delete**, the class has records, such as attendance, that Famlo keeps. It's already hidden from families.
