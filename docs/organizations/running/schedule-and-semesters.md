# Schedule, semesters and breaks

**Schedule** in your console shows everything your organization runs, on the semester it belongs to. Use it to see who is teaching what, fill gaps, name your terms and breaks, and copy one semester's classes into the next.

Owners, co-leaders and class organizers can open **Schedule**. Only owners and co-leaders can give a class a teacher or change semesters and breaks. Class organizers see the same information without those buttons. Teachers don't see **Schedule**. They reach their classes from **Today** and their **Teaching calendar**.

## Find your way around Schedule

Open **Schedule** in the left rail. On a phone, it's in the bottom tab bar. The page opens on today's date in the **Day** view.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/schedule-and-semesters-day.webp" alt="The Schedule page in the Day view, with the Day, Week, Month and List switch, the Group by choices and a class with no teacher highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/school-calendar" width={1280} height={660} />

- **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **List** (1) switch the view. Use the arrows to move back or forward, and **Today** to come back.
- **Group by** (2) sorts the day's classes into rows: **Teacher**, **Location** or **Age group**. Each row lays its classes out across the hours of the day.
- A class with a dashed red outline (3) has no teacher yet. When you group by **Teacher**, these sit together at the top in the **No teacher yet** row.

Next to the date you'll see how many classes are on that day, and in red, how many need a teacher and how many clash. A clash means one teacher is down for two classes at the same time. Those classes get a solid red outline, and the class says which one it clashes with.

The other views:

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Week** | A week's classes on a time grid, with the week of the semester above it. |
| **Month** | One row per week, each labeled with its semester and week number, such as "Fall semester · Week 5". Break weeks are shaded and named, and your co-op day is marked **Co-op** on every week. |
| **List** | Classes as a list, in date order. |

Once you've set up a semester, the page heading and the organization switcher at the top of the rail also show the current week, such as "Fall semester · Week 7".

The semester panel (its **Semester**, **Co-op day**, **Breaks** and **Next semester** sections, and your feed link) sits beside or below the calendar, depending on the width of your screen.

## Give a class a teacher

You can pick a teacher for any class right from the **Day** view. The **Needs you** list on **Today** brings you here when some of today's classes have no teacher.

1. Open **Schedule**, then choose **Day**.
2. Select the class. A panel opens with its time, place, ages and how many have signed up.
3. Under **Teacher**, select a person from your staff list (1).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/schedule-and-semesters-teacher.webp" alt="The class panel for Rocket Science Lab, saying nobody is teaching it yet, with the staff list highlighted and two people marked busy" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/school-calendar" width={1280} height={800} />

The person you pick replaces any teacher the class had, and the class moves into their row. The list marks the current teacher **Teaching**, and flags anyone already teaching at that time with "Busy:" and the other class's name.

For a class that meets every week, picking someone makes them the teacher of every meeting of that class, not just this one.

An assigned teacher can take the class's register, see its children and answer its families, without seeing the rest of your organization. See [What staff can do: roles and access](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

The panel also has **Take attendance**, which opens that meeting's register, and **Open class**, which opens the class page.

## Set up a semester

A semester names a stretch of your school year, such as "Fall 2026". Once you have one, every week on the calendar and in **Classes** says which week of the term it is. You need to be an owner or co-leader.

1. Open **Schedule**. If you have no semester yet, the panel says **No semester yet**.
2. Select the **Set up** button in the panel. It reads **Set up** and the season Famlo suggests, for example "Set up Fall".
3. Check the **Name** (whatever your families call it), **First day** and **Last day**. Famlo fills in a suggestion. You can change all of it.
4. Choose a **Co-op day** if you have one, or leave it as **No regular day**.
5. Select **Set up semester**.

You can also get here from **School settings**: **Semesters & co-op day** opens **Schedule**.

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. A semester that starts midweek has a short first week.

After your first semester, you add the next one when you copy classes forward: the copy creates the new semester for you if it doesn't exist yet. See [Copy a semester's classes forward](#copy-a-semesters-classes-forward).

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/organizations/schedule-and-semesters-panel.webp" alt="The semester panel for Fall 2026, showing its dates and progress, the co-op day, a Thanksgiving break, the next semester and the feed link, with Edit, Add a break, Copy Fall to Spring and Copy feed link highlighted" url="famlo.co/co-op/sunnyside-learning/school-calendar" width={1000} height={708} />

To change a semester's name or dates, select **Edit** (1) in the panel, then **Save**.

To delete a semester, select **Edit**, then **Delete** and the semester's name at the bottom. Deleting a semester removes it and its breaks. Its classes stay exactly as they are.

## Choose your co-op day

Your co-op day is the day your group usually meets. It's marked on every week of the semester in the **Month** view. It doesn't change any class.

If you haven't chosen one, the panel suggests the day most of your weekly classes meet on.

1. In the panel, under **Co-op day**, pick a day.
2. Select **Set your co-op day**. Once one is set, the button reads **Save**.

To remove it, choose **No regular day** and save.

## Add a break

A break, like Thanksgiving week or spring break, labels the weeks it covers on the calendar and in **Classes**.

:::warning
A break doesn't cancel anything. Meetings already scheduled in those weeks stay on, and families who booked them stay booked. If a class isn't meeting during the break, change its dates on the class. See [Dates, repeats and moving meetings](/docs/organizations/classes/schedules-and-recurring).
:::

1. In the panel, under **Breaks**, select **Add a break** (2).
2. Enter a **Name**, such as "Thanksgiving".
3. Choose the **First day off** and **Last day off**. Both must fall inside the semester.
4. Select **Add break**.

To remove a break, select the **×** beside it and confirm. Its weeks stop being labeled as a break.

## Which classes belong to a semester

A class belongs to the semester its first meeting falls in. You don't file classes into a semester yourself. If you set up your semesters after you've been running for a while, your existing classes are already sorted into them.

A weekly class that keeps going past a break, or past the end of the term, still belongs to the semester it started in. Canceled classes aren't counted. If you change a class's dates, it moves to whichever semester its new first meeting falls in.

## Copy a semester's classes forward

When a semester is done, you can copy its classes into the next one instead of setting each one up again. You need to be an owner or co-leader.

1. In the panel, under **Next semester**, select the copy button (3). It names the two seasons, such as **Copy Fall to Spring**. On **Classes**, the same thing is **Copy to next semester**.
2. If the next semester already exists, confirm the copy.
3. If it doesn't exist yet, a panel opens with a suggested name and dates. Check them, then select **Create and copy classes**.

What happens:

- Every class is copied as a **draft**, on the same weekday and time, moved forward by whole weeks. Nothing is published, and no family sees anything, until you publish each class.
- Prices come with the class, and the dates on them move forward too.
- Teachers and bookings are not copied. Choose next term's teachers from the **Day** view or the class.
- Classes that would start after the new semester ends are not copied. The message after the copy says how many.
- Copying again skips classes that are already there, so a second click doesn't make duplicates.

The message after the copy reads "Check the dates and teachers, then publish." Open each draft from **Classes** to review and publish it. See [The Classes list and Registrations](/docs/organizations/running/classes-list).

## Check these prices

Some prices are paid in installments or monthly. If the new semester is shorter, those prices are changed so they don't bill after the class ends. That means fewer payments, and a different total.

When that happens, the copy takes you to **Check these prices**: "These copied prices now bill for fewer months, to fit their new dates." Each row names a class and explains what changed.

1. Select a class to open it in the editor.
2. Check the price, and change it if you need to. See [Prices, spots and ways to join](/docs/organizations/tickets-and-pricing).
3. When you're finished, select **Done**.

## Your Teaching calendar feed

You can add your teaching schedule to Google, Apple or Outlook. Your **Teaching calendar** covers every class you run or teach, in every organization you lead, not just this one. You'll also find it in the account menu (your avatar), under **Teaching calendar**.

- In the panel, under **In your own calendar**, select **Copy feed link** (4), then add it to your calendar app as a subscription.
- If you see **Turn on the feed** instead, select it, then **Enable calendar feed**.

Your calendar app refreshes on its own schedule, so changes can take a while to show.

:::caution
Your Teaching calendar feed and your family calendar feed share one private link. If you regenerate the link or turn the feed off, you change both. Manage the link from your own **Calendar**, under **Subscribe**. Anyone with the link can see your calendar, so don't share it.
:::

To give families a calendar of your classes, use the feed link on your website instead. See [Analytics, the cookie banner and your calendar feed](/docs/organizations/website/connections). For step-by-step app instructions, see [How do I export my calendar to Google or Apple?](/docs/how-to/export-calendar).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Edit, Add a break or the copy button.**
Only owners and co-leaders can change semesters and breaks or copy a semester forward. Class organizers see the semester without those buttons. See [What staff can do: roles and access](/docs/organizations/members-and-roles).

**Setting up a semester says another semester "already covers some of these dates".**
Two semesters can't overlap. Change the dates, or edit the other semester first.

**I added a break, but classes still show that week.**
That's expected. A break only labels the weeks. Change the class's dates if it isn't meeting. Families with bookings hear about the change.

**A class shows up in the wrong semester.**
A class belongs to the semester its first meeting falls in. Check the class's first date, or the semester's dates.

**The person I want isn't in the teacher list.**
The list shows your staff. Add them first under **People** › **Staff & teachers**. See [Add staff, connect their accounts, and your Staff page](/docs/organizations/team/add-staff). If a class says "Written in as" a name "who isn't on your staff list", that name was typed in on the class. Pick a staff member to replace it.

**The copy button says the semester "has no classes to copy yet".**
No classes start within that semester's dates. Check the semester's dates under **Edit**.
