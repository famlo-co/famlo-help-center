# Welcome to Famlo

Famlo connects families with the co-ops, classes, field trips and meetups near
them, and gives the people who run those a place to organize them.

Which half of the help center you want depends on which side you are on. Most
people only ever need one.

## If you are a family

A Famlo account is free, and free events are free. Paid bookings include a
booking fee of 3.7% of the price plus $1.79 a ticket, plus the cost of
processing your payment. It's already in the price you see, and some
organizations pay it for you.

- **Find activities near you.** Search by what your children are into, their
  ages, whether it meets in person or online, and whether it takes Step Up or
  an ESA.
- **Register in one place.** One family profile covers every booking, so the
  allergies, pickup people and emergency contact you add once reach the
  organizers you book with.
- **Keep track of it all.** Your bookings appear on one calendar, which you can
  subscribe to from the calendar app you already use.
- **Talk to organizers.** Every conversation is in **Messages**, and a
  conversation about an activity can show its details beside it.
- **Keep your children's school records.** Every child gets a free school
  record. Classes you book on Famlo fill it in on their own, you add home
  learning, and it builds transcripts, diplomas and student IDs.
- **Use organizations' own websites.** Some organizations have a website of
  their own. You sign in there with your Famlo account and find your bookings in
  its **Parent portal**.

Once you're signed in, the bar across the top of famlo.co (1) takes you to
**Dashboard**, **Calendar**, **Family** and **Discover**. On a phone the same
places sit in a bar at the bottom: **Home**, **Discover**, **Calendar**,
**Messages** and **Family**.

## If you run an organization

A co-op, a class, a tutoring practice, a library program or a microschool all
work the same way. Listing your organization is free, and the **Free** plan has
no time limit. Families pay a booking fee on paid bookings (3.7% of the price
plus $1.79 a ticket, plus Stripe's processing), so you receive the full price
you set. You can choose to pay it yourself instead.

Two paid Famlo plans add more: **Scholarship** adds Step Up and ESA invoicing,
and **Website** adds your own website as well.

- **Get found.** Your listing appears on the map to families searching nearby.
- **Schedule classes.** One-off or repeating, with prices, spots, and a choice
  of who can find them.
- **Run the day.** Rosters with each child's allergies and pickup details,
  attendance with QR self check-in, and drop-off and pick-up times.
- **Bring in your team.** Give each person the access they need, area by area.
- **Handle the money.** Card payments go into your own Stripe account, and
  membership dues are charged on a schedule. Step Up and ESA seats carry no
  booking fee.
- **Have your own website.** On the **Website** plan, build a site in your
  console and put it on a web address you already own.

You run your organization from the console. Open it from your avatar menu ›
**Manage organizations**. The rail on the left (1) holds its places, from
**Today** down to **School settings**. You see only the places your role lets
you open, and **Website** appears once your organization has the Website plan
or a site.

## If something here is wrong

Every page has a **Was this page helpful?** control at the bottom. Answering
**No** opens a box where you can say what was missing or wrong, and that helps
decide which pages get fixed first.

For anything about your own account, [open a support ticket](/docs/account/support)
instead. Famlo Support reads every ticket and usually replies within one
business day.
